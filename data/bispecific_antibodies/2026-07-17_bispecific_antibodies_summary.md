1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies in 2026 is characterized by significant venture capital investment and advancement into clinical trials across oncology, ophthalmology, autoimmune diseases, and neurology. Key developments include the progression of VEGF/Ang2 bispecifics in ophthalmology and T-cell engagers in solid tumors and autoimmune conditions. Funding activity remains robust, with multiple biotechs securing Series A through D rounds to support IND-enabling studies and Phase 1/2 trials. Regulatory milestones include European Commission approval for Genmab’s epcoritamab and positive CHMP opinions for Johnson & Johnson’s teclistamab combination. The pipeline demonstrates a diversification of targets beyond traditional oncology, extending into metabolic and inflammatory indications. Competition between antibody-drug conjugates and bispecific antibodies remains a focal point, particularly in lung cancer and liver disease. 4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• OLN324 — Mechanism: VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody. Trial phase: Phase 3. Patient population: Diabetic Macular Edema and Wet Age-Related Macular Degeneration. Comparator: Vabysmo. Outcome: Designed to usurp Vabysmo’s market position following Phase 1b success. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet)
• BYN-003 — Mechanism: TL1AxIL-23p19 bispecific antibody with half-life extension (HLE) technology. Trial phase: Phase 1. Patient population: Healthy volunteers. Comparator: Not specified. Outcome: Study began in April 2026. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets)
• EBD Bispecific Antibodies — Mechanism: Bind Aβ Oligomers and Transferrin Receptors. Trial phase: Preclinical. Patient population: Cynomolgus Monkeys. Comparator: Native anti-AβO antibodies. Outcome: Showed improved brain penetration compared to native antibodies. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/15/3327603/0/en/acumen-pharmaceuticals-highlights-enhanced-brain-delivery-ebd-program-data-and-early-alzheimer-s-disease-insights-at-the-alzheimer-s-association-international-conference-aaic-2026.html)
• TEPKINLY (epcoritamab) — Mechanism: IgG1-bispecific antibody binding CD3 on T cells and CD20 on B cells. Trial phase: Approved (based on Phase 3). Patient population: Relapsed or Refractory Follicular Lymphoma. Comparator: Lenalidomide and Rituximab (R2). Outcome: Reduced risk of disease progression or death by 79% (HR 0.21) and achieved 96% Overall Response Rate. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/15/3327556/0/en/genmab-announces-net-sales-of-darzalex-daratumumab-for-second-quarter-of-2026.html)
• Teclistamab + Daratumumab — Mechanism: Off-the-shelf subcutaneous bispecific antibody that redirects T cells through BCMA and CD3 targets. Trial phase: Phase 3 (MajesTEC-3). Patient population: Relapsed/Refractory Multiple Myeloma. Comparator: Not specified in text. Outcome: 83.4% reduction in risk of disease progression or death. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma)
• TRIV-573 — Mechanism: Bispecific inhibiting KLK5/7 and Th2 cytokine IL-13. Trial phase: First-in-human clinical studies initiated. Patient population: Atopic dermatitis. Comparator: Not specified. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• ARC101 — Mechanism: Bispecific T cell engager. Trial phase: Phase 1. Patient population: Solid tumors. Comparator: Not specified. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• Lead Bispecific ADC (Sidewinder) — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody-drug conjugate. Trial phase: Planning clinical entry next year. Patient population: Lung cancer, head and neck cancers, gastrointestinal cancers, colorectal cancer. Comparator: Not specified. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• EPI-326 — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody (EGFR degrader). Trial phase: Clinical trial initiation early this year. Patient population: Non-small cell lung cancer and head and neck squamous cell carcinoma. Comparator: Not specified. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• PRO-203 — Mechanism: CD20xCD3 T-cell engager. Trial phase: Clinical study. Patient population: Systemic sclerosis. Comparator: Not specified. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• VIB305 — Mechanism: Masked T-cell engager prodrug. Trial phase: Phase 1. Patient population: EGFR-expressing solid tumors. Comparator: Not specified. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• CBX-250 — Mechanism: T-cell engager targeting peptide human leukocyte antigen (pHLA). Trial phase: Phase 1. Patient population: Acute myeloid leukemia, chronic myeloid leukemia, myelodysplastic syndromes, chronic myelomonocytic leukemia. Comparator: Not specified. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• Mosunetuzumab — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody. Trial phase: Phase 3 (SWOG S2308). Patient population: Low tumor burden follicular lymphoma. Comparator: Rituximab. Outcome: Evaluating monotherapy to limit toxicities. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-)
• Surovatamig — Mechanism: Novel CD19-directed bispecific antibody. Trial phase: Phase 3 (SOUNDTRACK-F1). Patient population: Previously untreated follicular lymphoma. Comparator: Chemotherapy. Outcome: Assessing combination with rituximab. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-)
• PF’4404 — Mechanism: Novel bispecific antibody designed to target VEGF and PD-L1. Trial phase: Phase 3 (SigVie-002 combo). Patient population: Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer. Comparator: Not specified. Outcome: Evaluating combination with sigvotatug vedotin. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/sigvotatug-vedotin-does-not-significantly-improve-survival-in-nsclc-trial)
