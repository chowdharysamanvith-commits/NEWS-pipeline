1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies demonstrates active development across oncology, ophthalmology, and autoimmune indications during the reporting period, though reporting on ASCO 2026 abstracts varies regarding specific modality classification (https://biopharmadive.com/news/asco-2026-abstracts-merck-biontech-lilly-moderna/820898/). While some ASCO 2026 coverage highlights antibody-drug conjugates and monoclonal combinations rather than bispecifics, other industry reports confirm significant advancements in bispecific and trispecific formats (https://biospectrumasia.com/opinion/40/27567/ai-tools-life-sciences-companies-are-using.html). Regeneron Pharmaceuticals highlights its VelociSuite technology platform as a producer of new classes of bispecific antibodies alongside its gene therapy and infectious disease assets (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/28/3303234/0/en/regeneron-s-ebola-antibody-recommended-by-world-health-organization-for-investigational-use-in-response-to-current-bundibugyo-ebolavirus-outbreak.html). NovaBridge Biosciences advances its pipeline with givastomig, a Claudin 18.2 X 4-1BB bispecific antibody, following positive Phase 1b results in gastric cancer (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/28/3302657/0/en/novabridge-to-present-at-the-2026-jefferies-global-healthcare-conference.html). Therini Bio initiates clinical evaluation for THN391 while advancing THN622, a fibrin/VEGF bispecific antibody, for retinal neurodegeneration (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/27/3302124/0/en/therini-bio-doses-first-patients-in-phase-1b-trial-of-novel-anti-inflammatory-therapy-thn391-for-diabetic-macular-edema.html). The industry faces ongoing manufacturing challenges for these complex protein formats, prompting a re-evaluation of production models to expand access and improve patient experience (https://lifescienceleader.com/doc/in-vivo-manufacturing-when-the-body-becomes-the-bioreactor-0001). First approvals for trispecific antibodies are projected by 2028, signaling a maturation of the multispecific therapeutic class (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/). Recent industry coverage highlights the operational integration of bispecific antibodies into community oncology settings alongside advancements in targeted therapy for non-small cell lung cancer (https://cancernetwork.com/view/asco-2026-the-presentations-that-may-shape-the-future-of-multiple-myeloma).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Givastomig — Mechanism: Claudin 18.2 X 4-1BB bispecific antibody that conditionally activates T cells via the 4-1BB signaling pathway in the tumor microenvironment — Phase: Phase 2 (global, randomized) — Patient Population: Claudin 18.2-positive gastric cancer and other gastrointestinal malignancies — Comparator: Standard-of-care therapy (add-on) (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/28/3302657/0/en/novabridge-to-present-at-the-2026-jefferies-global-healthcare-conference.html).
• THN622 — Mechanism: Bispecific anti-fibrin inflammatory epitope and anti-VEGF blocking antibody — Phase: Preclinical/Lead Candidate Selection — Patient Population: Retinal conditions characterized by vascular dysfunction including DME — Outcome: Dual mechanism intended to increase treatment efficacy, durability, and prevent fibrosis (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/27/3302124/0/en/therini-bio-doses-first-patients-in-phase-1b-trial-of-novel-anti-inflammatory-therapy-thn391-for-diabetic-macular-edema.html).
• Sutacimig — Mechanism of Action: Bispecific antibody — Trial Phase: Phase 1/2 (Part B complete) — Patient Population: Glanzmann thrombasthenia and Factor VII Deficiency — Clinical Endpoints: Safety, tolerability, and clinical efficacy over three months at multiple dose levels (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/27/3301881/0/en/hemab-therapeutics-to-participate-in-the-2026-jefferies-global-healthcare-conference.html).
• CT-202 — Mechanism of Action: Nectin-4 x CD3 TCE bispecific antibody, pH-dependent TCE designed to be preferentially active within the tumor microenvironment — Trial Phase: Phase 1 — Patient Population: Solid tumors (bladder, colorectal, lung, breast) — Clinical Endpoints: Phase 1 initiation expected (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/27/3301818/0/en/context-therapeutics-to-present-at-the-jefferies-global-healthcare-conference.html).
• Mechanism of Action: Bispecific and trispecific antibodies function by simultaneously binding to tumor markers (e.g., BCMA, GPRC5D, CD19) and T cell proteins (CD3) to activate immune responses (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/).
• Clinical Endpoints: JNJ-79635322 (trispecific) showed an overall response rate of 86.1% in relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma, compared to the established bispecific landscape (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/).
• Toxicity profile: Cytokine release syndrome (CRS) and immune effector cell-associated neurotoxicity syndrome (ICANS) are primary toxicities (https://cancernetwork.com/view/moving-bispecific-antibodies-into-community-practice-operational-strategies-and-expert-consensus).
• Monitoring technology: Wearable biometric technology (e.g., TempTraq) detects fever 6 hours earlier than traditional methods (https://cancernetwork.com/view/moving-bispecific-antibodies-into-community-practice-operational-strategies-and-expert-consensus).
• Clinical Endpoints or results: Nearly 80% of patients on bispecific therapies develop subsequent infections, prompting universal infection prophylaxis recommendations (https://cancernetwork.com/view/moving-bispecific-antibodies-into-community-practice-operational-strategies-and-expert-consensus).
