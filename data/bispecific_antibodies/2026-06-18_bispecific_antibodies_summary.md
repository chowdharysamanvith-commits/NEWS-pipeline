1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies in June 2026 demonstrates significant capital inflow, clinical progression, and manufacturing advancements across multiple modalities. Regulatory activity includes FDA Fast Track designation for NovaBridge Biosciences’ givastomig in HER2-negative gastric cancer and acceptance of supplemental Biologics License Applications for Roche’s combination therapies. Clinical development is active for assets such as Context Therapeutics’ CTIM-76 in ovarian cancer and Epcoritamab in large B-cell lymphoma, with Phase 3 trials evaluating frontline settings. Biotechnology firms secured substantial venture funding to advance bispecific programs targeting oncology and autoimmune indications, with Sidewinder Therapeutics raising $137 million and EpiBiologics securing $107 million. Manufacturing support technologies from Lonza and preclinical tooling from Bio X Cell are evolving to address the complexity of multispecific formats. Industry analysis indicates that AI-driven bispecific antibody development has attracted over $1 billion in pharma investments, signaling a shift toward strategic necessity for clinical risk mitigation. Strategic licensing agreements include AbbVie securing rights to RemeGen’s RC148 and Astellas partnering with Vir Biotechnology on a masked PSMA/CD3 engager. These developments indicate a robust pipeline expansion for T-cell engagers and multi-specific antibodies across solid tumors and hematologic malignancies. 4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Givastomig — Mechanism: Claudin 18.2 X 4-1BB bispecific antibody that conditionally activates T cells via the 4-1BB signaling pathway — Phase: Phase 1b — Population: First-line HER2-negative, CLDN18.2-positive metastatic gastric cancer — Result: Median PFS 16.9 months at 8 mg/kg dose (Source: https://cancernetwork.com/view/givastomig-earns-fda-fast-track-designation-in-her2-negative-gastric-cancer)
• CTIM-76 — Mechanism: Claudin 6 x CD3 bispecific antibody (T cell engaging) — Phase: Phase 1 — Population: Patients with platinum-resistant ovarian cancer who progressed on a median of 7 prior lines of therapy — Endpoint: 29% confirmed overall response rate per RECIST v1.1 (Source: https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/15/3311621/0/en/context-therapeutics-announces-positive-interim-efficacy-and-safety-results-from-ongoing-phase-1-clinical-trial-for-ctim-76.html)
• Epcoritamab — Mechanism: CD3×CD20 bispecific antibody — Phase: Phase 3 (EPCORE DLBCL-1) — Population: Relapsed/refractory large B-cell lymphoma ineligible for or relapsed after HDT-ASCT — Endpoint: Median PFS 3.5 months vs 3.0 months (HR 0.74, P=.0059); Complete Response (CR) rate 38% vs 26% (Source: https://cancernetwork.com/view/epcoritamab-yields-superior-pfs-vs-chemoimmunotherapy-in-pretreated-lbcl)
• Glofitamab/Epcoritamab — Mechanism: CD20×CD3 bispecific antibodies — Phase: Retrospective Analysis — Population: Large B-cell lymphoma awaiting CAR T-cell therapy — Result: 69% ORR during bridging vs 61% in conventional arm (Source: https://cancernetwork.com/view/cd20-cd3-bispecific-antibodies-may-be-feasible-bridging-strategy-in-lbcl)
• Mosunetuzumab — Mechanism: CD20×CD3 bispecific antibody (Subcutaneous) — Phase: Phase 1b/2 — Population: First-line follicular lymphoma with high tumor burden — Result: 90% ORR and 88% CR rate (Source: https://cancernetwork.com/view/elucidating-first-line-sc-mosunetuzumab-benefit-in-follicular-lymphoma)
• HER2 BATs — Mechanism: Arming activated T-cells with anti-CD3 x anti-HER2 bispecific antibodies — Phase: Phase 1/2 — Population: Metastatic breast cancer (≥2 prior lines) — Result: Median overall survival 21.4 months (Source: https://cancernetwork.com/view/64-a-phase-i-ii-study-of-pembrolizumab-and-anti-cd3-x-anti-her2-armed-activated-t-cells-in-metastatic-breast-cancer)
• VIR-5500 — Mechanism: PRO-XTEN dual-masked CD3 T-cell engager targeting PSMA — Phase: Phase 1 — Population: Advanced, metastatic prostate cancer — Endpoint: Masking technology designed to keep T-cell engagers inactive until reaching tumor microenvironment (Source: https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2026/)
• GS Ori-Go Vector Technology — Mechanism: Vector technology supporting lead-clone titers — Phase: Preclinical/Manufacturing — Result: Titers higher than 11 g/L for bispecifics (Source: https://drug-dev.com/lonza-advances-integrated-dna-to-ind-programs-through-acceleration-de-risking-and-innovation/)
