1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies in April 2026 highlights significant capital deployment and clinical progression among emerging biotechs alongside established industry players. Regeneron Pharmaceuticals continues to leverage its VelociSuite technology to produce new classes of bispecific antibodies alongside its established monoclonal antibody portfolio (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/20/3277447/0/en/regeneron-announces-investor-conference-presentations.html). Merck presented phase 1/2 data for its bispecific antibody MK-2010 in non-small cell lung cancer at AACR 2026 (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/). Promatix Biosciences highlighted next-generation bispecific ADCs designed to improve tumor selectivity (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/). Earendil Labs and Sanofi announced a major collaboration valued at approximately $2.56 billion to advance bispecific antibodies for autoimmune diseases (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/). Industry analysis indicates that while bispecific antibodies offer transformative potential through dual-target engagement, their complex structure presents significant manufacturing challenges (https://lifescienceleader.com/doc/radiotherapeutics-for-neuroendocrine-tumors-with-perspective-therapeutics-thijs-spoor-0002).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Merck MK-2010 — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody targeting PD-L1 and VEGF — Trial Phase: Phase 1/2 — Patient Population: PD-L1-positive non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) — Clinical Endpoints: 55% overall response rate (ORR) in previously untreated patients (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/)
• Promatix PBS293 — Mechanism: Bispecific ADC directed to EGFR and EphA2 conjugated to a cytotoxic agent — Trial Phase: Preclinical — Patient Population: Tumor cells expressing receptor pairs (KRAS model) — Clinical Endpoints: Internalized in tumor cells more efficiently than parental antibodies (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/)
• iOmx IOMX‑0675 — Mechanism: Simultaneously blocks immunosuppressive receptors LILRB1 and LILRB2 while sparing activating LILR family members — Trial Phase: Early Clinical — Patient Population: Solid tumors — Clinical Endpoints: Restores cytotoxic T-cell and NK-cell function (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/)
• Earendil HXN-1001 — Mechanism: Half-life extended anti-TL1A antibody (Company also advancing bispecifics via Sanofi deal) — Trial Phase: Phase IIa — Patient Population: Ulcerative colitis patients — Comparator: Placebo (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/)
• BCMA-directed bispecific antibodies — Mechanism: Promote T-cell redirection and activation against myeloma cells — Trial Phase: MajesTEC-3 (Phase 3 implied) — Patient Population: Early relapse multiple myeloma patients — Clinical Endpoints: Progression-free survival and depth of response reported as improved (https://cancernetwork.com/view/mechanistic-rationale-and-combination-strategies-in-multiple-myeloma)
• Talquetamab-tgvs — Mechanism: Utilized as bridging therapy with 3 ramp-up doses to stabilize disease — Patient Population: Relapsed/Refractory Multiple Myeloma (https://cancernetwork.com/view/analyzing-the-role-of-celmods-in-the-relapsed-refractory-myeloma-space)
• Elranatamab-bcmm — Mechanism: Potential for combination with iberdomide to shorten duration of therapy based on sustained MRD negativity — Patient Population: Relapsed/Refractory Multiple Myeloma (https://cancernetwork.com/view/analyzing-the-role-of-celmods-in-the-relapsed-refractory-myeloma-space)
