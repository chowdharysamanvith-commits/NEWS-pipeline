1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for antibody therapeutics demonstrates robust development activity across oncology and autoimmune indications during the reporting period. Biogen has secured full global rights to the CD38-targeting monoclonal antibody felzartamab, advancing its development across multiple immune diseases (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/biogen-expands-control-of-cd38-antibody-felzartamab-across-global-markets-17136). Sanofi’s R&D portfolio faces scrutiny following setbacks with antibody candidates amlitelimab and itepekimab, despite the commercial success of its antibody drug Dupixent (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/sanofi-interim-ceo-defends-hudsons-dupixent-legacy-accepts-number-setbacks-rd). Pfizer terminated a Phase 1 study for an immunostimulatory drug conjugate (ISAC) targeting PD-L1, citing strategic business reasons (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/sanofi-interim-ceo-defends-hudsons-dupixent-legacy-accepts-number-setbacks-rd). Compass Therapeutics reported topline secondary endpoint data for its lead DLL4 x VEGF-A bispecific antibody in biliary tract cancer (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/24/3281064/0/en/compass-therapeutics-to-announce-topline-secondary-endpoints-from-the-phase-2-3-companion-002-study-of-tovecimig-in-patients-with-biliary-tract-cancer.html). Regeneron Pharmaceuticals highlighted its proprietary VelociSuite technology's capability to generate new classes of bispecific antibodies alongside recent gene therapy approvals (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/23/3280340/0/en/regeneron-announces-agreement-with-u-s-government-to-help-lower-drug-costs-for-american-patients-and-will-provide-innovative-new-gene-therapy-for-free-in-the-u-s.html). Zanidatamab demonstrated improved progression-free and overall survival in the HERIZON-GEA-01 phase 3 trial for HER2-positive gastroesophageal adenocarcinoma (https://cancernetwork.com/view/3-things-you-should-know-about-the-evolving-therapeutic-landscape-of-gea). Supply chain disruptions in the Middle East are testing the resilience of global pharmaceutical logistics, particularly for temperature-sensitive biologics and monoclonal antibodies (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/how-the-iran-war-is-quietly-testing-global-drug-supply-chains-17137). Multiple biotechnology firms secured significant venture capital to advance bispecific T-cell engagers and antibody-drug conjugates into clinical trials (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26). These developments underscore the competitive landscape of antibody-driven therapies and the operational risks inherent in their distribution (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/how-the-iran-war-is-quietly-testing-global-drug-supply-chains-17137).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Felzartamab is a monoclonal antibody designed to target CD38, a surface protein expressed on plasma cells, enabling direct depletion of antibody-producing cells (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/biogen-expands-control-of-cd38-antibody-felzartamab-across-global-markets-17136)
• In a Phase 2 randomized study for late antibody-mediated rejection (AMR), approximately 82 percent of patients treated with felzartamab showed resolution of histologic rejection compared with around 20 percent in the placebo group (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/biogen-expands-control-of-cd38-antibody-felzartamab-across-global-markets-17136)
• Tovecimig is a DLL4 x VEGF-A bispecific antibody in Phase 2/3 for patients with biliary tract cancer where the primary endpoint of overall response rate was met (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/24/3281064/0/en/compass-therapeutics-to-announce-topline-secondary-endpoints-from-the-phase-2-3-companion-002-study-of-tovecimig-in-patients-with-biliary-tract-cancer.html)
• CTX-8371 is a PD-1 x PD-L1 bispecific antibody in Phase 1 for patients with TNBC, NSCLC, and Hodgkin lymphoma with expansion cohorts open for enrollment in post-checkpoint inhibitor setting (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/24/3281064/0/en/compass-therapeutics-to-announce-topline-secondary-endpoints-from-the-phase-2-3-companion-002-study-of-tovecimig-in-patients-with-biliary-tract-cancer.html)
• CTX-10726 is a PD-1 x VEGF-A bispecific antibody in Phase 1 for patients with RCC, gastroesophageal cancer, HCC, and endometrial cancer with IND cleared early 2026 (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/24/3281064/0/en/compass-therapeutics-to-announce-topline-secondary-endpoints-from-the-phase-2-3-companion-002-study-of-tovecimig-in-patients-with-biliary-tract-cancer.html)
• Zanidatamab is a HER2-targeting bispecific monoclonal antibody in Phase 3 HERIZON-GEA-01 for HER2-positive GEA with Median PFS 12.4 months vs 8.1 months (Arm B/C vs A) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/3-things-you-should-know-about-the-evolving-therapeutic-landscape-of-gea)
• Teclistamab is a BCMA-directed bispecific antibody in Phase 3 MajesTEC-3 for early relapse Multiple Myeloma where combination with daratumumab showed rapid and deep response (https://cancernetwork.com/view/discussing-proper-utilization-of-bcma-bispecifics-in-early-relapse-multiple-myeloma)
• Teclistamab promotes T-cell redirection and activation against myeloma cells (https://cancernetwork.com/view/mechanistic-rationale-and-combination-strategies-in-multiple-myeloma)
• Teclistamab signature risks include cytokine release syndrome (CRS) and immune effector cell-associated neurotoxicity syndrome (ICANS) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/discussing-proper-utilization-of-bcma-bispecifics-in-early-relapse-multiple-myeloma)
• Sanofi’s anti-OX40L-ligand antibody amlitelimab flunked a Phase 2 asthma trial (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/sanofi-interim-ceo-defends-hudsons-dupixent-legacy-accepts-number-setbacks-rd)
• Sanofi’s TSLP and IL-13 inhibitor lunsekimig achieved success in a pair of Phase 2 respiratory studies but included a Phase 2 failure for eczema (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/sanofi-interim-ceo-defends-hudsons-dupixent-legacy-accepts-number-setbacks-rd)
• Pfizer’s PF‑08046037 is an immunostimulatory drug conjugate (ISAC) with a TLR7 agonist payload targeting PD-L1 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/sanofi-interim-ceo-defends-hudsons-dupixent-legacy-accepts-number-setbacks-rd)
• Disruptions to Middle East logistics corridors are raising costs and testing resilience for monoclonal antibodies and immunotherapies requiring tightly controlled temperatures (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/how-the-iran-war-is-quietly-testing-global-drug-supply-chains-17137)
• PRO-203 is a CD20xCD3 T-cell engager in Phase 1/2 for patients with severe autoimmune disease (systemic sclerosis) (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• CBX-250 is a T-cell engager (pHLA) in Phase 1 for patients with acute myeloid leukemia, chronic myeloid leukemia, myelodysplastic syndromes, and chronic myelomonocytic leukemia with readout due at the end of 2026 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
