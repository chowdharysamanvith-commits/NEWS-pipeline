MONTHLY PHARMA INTELLIGENCE BRIEF

1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies is defined by significant venture capital investment and diverse clinical applications spanning oncology, autoimmune diseases, and ophthalmology. Multiple biotech firms have secured substantial funding to advance bispecific T-cell engagers and dual-targeting antibodies into clinical trials, including Ollin Biosciences raising $330 million and Bionyra Pharma raising $165 million. Analytical advancements in mass spectrometry are concurrently enhancing the characterization of these complex biotherapeutics to support development. The pipeline includes candidates targeting EGFR, VEGF, and immune checkpoints across various therapeutic areas such as non-small cell lung cancer and multiple myeloma. Regulatory and manufacturing scalability remains a key focus alongside efficacy, evidenced by WuXi Biologics securing South Korean GMP certification. Clinical data is increasingly validating survival benefits, such as Akeso and Summit Therapeutics reporting a 34% reduction in risk of death with ivonescimab. Strategic alliances are expanding, highlighted by a $2.56 billion deal between Earendil Labs and Sanofi. These developments collectively indicate a shift toward integrating bispecific modalities across oncology and autoimmune indications. Manufacturing infrastructure is evolving to support the specific demands of these complex molecules through digital transformation and expanded capacity.

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• OLN324 is a VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody targeting dry age-related macular degeneration. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• MMT-205 activates Tie2 and inhibits VEGF as a potential best-in-class bispecific antibody. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• TRIV-573 inhibits KLK5/7 and Th2 cytokine IL-13 for atopic dermatitis. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• ARC101 is a bispecific T cell engager for solid tumors. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• EPI-326 is an EGFR degrader bispecific antibody. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• LBL-051 is a trispecific T-cell engager targeting BCMA, CD19 and CD3. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• PRO-203 is a CD20xCD3 T-cell engager designed to treat severe autoimmune disease. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• OLN324 is a VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody that topped Vabysmo in an earlier Phase 1b trial for diabetic macular edema. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet)
• BYN-003 is a TL1AxIL-23p19 bispecific antibody with half-life extension technology that began a Phase 1 study in healthy volunteers in April. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets)
• VERAXA’s BiTAC strategy utilizes two complementary precursors to enable tumor-restricted activation of the therapeutic effect. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/02/3321216/0/en/veraxa-biotech-initiates-cell-line-development-with-atum-to-advance-its-lead-bitac-tce-program.html)
• Givastomig is a CLDN18.2 x 4-1BB bispecific antibody targeting Claudin 18.2-positive tumor cells and conditionally activating T cells through the 4-1BB signaling pathway. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/29/3318837/0/en/novabridge-biosciences-appoints-dr-srishti-gupta-as-chief-executive-officer.html)
• Epcoritamab is a bispecific antibody (DuoBody technology) showing statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvement in progression-free survival when combined with lenalidomide. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/29/3319290/0/en/genmab-announces-positive-phase-3-results-for-epcoritamab-plus-lenalidomide-in-patients-with-relapsed-refractory-diffuse-large-b-cell-lymphoma-demonstrating-statistically-significa.html)
• Ziihera (Zanidatamab) is a HER2-targeted bispecific antibody engineered using Zymeworks’ proprietary Azymetric technology. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/29/3318760/0/en/zymeworks-to-acquire-theravance-biopharma-inc.html)
• Teclistamab mechanism involves redirecting T cells through BCMA and CD3 targets to activate the immune system against myeloma cells. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma)
• MajesTEC-3 trial demonstrated an 83.4% reduction in the risk of disease progression or death with teclistamab plus daratumumab compared with standard of care (HR, 0.17). (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma)
• PF'4404 is a novel bispecific antibody designed to target VEGF and PD-L1. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/sigvotatug-vedotin-does-not-significantly-improve-survival-in-nsclc-trial)
• Surovatamig is a novel CD19-directed bispecific antibody being evaluated in the SOUNDTRACK-F1 trial. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/how-are-key-lymphoma-data-from-asco-and-eha-2026-shaping-treatment)
• Ivonescimab combines the activity of two of the best-selling cancer medicines (bispecific format) and reduced the risk of death by 34% compared to standard treatment. (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/)
• Mass spectrometry platforms are advancing biotherapeutic characterization, including intact and top-down workflows for antibodies. (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/mass-spectrometry-platforms-push-deeper-into-proteomics-and-biopharma-at-asms-2026-17277)
• Charge detection mass spectrometry is used for high-mass analyte characterization relevant to complex biotherapeutics. (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/at-asms-2026-mass-spectrometry-stakes-its-claim-17280)
• AI-driven proteomics and informatics are integrated into analytical workflows for biotherapeutics. (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/at-asms-2026-mass-spectrometry-stakes-its-claim-17280)
• Clinical trials in follicular lymphoma are prioritizing chemotherapy-free regimens to avoid T-cell toxicities associated with agents like bendamustine. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-)
