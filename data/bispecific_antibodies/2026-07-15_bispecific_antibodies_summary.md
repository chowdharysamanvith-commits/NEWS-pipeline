1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies in June and July 2026 is characterized by significant venture capital investment and advancement into late-stage clinical trials across oncology, autoimmune, ophthalmology, and hemostasis indications. Multiple biotechnology firms have secured funding to progress bispecific T-cell engagers and dual-targeting antibodies, with a notable concentration on autoimmune diseases and solid tumors (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26). Key developments include Ollin Biosciences advancing a VEGF/Ang2 bispecific into Phase 3 trials and Triveni Bio initiating first-in-human studies for an atopic dermatitis bispecific (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26). Clinical data presented at the ISTH 2026 Congress and ASCO Breakthrough 2026 highlighted sustained bleed reduction in hemostasis disorders and improved survival in lung cancer and multiple myeloma (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/14/3326747/0/en/hemab-therapeutics-presents-clinical-and-preclinical-data-from-sutacimig-in-glanzmann-thrombasthenia-and-factor-vii-deficiency-at-the-isth-2026-congress.html) (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/). Regulatory pathways and manufacturing capabilities for these complex modalities are being supported by increased infrastructure investment, including GMP certifications for complex biologics (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody). The sector demonstrates a strategic shift toward multi-specific modalities, including trispecific T-cell engagers and masked prodrugs, to enhance efficacy and safety profiles (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• OLN324 (Ollin Biosciences) — Mechanism: VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody — Patient population: Patients with dry age-related macular degeneration — Outcome: Toppling Genentech’s Vabysmo in an early trial (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).
• MMT-205 (Memento Medicines) — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody activating Tie2 and inhibiting VEGF — Patient population: Diabetic macular edema and neovascular age-related macular degeneration — Outcome: Described as a potential best-in-class bispecific antibody (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).
• TRIV-573 (Triveni Bio) — Mechanism: Bispecific inhibiting KLK5/7 and Th2 cytokine IL-13 — Patient population: Atopic dermatitis — Outcome: Initiated first-in-human clinical studies in the past few weeks (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).
• Sutacimig (Hemab Therapeutics) — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody — Patient population: Glanzmann Thrombasthenia and Factor VII Deficiency — Outcome: Phase 2 LTE data show sustained bleed reduction and manageable safety and tolerability (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/14/3326747/0/en/hemab-therapeutics-presents-clinical-and-preclinical-data-from-sutacimig-in-glanzmann-thrombasthenia-and-factor-vii-deficiency-at-the-isth-2026-congress.html).
• BiTAC® Platform (VERAXA Biotech) — Mechanism: Bispecific T cell engagers (TCEs) — Patient population: Oncology — Outcome: Utilize a Boolean "AND-gate" logic, requiring the co-expression of two distinct targets on the same cancer cell for activation (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/13/3326056/0/en/veraxa-biotech-to-launch-ai-enabled-drug-discovery-collaboration-with-ardigen-to-support-growing-bitac-pipeline.html).
• Teclistamab (Johnson & Johnson) — Mechanism: Off-the-shelf subcutaneous bispecific antibody that redirects T cells through two targets, BCMA and CD3 — Patient population: Relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma who received 1 to 3 prior lines of therapy — Outcome: At approximately 3 years of follow-up, teclistamab plus daratumumab reduced the risk of disease progression or death by 83.4% compared with standard of care (HR, 0.17; 95% CI, 0.12-0.23; P <.0001) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma/).
• Ivonescimab (Akeso / Summit Therapeutics) — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody — Patient population: Squamous non-small cell lung cancer — Outcome: Reduced the risk of death in patients with squamous non-small cell lung cancer by 34% compared to standard treatment (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/).
• Mosunetuzumab — Mechanism: CD20xCD3 bispecific antibody — Patient population: Low tumor burden follicular lymphoma — Comparator: Rituximab — Trial: Phase 3 SWOG S2308 (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-).
• Epcoritamab — Mechanism: CD20xCD3 bispecific antibody — Patient population: Untreated follicular lymphoma and elderly/frail DLBCL — Comparator: Chemoimmunotherapy and R-miniCVP — Trial: Phase 3 EPCORE FL-2 and Phase 2 study (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/how-are-key-lymphoma-data-from-asco-and-eha-2026-shaping-treatment).
• Surovatamig — Mechanism: CD19-directed bispecific antibody — Patient population: Previously untreated follicular lymphoma — Comparator: Chemotherapy — Trial: Phase 3 SOUNDTRACK F1 (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-).
• PF’4404 (Pfizer) — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody targeting VEGF and PD-L1 — Patient population: Early-stage lung cancer and tumors expressing IB6 — Outcome: Combination with sigvotatug vedotin to induce immunogenic cell death (https://cancernetwork.com/view/sigvotatug-vedotin-does-not-significantly-improve-survival-in-nsclc-trial).
