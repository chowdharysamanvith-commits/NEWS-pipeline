1. Overview
This Monthly Pharma Intelligence Brief synthesizes data from the provided pharmaceutical news articles, focusing specifically on developments related to bispecific antibodies. The primary intelligence highlights mixed clinical results for Sanofi’s bispecific drug Lunsekimig, which succeeded in asthma and nasal polyps studies but failed in an eczema trial. Compass Therapeutics is advancing multiple bispecific candidates, including tovecimig and CTX-8371, through Phase 2/3 and Phase 1 trials respectively. Merck presented phase 1/2 data for its bispecific antibody MK-2010 in non-small cell lung cancer, demonstrating significant overall response rates. Regulatory and manufacturing infrastructure is expanding, evidenced by WuXi Biologics securing South Korean GMP certification for bispecific manufacturing and Earendil Labs partnering with Sanofi on a $2.56 billion deal. The competitive environment for TIGIT-targeting therapies continues to shift, with AstraZeneca’s rilvegostomig remaining a key candidate following high-profile phase 3 failures by competitors. BCMA-directed bispecific antibodies have evolved into a significant modality for early relapse multiple myeloma, with experts discussing treatment sequencing and toxicity management. (https://biopharmadive.com/news/ai-pharma-scientist-drug-research-owkin/818280/) (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/24/3281064/0/en/compass-therapeutics-to-announce-topline-secondary-endpoints-from-the-phase-2-3-companion-002-study-of-tovecimig-in-patients-with-biliary-tract-cancer.html) (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/) (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing) (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/) (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/gilead-loosens-ties-arcus-after-tigit-dream-finally-ends-phase-3-failure) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/discussing-proper-utilization-of-bcma-bispecifics-in-early-relapse-multiple-myeloma/)

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Lunsekimig — Mechanism of action: Bispecific drug aimed at two popular targets — Trial phase: Mid-stage tests — Patient population: Patients with asthma, nasal polyps, and eczema — Clinical endpoints or results: Succeeded in studies for asthma and nasal polyps but missed its mark in an eczema trial. (https://biopharmadive.com/news/ai-pharma-scientist-drug-research-owkin/818280/)
• Tovecimig — Mechanism of action: DLL4 x VEGF-A bispecific antibody — Trial phase: Phase 2/3 COMPANION-002 study — Patient population: Patients with biliary tract cancer — Clinical endpoints or results: Primary endpoint of overall response rate met; Secondary endpoints (PFS/OS) expected in April 2026. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/24/3281064/0/en/compass-therapeutics-to-announce-topline-secondary-endpoints-from-the-phase-2-3-companion-002-study-of-tovecimig-in-patients-with-biliary-tract-cancer.html)
• CTX-8371 — Mechanism of action: PD-1 x PD-L1 bispecific antibody — Trial phase: Phase 1 study — Patient population: Triple-negative breast cancer and non-small cell lung cancer — Clinical endpoints or results: Doses of 3.0 mg/kg and 10.0 mg/kg being evaluated. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/24/3281064/0/en/compass-therapeutics-to-announce-topline-secondary-endpoints-from-the-phase-2-3-companion-002-study-of-tovecimig-in-patients-with-biliary-tract-cancer.html)
• MK-2010 — Mechanism of action: Bispecific antibody — Trial phase: Phase 1/2 — Patient population: PD-L1-positive NSCLC patients — Clinical endpoints or results: 55% overall response rate in untreated patients, 18-22% in previously treated cohorts. (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/)
• PBS293-MMAE — Mechanism of action: Cis-bispecific antibody-drug conjugate utilizing an "AND-gate" mechanism — Trial phase: Preclinical — Patient population: KRAS mutant colorectal cell line HCT116 — Comparator treatment: Cetuximab-MMAE — Clinical endpoints or results: 50x greater potency and reduced cytotoxicity in normal human keratinocytes. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/21/3277652/0/en/promatix-biosciences-highlights-the-power-of-its-logic-gated-proteomics-driven-bispecific-discovery-platform-to-expand-the-tumour-selective-target-space-for-next-generation-adcs-at.html)
• BCMA-directed bispecifics — Mechanism of action: Bispecific antibodies promote T-cell redirection and activation against myeloma cells — Trial phase: Phase 3 MajesTEC-3 trial — Patient population: Patients with early relapse multiple myeloma — Clinical endpoints or results: Responses with teclistamab and daratumumab are described as "magnitudes of order" better than other second-line and later therapies. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/discussing-proper-utilization-of-bcma-bispecifics-in-early-relapse-multiple-myeloma/)
• Talquetamab-tgvs — Mechanism of action: Bispecific antibody (T-cell redirecting) — Trial phase: Clinical practice/Real-world data — Patient population: Relapsed/Refractory Multiple Myeloma — Clinical endpoints or results: Used frequently as bispecific bridging therapy with 3 ramp-up doses and 1 dose as an outpatient to stabilize disease. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/analyzing-the-role-of-celmods-in-the-relapsed-refractory-myeloma-space/)
