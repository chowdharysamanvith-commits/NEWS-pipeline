1. Overview
The pharmaceutical sector is witnessing significant investment in PD-1/VEGF bispecific antibody programs, driven by initial successes such as ivonescimab in lung cancer trials (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/). While lung cancer remains a primary focus, analysts suggest indications like hepatocellular carcinoma may offer better mechanistic payoff for this modality (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/). Recent ASCO 2026 data highlighted a competitive landscape between bispecifics and antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) (https://biopharmadive.com/news/asco-2026-daraxonrasib-ivonescimab-proteus-erleada-results/821622/). Manufacturing capabilities for bispecific antibodies are expanding rapidly in the Asia-Pacific region, particularly in China (https://biospectrumasia.com/analysis/38/27704/cdmos-atmp-growth-upswing-in-apac.html). Concurrently, U.S. legislative efforts aim to increase scrutiny on biotechnology licensing deals involving Chinese entities, potentially impacting cross-border development (https://biopharmadive.com/news/binsa-china-biotech-deals-house-moolenaar-dingell/821841/). Significant licensing agreements include Innovent Biologics securing a $1.2 billion partnership with Takeda for IBI363 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/innovent-fresh-12b-deal-posts-phase-3-win-cldn182-adc). Regeneron Pharmaceuticals expanded its alliance with CytomX Therapeutics to develop conditionally active bispecifics, increasing the deal's potential value to approximately $4 billion (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/regeneron-pays-37m-adds-2b-biobucks-expand-cytomx-pact). Gilead Sciences completed an acquisition of Ouro Medicines to advance gamgertamig, a T-cell engager for autoimmune diseases (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/04/3307098/0/en/gilead-sciences-and-lakefront-complete-acquisition-of-ouro-medicines-to-further-expand-inflammation-pipeline.html). Analytical workflows combining icIEF and mass photometry are being adopted to address structural complexity in bispecific antibody characterization (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/combining-icief-and-mass-photometry-to-simplify-antibody-characterization-17227). WuXi Biologics achieved Good Manufacturing Practice certification in South Korea for the commercial production of a bispecific antibody (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Ivonescimab demonstrated a 34% reduction in the risk of death in a China-based Phase 3 trial compared to standard chemotherapy plus a PD-1 inhibitor (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/).
• Global Phase 3 data for ivonescimab showed a 21% reduction in death risk compared to chemotherapy alone, which was not statistically significant (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/).
• Rilvegostomig demonstrated substantial activity in combination with trastuzumab deruxtecan for HER2-negative breast cancer within the I-SPY 2.2 trial (https://cancernetwork.com/view/rilvegostomig-combo-demonstrates-activity-in-her2-negative-breast-cancer).
• Izalontamab brengitecan met dual primary endpoints in phase 3 testing for metastatic triple-negative breast cancer (https://cancernetwork.com/view/izalontamab-brengitecan-improves-os-pfs-in-advanced-metastatic-tnbc).
• Cullinan CLN-978: CD19xCD3 T cell engager mechanism; Phase 1; Patients with treatment-refractory moderate to severe SLE and difficult-to-treat RA; Clinical benefit including remissions demonstrated (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/06/3307681/0/en/cullinan-therapeutics-presents-initial-clinical-data-for-cln-978-a-cd19xcd3-t-cell-engager-at-the-eular-2026-congress.html).
• Shattuck SL-846: DR3xIL-23R blocking bispecific antibody mechanism; IND-enabling toxicology study; Preclinical data showed equipotent or more potent than risankizumab and icotrokinra controls (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/08/3307885/0/en/shattuck-labs-announces-phase-1-results-for-sl-325.html).
• Bio-Techne / Refeyn: icIEF fractionation and mass photometry — Bispecific antibodies — Simultaneous assessment of charge and size variants at single-molecule resolution in a four-hour workflow (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/bio-techne-and-refeyn-close-critical-gap-in-bispecific-antibody-and-biosimilar-characterization-17223).
• WuXi Biologics received Good Manufacturing Practice certification from South Korea’s Ministry of Food and Drug Safety for three manufacturing facilities (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing).
• The certification enables end-to-end commercial manufacturing across drug substance, fill-finish, and packaging for complex biologics (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing).
• Genentech glofitamab: Bispecific antibody — SKYGLO Trial — CD20-positive large B-cell lymphoma — Evaluating addition to polatuzumab vedotin and R-CHP as frontline treatment (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard).
• Genmab/AbbVie epcoritamab: Subcutaneous bispecific antibody — EPCORE DLBCL-2 Trial — Newly diagnosed, high-risk DLBCL — Assessing addition to R-CHOP (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard).
