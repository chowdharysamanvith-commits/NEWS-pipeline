1. Overview
The bispecific antibody sector demonstrates sustained investor interest and clinical momentum despite broader challenges in immuno-oncology modalities. Significant venture capital funding was secured by EpiBiologics, Third Arc Bio, and Sidewinder Therapeutics to advance bispecific programs into clinical trials. Strategic partnerships emerged, including a ~$2.56 billion licensing agreement between Sanofi and Earendil Labs for autoimmune bispecific antibodies. Regulatory frameworks are evolving with the FDA Modernization Act 2.0 encouraging alternatives to animal testing for antibody-based therapeutics. Market analysis projects a $60 billion opportunity for the bispecific antibody market by 2031 and a $2 billion opportunity for bispecific antibody-drug conjugates by 2030. Clinical focus areas include relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma, where bispecifics are utilized as bridging therapy, and ulcerative colitis. UCB is identified as a company betting on T-cell engagers to treat autoimmune conditions alongside these oncology-focused developments.
