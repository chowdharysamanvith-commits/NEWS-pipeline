1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies is characterized by emerging biotech entrants advancing clinical candidates and established contract development and manufacturing organizations (CDMOs) expanding specialized production capabilities in the Asia-Pacific region. (https://biopharmadive.com/news/bionyra-immune-drug-startup-sofinnova-jeito/823296/) (https://biospectrumasia.com/analysis/38/27704/cdmos-atmp-growth-upswing-in-apac.html)
Bionyra emerged from stealth with significant funding to develop next-generation immune drugs, including a bispecific antibody targeting inflammatory bowel disease (IBD). (https://biopharmadive.com/news/bionyra-immune-drug-startup-sofinnova-jeito/823296/)
Concurrently, manufacturing infrastructure in China is evolving to support the complex production requirements of bispecific and multispecific antibodies, with major CDMOs securing partnerships to enhance development and commercialization pipelines. (https://biospectrumasia.com/analysis/38/27704/cdmos-atmp-growth-upswing-in-apac.html)
Significant capital allocation occurred in June 2026, with Ollin Biosciences advancing its VEGF/Ang2 bispecific candidate into Phase 3 trials following a substantial Series B funding round. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet)
Regulatory milestones were achieved by NovaBridge Biosciences, which received FDA Fast Track Designation for givastomig for first-line HER2-negative metastatic gastric cancer. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/29/3318837/0/en/novabridge-biosciences-appoints-dr-srishti-gupta-as-chief-executive-officer.html)
In oncology, bispecific antibodies are increasingly positioned as standard-of-care options in hematologic malignancies, with Johnson & Johnson receiving a positive CHMP opinion for teclistamab plus daratumumab in relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma)
Strategic focus is shifting toward chemotherapy-free regimens in follicular lymphoma and optimizing target selection in PD-1/VEGF bispecific development. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/how-are-key-lymphoma-data-from-asco-and-eha-2026-shaping-treatment)
Manufacturing intelligence strategies aim to optimize bioprocess efficiency and ensure product quality for complex therapeutic proteins. (https://lifescienceleader.com/doc/from-process-monitoring-to-manufacturing-intelligence-real-time-analytics-and-predictive-control-in-biomanufacturing-0001)
These developments indicate a strategic shift toward dual-targeting biologics to address unmet needs in immune conditions where single-target therapies show limitations. (https://biopharmadive.com/news/bionyra-immune-drug-startup-sofinnova-jeito/823296/)

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• BYN-003 — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody that combines TL1A with a subtype of IL-23, an anti-inflammatory target. — Phase: Phase 1. — Patient Population: Inflammatory bowel disease and atopic dermatitis. — Outcome: Claims to be the only bispecific molecule for these diseases that already has some level of clinical validation for one of its building blocks. (https://biopharmadive.com/news/bionyra-immune-drug-startup-sofinnova-jeito/823296/)
• OLN324 — Mechanism: VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody. — Phase: Phase 3. — Patient Population: Patients with diabetic macular edema and wet age-related macular degeneration. — Outcome: Topped Vabysmo in an earlier Phase 1b trial for diabetic macular edema. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet)
• Givastomig — Mechanism: Conditional T-cell activation through the 4-1BB signaling pathway in the tumor microenvironment where CLDN18.2 is expressed. — Phase: Phase 1b data demonstrated robust efficacy and favorable tolerability in combination with immunochemotherapy for first-line gastric cancer. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/29/3318837/0/en/novabridge-biosciences-appoints-dr-srishti-gupta-as-chief-executive-officer.html)
• Teclistamab — Mechanism: Redirects T cells through 2 targets, BCMA and CD3. — Trial: Phase 3 MajesTEC-3. — Patient Population: Adult patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma who have received at least 1 prior therapy. — Results: Reduced risk of disease progression or death by 83.4% (HR, 0.17); 3-year OS rate 83.3% vs 65.0%. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma)
• Ivonescimab — Mechanism: Combines the activity of two cancer medicines in a single candidate. — Phase: Clinical Trial. — Patient Population: Squamous non-small cell lung cancer. — Outcome: Reduced the risk of death in squamous non-small cell lung cancer by 34%. (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/)
• GS Ori-Go — Mechanism: Vector technology. — Outcome: Demonstrates strong bulk pool stability and more than a 30% increase in titers across multiple modalities, including monoclonal antibodies, bispecifics, and fusion proteins. (https://drug-dev.com/lonza-advances-integrated-dna-to-ind-programs-through-acceleration-de-risking-and-innovation/)
• Surovatamig (AZD0486) — Mechanism: Novel CD19-directed bispecific antibody. — Trial: Phase 3 SOUNDTRACK F1. — Patient Population: Untreated follicular lymphoma. — Comparator: Chemotherapy. — Regimen: Surovatamig with rituximab. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-)
• Epcoritamab — Mechanism: CD20 bispecific antibody. — Trial: Phase 3 EPCORE FL-2. — Patient Population: Previously untreated follicular lymphoma. — Comparator: Chemoimmunotherapy and maintenance. — Regimen: Epcoritamab plus rituximab and lenalidomide. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-)
