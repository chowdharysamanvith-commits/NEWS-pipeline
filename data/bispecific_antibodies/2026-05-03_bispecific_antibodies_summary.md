MONTHLY PHARMA INTELLIGENCE BRIEF

1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies saw significant clinical data presentations at the AACR 2026 congress, highlighting advancements in oncology therapeutics. (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/) Hemab Therapeutics secured significant capital through an initial public offering to advance its bispecific antibody program, sutacimig, for rare clotting disorders. (https://biopharmadive.com/news/seaport-hemab-ipo-price-biotech-2026/818965/) Pfizer reported positive Phase 3 data for its dual-acting antibody drug, Elrexfio, in multiple myeloma, positioning it against Johnson & Johnson’s approved bispecific Tecvayli. (https://biopharmadive.com/news/fda-real-time-trials-abbvie-kestrel-lilly-profluent/818719/) Compass Therapeutics faced a sharp stock decline after its DLL4xVEGF-A bispecific antibody, tovecimig, failed to meet the overall survival endpoint in a Phase 2/3 study for biliary tract cancer. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/compass-therapeutics-stock-heads-south-after-cancer-bispecific-misses-overall-survival) BeOne Medicines received FDA priority review for zanidatamab, a HER2-targeted bispecific antibody, in combination with tislelizumab and chemotherapy for first-line HER2-positive gastric cancers. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/fda-grants-priority-review-to-tislelizumab-zanidatamab-in-1l-her2-gastric-cancers) Manufacturing capabilities are expanding to support these complex modalities, with Thermo Fisher Scientific opening a dedicated Bioprocess Design Center to accelerate biologics development. (https://biospectrumasia.com/news/41/27560/thermo-fisher-scientific-opens-flagship-bioprocess-design-centre-in-us.html) WuXi Biologics secured South Korean Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) certification for the commercial manufacturing of a bispecific antibody targeting biliary tract cancer. (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing) Earendil Labs and Sanofi have entered a licensing agreement valued at approximately $2.56 billion to advance bispecific antibodies for autoimmune diseases. (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/) Bristol Myers Squibb terminated a legacy collaboration with Zymeworks, discontinuing a CD40/FAP bispecific antibody in phase 1 development. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bms-calls-time-zymeworks-collab-ending-work-phase-1-stage-cancer-bispecific) Janux Therapeutics halted clinical development of its masked T-cell engager JANX008 due to subpar activity in solid tumors. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/janux-axes-solid-tumor-t-cell-engager-after-poor-phase-1-showing)

2. Key Developments
• Hemab Therapeutics — sutacimig — Glanzmann thrombasthenia — Phase 3 advancement planned for Glanzmann thrombasthenia this year; mid-stage trial data expected late 2026 or early 2027 for Factor VII syndrome (https://biopharmadive.com/news/seaport-hemab-ipo-price-biotech-2026/818965/).
• Pfizer — Elrexfio — Multiple Myeloma — Phase 3 trial met main objective by delaying progression or death compared to standard therapy (https://biopharmadive.com/news/fda-real-time-trials-abbvie-kestrel-lilly-profluent/818719/).
• Compass Therapeutics — Tovecimig — Unresectable advanced, metastatic or recurrent biliary tract cancer — Phase 2/3 study met primary endpoint for Overall Response Rate (17.1% vs 5.3%) and Progression-Free Survival (4.7 months vs 2.6 months) but missed Overall Survival endpoint (8.9 months vs 9.4 months) (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/compass-therapeutics-stock-heads-south-after-cancer-bispecific-misses-overall-survival).
• Merck — MK-2010 — PD-L1-positive non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) — Phase 1/2 trial data showing 55% ORR in untreated patients (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/).
• BeOne Medicines — Zanidatamab — HER2+ Gastric/GEJ/Esophageal Adenocarcinoma — FDA Priority Review for sBLA in combination with tislelizumab and chemotherapy (https://cancernetwork.com/view/fda-grants-priority-review-to-tislelizumab-zanidatamab-in-1l-her2-gastric-cancers).
• Amgen — Tarlatamab — Small Cell Lung Cancer — Phase 3 (Approved/Combination) (https://the-scientist.com/next-generation-precision-medicine-platforms-come-for-cancer-74414).
• Janux Therapeutics — JANX008 — Metastatic solid tumors — Discontinued due to subpar activity (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/janux-axes-solid-tumor-t-cell-engager-after-poor-phase-1-showing).
• Bristol Myers Squibb — CD40/FAP bispecific antibody — Solid tumors — Discontinued/Removed from pipeline (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bms-calls-time-zymeworks-collab-ending-work-phase-1-stage-cancer-bispecific).
• WuXi Biologics — Bispecific Antibody — Biliary Tract Cancer — GMP Certification (South Korea) (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing).
• Earendil Labs — Bispecific Antibodies — Autoimmune Diseases — Licensing Deal (~$2.56B) (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
- sutacimig — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody targeting clotting pathways — Trial Phase: Phase 2 completed, Phase 3 planned — Patient Population: Glanzmann thrombasthenia (rare genetic bleeding disorder) and Factor VII deficiency — Comparator: Not explicitly stated for Phase 2, Phase 3 design not detailed — Clinical Endpoints: Clinically meaningful bleeding reduction in Phase 2 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/seaport-hemab-chart-courses-180m-ipos-fund-depression-clotting-candidates).
- Elrexfio — Mechanism: Dual-acting antibody drug — Trial Phase: Phase 3 — Patient Population: Multiple Myeloma — Comparator: Johnson & Johnson’s Darzalex, Bristol Myers Squibb’s Pomalyst, and dexamethasone — Clinical Endpoints: Met main objective by delaying progression or death compared to standard therapy (https://biopharmadive.com/news/fda-real-time-trials-abbvie-kestrel-lilly-profluent/818719/).
- Tovecimig — Mechanism: DLL4xVEGF-A bispecific antibody — Trial Phase: Phase 2/3 — Patient Population: 168 adults with unresectable advanced, metastatic or recurrent biliary tract cancer — Comparator: Chemotherapy alone — Clinical Endpoints: Overall Response Rate 17.1% vs 5.3% (met), Progression-Free Survival 4.7 months vs 2.6 months (met), Overall Survival 8.9 months vs 9.4 months (missed) (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/compass-therapeutics-stock-heads-south-after-cancer-bispecific-misses-overall-survival).
- MK-2010 — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody (side effects include hypertension and excess protein in urine, common with VEGF inhibitors) — Trial Phase: Phase 1/2 — Patient Population: PD-L1-positive NSCLC (previously untreated and previously treated) — Comparator: Not explicitly stated, but compared to Akeso ivonescimab and BioNTech pumitamig — Clinical Endpoints: 55% ORR in untreated patients at 20 mg/kg; 18-22% ORR in previously treated patients (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/).
- Zanidatamab — Mechanism: HER2-targeted bispecific antibody — Trial Phase: Phase 3 HERIZON-GEA-01 — Patient Population: Unresectable, locally advanced or metastatic HER2-positive adenocarcinoma of the stomach, GEJ, or esophagus — Comparator: Trastuzumab and physician’s choice of chemotherapy (Arm A) — Clinical Endpoints: Median PFS 12.4 months vs 8.1 months (HR 0.63); Median OS 26.4 months vs 19.2 months (HR 0.72) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/fda-grants-priority-review-to-tislelizumab-zanidatamab-in-1l-her2-gastric-cancers).
- Tarlatamab — Mechanism: Bispecific T-cell engager targeting CD3 and DLL3 — Trial Phase: Phase 3 — Patient Population: Small Cell Lung Cancer — Comparator: Not explicitly stated in summary — Clinical Endpoints: Improved median overall survival from 8.3 months to 13.6 months; Combination with an anti-PD-L1 antibody increased median overall survival to 25.3 months (https://the-scientist.com/next-generation-precision-medicine-platforms-come-for-cancer-74414).
- JANX008 — Mechanism: Masked T-cell engager (binds EGFR and CD3) — Trial Phase: Phase 1 — Patient Population: Metastatic solid tumors — Comparator: Not mentioned — Clinical Endpoints: Subpar magnitude and consistency of activity (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/janux-axes-solid-tumor-t-cell-engager-after-poor-phase-1-showing).
