1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape regarding bispecific antibodies is marked by significant advancements in development, manufacturing capabilities, and regulatory approvals across oncology, autoimmune, and infectious disease sectors. Bionyra emerged from stealth with $165 million to advance a pipeline including a bispecific antibody targeting inflammatory bowel disease and atopic dermatitis (https://biopharmadive.com/news/bionyra-immune-drug-startup-sofinnova-jeito/823296/). Concurrently, Contract Development and Manufacturing Organisations (CDMOs) in China and South Korea are increasing infrastructure to support the production of bispecific antibodies alongside other advanced therapies (https://biospectrumasia.com/analysis/38/27704/cdmos-atmp-growth-upswing-in-apac.html). Industry investment remains active, with venture capital firms co-leading funding rounds for startups developing next-generation biologics (https://biopharmadive.com/news/bionyra-immune-drug-startup-sofinnova-jeito/823296/). China Medical System secured approval for Silevimig, identified as the world’s first bispecific antibody for passive immunization against rabies (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/22/3315186/0/en/cms-class-1-innovative-drug-silevimig-injection-approved-in-china-becoming-the-world-s-first-bispecific-antibody-for-passive-immunization-against-rabies.html). Johnson & Johnson reported positive Phase 3 data for talquetamab in combination with daratumumab for multiple myeloma, supporting earlier line treatment strategies (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/18/3314021/0/de/neue-daten-zu-talvey-talquetamab-in-kombination-mit-daratumumab-belegen-das-potenzial-einer-neuartigen-bispezifischen-kombinationsbehandlung-bei-rezidiviertem-oder-refrakt%c3%a4rem-mult.html). Akeso and Summit Therapeutics presented clinical data for ivonescimab, which reduced the risk of death by 34% in squamous non-small cell lung cancer (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/). Strategic partnerships are forming to enable the development and manufacturing of complex antibody formats, such as the $2.56 billion deal between Earendil Labs and Sanofi (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/). These developments indicate a shift toward dual-targeting modalities to address unmet needs in immune conditions and oncology where single-target therapies face efficacy ceilings (https://biopharmadive.com/news/bionyra-immune-drug-startup-sofinnova-jeito/823296/).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• BYN-003 — Bispecific antibody mechanism combining TL1A and IL-23 — Phase 1 — Inflammatory bowel disease and atopic dermatitis — Claims to be the only bispecific molecule for these diseases with clinical validation for one building block (https://biopharmadive.com/news/bionyra-immune-drug-startup-sofinnova-jeito/823296/)
• Talquetamab — GPRC5DxCD3 bispecific antibody engaging T cells — Phase 3 MonumenTAL-3 — Relapsed or Refractory Multiple Myeloma — Comparator: Daratumumab + Pomalidomide + Dexamethasone (DPd) — Progression-free survival of 81.3% and overall survival of 89.2% at 24 months in combination with daratumumab (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/18/3314021/0/de/neue-daten-zu-talvey-talquetamab-in-kombination-mit-daratumumab-belegen-das-potenzial-einer-neuartigen-bispezifischen-kombinationsbehandlung-bei-rezidiviertem-oder-refrakt%c3%a4rem-mult.html)
• Ivonescimab — Combines the activity of two best-selling cancer medicines — Clinical Trial — Squamous non-small cell lung cancer — Reduced the risk of death by 34% compared to standard treatment in a clinical trial conducted in China (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/)
• GS Ori-Go Vector — Supports lead-clone titers higher than 11 g/L with more than a 30% increase in titers across bispecifics — Biomanufacturing Technology (https://drug-dev.com/lonza-advances-integrated-dna-to-ind-programs-through-acceleration-de-risking-and-innovation/)
• Single-Cell Spatial Pharmacobiology (SSP) — Reveals specific extracellular matrix barriers to drug delivery for large molecules like multispecific antibodies — Vanderbilt University Medical Center Tool (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/new-single-cell-tool-shows-why-antibody-based-drugs-fail-17268)
• MMT-205 — Bispecific antibody which activates Tie2 and inhibits VEGF — IND-enabling studies — Retinal diseases (nAMD, DME) — Greater Tie2 activation and improved integrity of retinal vasculature in preclinical models (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• PRO-203 — Binds CD20+ B-cells and CD3 on T-cells to form an immunological synapse for T-cell dependent cellular cytotoxicity (TDCC) — Phase 1/2 — Systemic Sclerosis / Lupus Nephritis (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/22/3315254/0/en/prolium-bioscience-announces-first-patient-dosed-in-its-multinational-phase-1-2-study-of-pro-203-in-systemic-sclerosis-and-last-patient-last-visit-in-an-investigator-led-study-of-p.html)
• Silevimig — Indicated for passive immunization against rabies and is administered via injection — Regulatory Approval (China) (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/22/3315186/0/en/cms-class-1-innovative-drug-silevimig-injection-approved-in-china-becoming-the-world-s-first-bispecific-antibody-for-passive-immunization-against-rabies.html)
• Obrixtamig — Bispecific Antibodies and T-cell engager therapies — Phase 3 — Oncology (Solid Tumours) (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/22/3315071/0/en/oxford-biotherapeutics-third-partnered-programme-with-boehringer-ingelheim-enters-the-clinic-and-its-first-obrixtamig-advances-to-phase-3.html)
• Glofitamab — Bispecific antibody (CD20-positive) — Phase 3 (SKYGLO trial) — CD20-positive large B-cell lymphoma — Comparator: Polatuzumab vedotin and R-CHP (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard)
• Epcoritamab — Subcutaneous bispecific antibody — Phase 3 (EPCORE DLBCL-2 trial) — Newly diagnosed, high-risk DLBCL — Comparator: R-CHOP (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard)
• VIR-5500 — Bispecific PSMA and CD3-binding T-cell engager with PRO-XTEN masking technology — Phase 1 — Advanced, metastatic prostate cancer — Designed to reduce off-target effects and improve therapeutic index (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2026/)
