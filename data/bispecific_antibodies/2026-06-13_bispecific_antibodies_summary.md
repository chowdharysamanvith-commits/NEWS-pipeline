1. Overview
The biopharmaceutical landscape in June 2026 demonstrates sustained investment and strategic activity centered on bispecific antibody technologies and related modalities (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fueled-ma-and-partnerships-biotech-poised-continued-growth-ey). Ernst & Young projects biotech revenues will grow by at least 12% in 2026, driven significantly by M&A and partnerships, with Chinese biotech companies increasingly viewed as credible sources for advanced technologies like bispecific antibodies (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fueled-ma-and-partnerships-biotech-poised-continued-growth-ey). Multiple biotechnology firms have secured substantial venture capital funding to advance bispecific programs into clinical development (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26). The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies is expanding beyond relapsed/refractory settings into frontline treatment paradigms, driven by positive clinical trial designs (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard). Regulatory progress is evident in China, where anbenitamab received approval for gastric cancer in May 2026, signaling accelerated access pathways for advanced therapies in the region (https://cancernetwork.com/view/anbenitamab-regimen-meets-phase-3-end-points-in-her2-breast-cancer). Recent data indicates that randomized phase 3 trials evaluating bispecific antibodies in untreated patients have completed enrollment, signaling a shift in standard-of-care potential for aggressive B-cell lymphomas (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard). Concurrently, industry leaders are securing pipelines through high-value licensing agreements targeting bispecific and trispecific modalities for solid tumors and prostate cancer (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2026/). Market dynamics show increased deal volume in 2026 driven by cancer and autoimmune drugs, with specific focus on Chinese biotech innovation and licensing (https://pharmavoice.com/news/competition-merck-summit-akeso-cancer-adc-bispecific/822585/).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Ollin Biosciences / VelaVigo Bio: The TSHRxIGF-1R bispecific targets the TSH receptor and IGF-1R to treat thyroid eye disease and Graves’ disease by stopping autoantibodies from activating the receptor (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ethyreal-bio-manifests-101m-pack-biotechs-gunning-amgens-tepezza).
• Third Arc Bio: The lead program, ARC101, is a bispecific T cell engager currently in a phase 1 trial for solid tumors (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).
• EpiBiologics: The lead bispecific antibody, EPI-326, functions as an EGFR degrader and is planned for clinical trials in non-small cell lung cancer and head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).
• Prolium Bioscience: The clinical candidate PRO-203 is a CD20xCD3 T-cell engager already being studied in systemic sclerosis (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).
• Vibrant Therapeutics: The lead asset VIB305 is a masked T-cell engager prodrug designed to treat EGFR-expressing solid tumors and is being evaluated in phase 1 clinical trials in Australia and China (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).
• Crossbow Therapeutics: The lead candidate CBX-250 is a T-cell engager designed to target a peptide human leukocyte antigen (pHLA) found on myeloid cancer cells (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).
• Celldex Therapeutics: CDX-622 is a bispecific antibody targeting Stem Cell Factor (SCF) and Thymic Stromal Lymphopoietin (TSLP) to simultaneously reduce tissue mast cells and inhibit Type 2 inflammatory responses (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/10/3310079/24180/en/celldex-announces-multiple-upcoming-presentations-from-leading-mast-cell-targeting-pipeline-at-the-upcoming-2026-european-academy-of-allergy-and-clinical-immunology-annual-congress.html).
• Cullinan Therapeutics: CLN-978 is a CD19xCD3 bispecific T cell engager engineered for high affinity binding to CD19 and CD3, with a molecular size of 65 kDa and a single-domain antibody binding to human serum albumin to extend half-life (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/10/3309602/0/en/cullinan-therapeutics-shares-new-clinical-data-across-its-portfolio-of-t-cell-engager-programs-targeting-cd19-and-bcma-in-autoimmune-diseases.html).
• Cullinan Therapeutics: In the OUTRACE SLE trial, 10 of 14 patients (71%) with ≥4 weeks follow-up achieved a ≥4-point reduction in hSLEDAI, with 5 achieving DORIS remission (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/10/3309602/0/en/cullinan-therapeutics-shares-new-clinical-data-across-its-portfolio-of-t-cell-engager-programs-targeting-cd19-and-bcma-in-autoimmune-diseases.html).
• Cullinan Therapeutics: In the OUTRACE RA trial, 5 of 7 patients (71%) showed disease activity improvement, including one DAS28-ESR remission in a patient treated with a single 30 μg target dose (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/10/3309602/0/en/cullinan-therapeutics-shares-new-clinical-data-across-its-portfolio-of-t-cell-engager-programs-targeting-cd19-and-bcma-in-autoimmune-diseases.html).
• Cullinan Therapeutics: Velinotamig is a BCMAxCD3 T cell engager currently in Phase 1 development for autoimmune diseases (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/10/3309602/0/en/cullinan-therapeutics-shares-new-clinical-data-across-its-portfolio-of-t-cell-engager-programs-targeting-cd19-and-bcma-in-autoimmune-diseases.html).
• Genentech/Genmab: Glofitamab and Epcoritamab are in Phase 3 trials for newly diagnosed high-risk DLBCL or CD20-positive large B-cell lymphoma, comparing against standard chemotherapy regimens (R-CHOP or polatuzumab vedotin plus R-CHP) (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard).
• AbbVie/RemeGen: RC148 is a PD-1/VEGF-targeted bispecific antibody where early clinical studies showed favorable antitumor activity in combination with an ADC (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2026/).
• BeOne Medicines/Huahui Health: HH160 is a trispecific antibody targeting PD-1, CTLA-4, and VEGF-A with positive preclinical results presented at 2025 AACR (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2026/).
• Astellas/Vir Biotechnology: VIR-5500 is a bispecific PSMA and CD3-binding T-cell engager with PRO-XTEN masking technology in Phase 1 development for advanced, metastatic prostate cancer (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2026/).
• CSPC Pharmaceutical Group: Anbenitamab mechanism of action is designed as a HER2 bispecific antibody targeting HER2-positive malignancies (https://cancernetwork.com/view/anbenitamab-regimen-meets-phase-3-end-points-in-her2-breast-cancer).
• CSPC Pharmaceutical Group: Phase 3 HEALER trial (NCT05838066) evaluated anbenitamab plus albumin-bound docetaxel versus trastuzumab, pertuzumab, and docetaxel (THP) in approximately 880 patients with HER2-positive advanced breast cancer (https://cancernetwork.com/view/anbenitamab-regimen-meets-phase-3-end-points-in-her2-breast-cancer).
• CSPC Pharmaceutical Group: Phase 3 Neo-Healer trial (NCT06747338) primary endpoint was total pathological complete response (tpCR; ypT0/is ypN0) assessed by a blinded independent review committee in 521 patients with early or locally advanced HER2-positive breast cancer (https://cancernetwork.com/view/anbenitamab-regimen-meets-phase-3-end-points-in-her2-breast-cancer).
