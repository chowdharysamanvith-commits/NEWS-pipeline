1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies is currently defined by intense competition in the PD-1/VEGF space, led by Akeso’s ivonescimab, alongside significant regulatory scrutiny regarding cross-border licensing deals with Chinese biotechnology firms (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/). While ivonescimab demonstrated a 34% reduction in death risk in a China-based Phase 3 lung cancer trial, global data released in May 2025 showed a non-statistically significant 21% reduction, sparking debate over the optimal indication between lung and liver cancers (https://biopharmadive.com/news/asco-2026-daraxonrasib-ivonescimab-proteus-erleada-results/821622/). Major players including BioNTech, Merck & Co., and Pfizer are advancing their own PD-1/VEGF bispecific programs, though analysts suggest liver tumors may offer a better payoff due to VEGF pathway reliance (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/). Concurrently, the U.S. House has proposed the Biotech Investment National Security Act to increase scrutiny on licensing deals involving China-based drugmakers, impacting partnerships such as those between Bristol Myers Squibb and Hengrui Pharma (https://biopharmadive.com/news/binsa-china-biotech-deals-house-moolenaar-dingell/821841/). Mergers and acquisitions remain active, with RallyBio merging with Avenzo Therapeutics to consolidate a pipeline including bispecific ADCs, and UCB acquiring Candid Therapeutics for its bispecific antibody portfolio (https://biopharmadive.com/news/avenzo-rallybio-reverse-merger-cancer-drugs/821613/). Regeneron Pharmaceuticals expanded its collaboration with CytomX Therapeutics to develop conditionally active bispecifics, increasing the deal's potential value to approximately $4 billion (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/regeneron-pays-37m-adds-2b-biobucks-expand-cytomx-pact). Takeda and Innovent Biologics presented long-term Phase 1 data for their PD-1/IL-2 bispecific IBI363 in non-small cell lung cancer, showing durable survival benefits (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/asco-long-term-early-data-backs-tremendous-potential-takedas-innovent-oncology-collab). Manufacturing infrastructure is also expanding to support these complex modalities, with Dark Horse Consulting Group partnering with Altruist Biologics to streamline biologics production in China (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/03/3305905/0/en/dark-horse-consulting-group-and-altruist-biologics-announce-memorandum-of-understanding-to-advance-biologics-manufacturing-and-development-in-china.html). WuXi Biologics secured Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) certification from South Korea’s Ministry of Food and Drug Safety for facilities enabling commercial manufacturing of a bispecific antibody targeting biliary tract cancer (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing). These developments underscore a growing industry focus on complex biologics modalities and the infrastructure required to support their global supply chains (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Ivonescimab — Mechanism: PD-1 and VEGF bispecific — Phase: Phase 3 — Patient Population: Squamous non-small cell lung cancer — Comparator: Standard chemotherapy plus a PD-1 inhibitor — Results: 34% reduction in risk of death in China-based trial (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/)
• IBI363 — Mechanism: PD-1/IL-2 alpha-biased bispecific — Phase: Phase 1 — Patient Population: Advanced immunotherapy-resistant non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) — Comparator: None specified in text — Results: 3 mg dose group in squamous NSCLC achieved median progression-free survival of 10.1 months and median overall survival of 18.2 months (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/asco-long-term-early-data-backs-tremendous-potential-takedas-innovent-oncology-collab)
• CLN-978 — Mechanism: CD19xCD3 T cell engager — Phase: Phase 1 — Patient Population: Treatment-refractory moderate to severe SLE and difficult-to-treat RA — Comparator: None specified — Results: Clinical benefit, including remissions, demonstrated in both SLE and RA patients following a single target dose (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/06/3307681/0/en/cullinan-therapeutics-presents-initial-clinical-data-for-cln-978-a-cd19xcd3-t-cell-engager-at-the-eular-2026-congress.html)
• Teclistamab — Mechanism: BCMA-directed bispecific antibody — Phase: Retrospective Cohort Study — Patient Population: Multiple Myeloma — Comparator: BCMA-directed CAR T-cell therapy — Results: Lower incidence of immune-mediated toxicities such as cytokine release syndrome compared to CAR T (https://cancernetwork.com/view/bcma-car-t-cell-therapy-improves-os-vs-teclistamab-in-multiple-myeloma)
• RC148 — Mechanism: PD-1/VEGF-targeted bispecific antibody — Phase: Early Clinical — Patient Population: Advanced solid tumors — Comparator: Combination with ADC — Results: Initial favorable antitumor activity in combination with an ADC (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2025/)
• VIR-5500 — Mechanism: Bispecific PSMA and CD3-binding T-cell engager with PRO-XTEN dual-masked technology — Phase: Phase 1 — Patient Population: Advanced, metastatic prostate cancer — Comparator: None specified — Results: Designed to keep T-cell engagers masked until reaching the tumor microenvironment to reduce off-target effects (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2025/)
