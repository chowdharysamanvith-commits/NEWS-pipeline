1. Overview
The monthly intelligence brief synthesizes pharmaceutical news with a specific focus on bispecific antibody developments derived from the provided collection. Pfizer announced that its dual-acting antibody drug Elrexfio met the main objective in a Phase 3 trial for multiple myeloma. Compass Therapeutics faced market volatility after its DLL4xVEGF-A bispecific antibody tovecimig failed to meet the overall survival endpoint in a phase 2/3 study for biliary tract cancer. Regeneron Pharmaceuticals continues to leverage its VelociSuite technology to produce new classes of bispecific antibodies alongside its broader biologic portfolio. Amgen reported Phase 3 study results for tarlatamab in small cell lung cancer, demonstrating improved median overall survival. Industry storylines indicate AbbVie is actively pursuing opportunities in the PD-1/VEGF bispecific space through strategic business moves. WuXi Biologics secured Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) certification from South Korea’s Ministry of Food and Drug Safety for the commercial manufacturing of a bispecific antibody. Additionally, emerging companies like Promatix Biosciences and iOmx Therapeutics are advancing bispecific platforms targeting dual receptors. These activities highlight the intense competition and innovation within the bispecific antibody sector.

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Elrexfio — Mechanism: Dual-acting antibody drug — Trial Phase: Phase 3 — Patient Population: People whose disease got worse after an initial treatment round — Comparator: Combination of Johnson & Johnson’s Darzalex, Bristol Myers Squibb’s Pomalyst and dexamethasone — Clinical Endpoints: Delay progression or death compared to standard therapy (https://biopharmadive.com/news/fda-real-time-trials-abbvie-kestrel-lilly-profluent/818719/)
• Tovecimig — Mechanism: DLL4xVEGF-A bispecific antibody — Trial Phase: Phase 2/3 — Patient Population: 168 adults with unresectable advanced, metastatic or recurrent biliary tract cancer — Clinical Endpoints: 4.7-month median progression-free survival versus 2.6 months for control; 8.9-month median overall survival versus 9.4 months for control (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/compass-therapeutics-stock-heads-south-after-cancer-bispecific-misses-overall-survival)
• Sutacimig — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody targeting clotting disorders — Trial Phase: Phase 2/3 — Patient Population: Glanzmann thrombasthenia and Factor VII deficiency — Outcome: Phase 2 study demonstrated clinically meaningful bleeding reduction (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/seaport-hemab-chart-courses-180m-ipos-fund-depression-clotting-candidates)
• MK-2010 — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody with side effects including hypertension and excess protein in urine — Trial Phase: Phase 1/2 — Patient Population: Previously untreated PD-L1-positive non-small cell lung cancer — Clinical Endpoints: 55% overall response rate (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/)
• Tarlatamab — Mechanism: Bispecific T cell engager binding CD3 and Delta-like protein 3 (DLL3) — Trial Phase: Phase 3 — Patient Population: Small cell lung cancer — Comparator: Platinum-based chemotherapy — Clinical Endpoints: Median overall survival improved from 8.3 months to 13.6 months (https://the-scientist.com/next-generation-precision-medicine-platforms-come-for-cancer-74414)
• Xaluritamig — Mechanism: Bispecific T cell engager binding CD3 and STEAP1 with avidity-dependent activity — Trial Phase: Phase 3 — Patient Population: Castrate-resistant prostate cancer — Clinical Endpoints: Promising preclinical efficacy and safety studies (https://the-scientist.com/next-generation-precision-medicine-platforms-come-for-cancer-74414)
• Zanidatamab — Mechanism: HER2-targeting bispecific monoclonal antibody — Trial Phase: Phase 3 HERIZON-GEA-01 — Patient Population: Patients with HER2-positive, unresectable, locally advanced, recurrent, or metastatic GEA — Clinical Endpoints: Median PFS 12.4 months (Arm C) vs 8.1 months (Arm A); Median OS 26.4 months (Arm C) vs 19.2 months (Arm A) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/3-things-you-should-know-about-the-evolving-therapeutic-landscape-of-gea)
• Teclistamab — Mechanism: BCMA-directed bispecific antibody — Trial Phase: Phase 3 MajesTEC-3 — Patient Population: Early relapse multiple myeloma patients — Regimen: teclistamab plus daratumumab — Clinical Endpoints: Expected rapid and deep response (https://cancernetwork.com/view/discussing-proper-utilization-of-bcma-bispecifics-in-early-relapse-multiple-myeloma)
• PBS293 — Mechanism: Directed to cell signaling factors EGFR and EphA2 to bind tumor cells and internalize more efficiently than bivalent parental antibodies — Trial Phase: Preclinical — Patient Population: Solid Tumors (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/)
• IOMX-0675 — Mechanism: Blocks immunosuppressive receptors LILRB1 and LILRB2 while sparing closely related activating LILR family members — Trial Phase: Early Clinical — Patient Population: Solid Tumors (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/)
