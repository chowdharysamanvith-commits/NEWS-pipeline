1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies is expanding across ophthalmology, immunology, oncology, and hematology, driven by significant capital investment and clinical validation during June and July 2026. Ollin Biosciences secured $330 million to advance its VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody OLN324 through Phase 3 trials for retinal diseases, positioning it against Genentech’s Vabysmo (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet). Bionyra Pharma emerged from stealth with $165 million to develop a pipeline including the TL1AxIL-23p19 bispecific antibody BYN-003 for inflammatory bowel disease (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets). VERAXA Biotech announced an AI-enabled collaboration with Ardigen to optimize target selection for its BiTAC® pipeline of bispecific T cell engagers and ADCs (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/13/3326056/0/en/veraxa-biotech-to-launch-ai-enabled-drug-discovery-collaboration-with-ardigen-to-support-growing-bitac-pipeline.html). Hemab Therapeutics presented Phase 2 long-term extension data for its bispecific antibody sutacimig in Glanzmann thrombasthenia, supporting a planned Phase 3 initiation (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/12/3325841/0/en/hemab-therapeutics-presents-new-clinical-data-from-hmb-002-in-von-willebrand-disease-and-introduces-hmb-003-for-heavy-menstrual-bleeding-at-the-isth-2026-congress.html). Context Therapeutics reported positive interim efficacy data for its Claudin 6 x CD3 TCE bispecific antibody, CTIM-76, in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/10/3325745/0/en/context-therapeutics-announces-inducement-grant-under-nasdaq-listing-rule-5635-c-4.html). Johnson & Johnson received a positive CHMP opinion for teclistamab plus daratumumab in relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma based on phase 3 data (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma). In the lymphoma space, frontline trials for glofitamab and epcoritamab are evaluating the integration of bispecific antibodies into standard R-CHOP-based regimens (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard). Akeso and Summit Therapeutics presented clinical data for ivonescimab, which demonstrated survival benefits in squamous non-small cell lung cancer (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/). These developments highlight a strategic shift toward dual-targeting modalities to improve therapeutic windows and address unmet medical needs in complex disease areas.

2. Key Developments
• Ollin Biosciences — OLN324 — Diabetic Macular Edema / Wet Age-Related Macular Degeneration — Phase 3 (planned) — OLN324, a VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody, topped Vabysmo in an earlier phase 1b trial and is advancing into at least three Phase 3 trials (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet).
• Ollin Biosciences — OLN102 — Thyroid Eye Disease — Phase 1 (planned) — OLN102 is designed to hit both IGF-1R and thyroid-stimulating hormone receptor (TSHR) and is poised to challenge Amgen’s Tepezza (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet).
• Bionyra Pharma — BYN-003 — Inflammatory Bowel Disease — Phase 1 — BYN-003 is a TL1AxIL-23p19 bispecific antibody with half-life extension technology that began a Phase 1 study in healthy volunteers in April (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets).
• VERAXA Biotech — BiTAC® Pipeline — Oncology — Preclinical/Discovery — The BiTAC platform utilizes bispecific T cell engagers and bispecific ADCs utilizing Boolean "AND-gate" logic to require co-expression of two distinct targets for activation (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/13/3326056/0/en/veraxa-biotech-to-launch-ai-enabled-drug-discovery-collaboration-with-ardigen-to-support-growing-bitac-pipeline.html).
• Hemab Therapeutics — Sutacimig — Glanzmann Thrombasthenia — Phase 2 (Long-Term Extension) — Sutacimig, a bispecific antibody, showed sustained prophylaxis with meaningful reduction in annualized treated bleeding rate (ATBR) in the LTE portion of an ongoing Phase 2 trial (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/12/3325841/0/en/hemab-therapeutics-presents-new-clinical-data-from-hmb-002-in-von-willebrand-disease-and-introduces-hmb-003-for-heavy-menstrual-bleeding-at-the-isth-2026-congress.html).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• OLN324 — Mechanism: VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody; Trial Phase: Phase 3 (planned); Patient Population: Diabetic Macular Edema and Wet Age-Related Macular Degeneration; Outcome: Topped Vabysmo in an earlier phase 1b trial (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet).
• OLN102 — Mechanism: Dual targeting of IGF-1R and thyroid-stimulating hormone receptor (TSHR); Trial Phase: Phase 1 (planned); Patient Population: Thyroid Eye Disease; Outcome: Designed to offer unique clinical outcomes via dual targeting (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet).
