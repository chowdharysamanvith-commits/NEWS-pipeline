1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies in June 2026 is characterized by significant venture capital investment, clinical progression across oncology and ophthalmology, and strategic regulatory milestones. Multiple biotechnology firms secured substantial funding rounds to advance bispecific programs into pivotal trials, with notable activity in eye diseases, solid tumors, and hematologic malignancies. Major players including Johnson & Johnson and Genmab reported positive Phase 3 data supporting bispecific antibodies as standard-of-care options in lymphoma and multiple myeloma. Regulatory advancements included FDA Fast Track Designations and CHMP recommendations, while manufacturing capabilities expanded with GMP certifications in South Korea. Analytical capabilities for characterizing complex biotherapeutics advanced, with mass spectrometry becoming central to biotherapeutic development workflows. The sector also saw increased competition and strategic positioning between bispecific antibodies and antibody-drug conjugates in oncology. 4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• OLN324 — Mechanism: VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody — Indication: Dry age-related macular degeneration / Diabetic Macular Edema — Trial Phase: Phase 3 — Outcome: Phase 1b data showing superiority over Vabysmo in the lead indication (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet)
• BYN-003 — Mechanism: TL1AxIL-23p19 bispecific antibody incorporating half-life extension technology — Indication: Inflammatory Bowel Disease — Trial Phase: Phase 1 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets)
• Epcoritamab — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody (CD20 x CD3) — Indication: Relapsed/Refractory Diffuse Large B-Cell Lymphoma — Trial Phase: Phase 3 (EPCORE DLBCL-4) — Outcome: Statistically significant improvement in progression-free survival when combined with lenalidomide (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/29/3319290/0/en/genmab-announces-positive-phase-3-results-for-epcoritamab-plus-lenalidomide-in-patients-with-relapsed-refractory-diffuse-large-b-cell-lymphoma-demonstrating-statistically-significa.html)
• Givastomig — Mechanism: CLDN18.2 x 4-1BB bispecific antibody that conditionally activates T cells in the tumor microenvironment — Indication: HER2-Negative Metastatic Gastric Cancer — Trial Phase: Phase 1/2 (Fast Track Designation) (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/29/3318837/0/en/novabridge-biosciences-appoints-dr-srishti-gupta-as-chief-executive-officer.html)
• Teclistamab — Mechanism: BCMA x CD3 bispecific antibody redirects T cells — Indication: Relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma — Trial Phase: Phase 3 (MajesTEC-3) — Outcome: 83.4% reduction in risk of progression or death; 3-year OS rate 83.3% vs 65.0% with standard of care (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma)
• Ivonescimab — Mechanism: Combines the activity of two of the best-selling cancer medicines (PD-1/VEGF) — Indication: Squamous Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer — Trial Phase: Phase III — Outcome: Reduced risk of death by 34% compared to standard treatment (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/)
• Sutacimig (HMB-001) — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody — Indication: Glanzmann thrombasthenia and Factor VII deficiency — Trial Phase: Clinical development — Outcome: Granted Breakthrough Therapy Designation (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/26/3318236/0/en/hemab-therapeutics-to-present-clinical-and-preclinical-data-from-multiple-blood-coagulation-programs-at-the-isth-2026-congress.html)
• Hemlibra — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody — Indication: Hemophilia A — Trial Phase: Commercial/Post-marketing — Outcome: Reached CHF 4.8 billion in sales in 2025, up 11% year-on-year (https://www.labiotech.eu/top-pharma-strategy/roche-strategy/)
• Analytical Characterization — Technology: Mass spectrometry — Application: Biotherapeutic characterization including antibodies and ADCs — Context: Central to analytical infrastructure supporting biologics development (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/at-asms-2026-mass-spectrometry-stakes-its-claim-17280)
