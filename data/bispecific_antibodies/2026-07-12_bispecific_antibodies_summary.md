MONTHLY PHARMA INTELLIGENCE BRIEF

1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies in mid-2026 is characterized by significant venture capital investment and diverse clinical progression across oncology, ophthalmology, and autoimmune indications. Key developments include the advancement of VEGF/Ang2 and Tie2/VEGF bispecifics into Phase 3 and Phase 1 trials, alongside a robust pipeline of T-cell engagers targeting solid tumors and autoimmune diseases. Regulatory milestones include a positive CHMP opinion for Johnson & Johnson’s teclistamab combination therapy in multiple myeloma and GMP certification for WuXi Biologics’ bispecific manufacturing facilities. Funding activity remains high, with companies securing substantial capital to support clinical proof-of-concept studies and regulatory filings. The sector demonstrates a shift toward complex modalities, including bispecific antibody-drug conjugates and multispecific T-cell engagers, to address unmet medical needs in resistant cancers and inflammatory conditions. Strategic licensing and subsidiary formations are evident as companies leverage external assets to accelerate pipeline development. Market analysis indicates intensifying competition between antibody-drug conjugates and bispecific antibodies, particularly within the lung cancer therapeutic area.

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• OLN324 (Ollin Biosciences): VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody mechanism; Phase 3 trials for Diabetic Macular Edema and wet Age-Related Macular Degeneration; Patient population includes those with vision-threatening diseases; Comparator treatment is Vabysmo (faricimab); Phase 1b data showed OLN324 topped Vabysmo in efficacy (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet).
• OLN102 (Ollin Biosciences): Dual-targeting IGF-1R and TSHR bispecific antibody mechanism; Phase 1 trial for Thyroid Eye Disease; Patient population includes patients with thyroid eye disease; Comparator treatment is Tepezza (teprotumumab); Designed to offer unique clinical outcomes via dual targeting (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet).
• MMT-205 (Memento Medicines): Activates Tie2 and inhibits VEGF; Indication: Diabetic macular edema and neovascular age-related macular degeneration; Stage: IND-enabling (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).
• TRIV-573 (Triveni Bio): Inhibits KLK5/7 and Th2 cytokine IL-13; Indication: Atopic dermatitis; Stage: Phase 1/Phase 2 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).
• ARC101 (Third Arc Bio): Bispecific T cell engager; Indication: Solid tumors; Stage: Phase 1 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).
• EPI-326 (EpiBiologics): EGFR degrader bispecific antibody; Indication: Non-small cell lung cancer and head and neck squamous cell carcinoma; Stage: Phase 1 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).
• LBL-051 (Oblenio Bio): Trispecific T-cell engager targeting BCMA, CD19 and CD3; Indication: Severe autoimmune diseases; Stage: Clinical testing (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).
• BYN-003 (Bionyra Pharma): TL1AxIL-23p19 bispecific antibody with half-life extension technology; Phase 1 study in healthy volunteers; Patient population is healthy volunteers; Study began in April 2026 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets).
• CTIM-76 (Context Therapeutics): Claudin 6 x CD3 T cell engager bispecific antibody mechanism; Phase 1 clinical trial; Patient population includes patients with platinum-resistant ovarian cancer who progressed on a median of 7 prior lines of therapy; Clinical endpoint was 29% confirmed overall response rate per RECIST v1.1 (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/10/3325745/0/en/context-therapeutics-announces-inducement-grant-under-nasdaq-listing-rule-5635-c-4.html).
• VXA-102 (VERAXA Biotech AG): BiTAC-T cell engager (conditionally active T cell engager) mechanism; Preclinical/IND-enabling stage; Patient population includes various solid tumor indications; Goal is IND/CTA-ready by beginning of 2028 (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/10/3325749/0/en/veraxa-biotech-ag-appoints-carl-von-halem-as-interim-chief-financial-officer-and-successor-to-torsten-b%c3%bcrgermeister.html).
• Teclistamab (Johnson & Johnson): Redirection of T cells to two cellular targets (BCMA and CD3) to activate the immune system against cancer; Phase 3 randomized study (MajesTEC-3) evaluating teclistamab plus daratumumab subcutaneous versus investigator’s choice; 83.4% reduction in risk of disease progression or death (HR, 0.17) and overall survival benefit (HR, 0.46) observed after nearly three years of follow-up (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/06/3322352/0/de/empfehlung-des-chmp-bringt-tecvayli-teclistamab-von-johnson-johnson-in-kombination-mit-daratumumab-als-potenziellen-behandlungsstandard-f%c3%bcr-das-rezidivierte-refrakt%c3%a4re-multiple-mye.html).
• Ivonescimab (Akeso/Summit): Bispecific antibody (combines activity of two best-selling cancer medicines); Phase 3; Patients with squamous non-small cell lung cancer; Reduced risk of death by 34% (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/).
• Glofitamab/Epcoritamab (Genentech/Genmab): Frontline lymphoma trials (SKYGLO and EPCORE DLBCL-2) completed enrollment to evaluate glofitamab and epcoritamab respectively in newly diagnosed high-risk DLBCL (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard).
• WuXi Biologics Program: Bispecific antibody targeting biliary tract cancer; Commercial manufacturing stage; Drug Substance Facility 5, Drug Product Facility 2, and Drug Product Packaging Center certified (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody).
