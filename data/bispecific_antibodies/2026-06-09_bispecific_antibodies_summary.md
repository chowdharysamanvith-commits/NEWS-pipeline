1. Overview
The pharmaceutical sector is witnessing significant investment in PD-1/VEGF bispecific antibody programs, driven largely by the performance of ivonescimab in oncology indications (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/). While initial enthusiasm centers on lung cancer, emerging data suggests hepatocellular carcinoma and gastric cancers may offer superior mechanistic payoff due to VEGF pathway reliance (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/). Concurrently, the Asia-Pacific region is expanding manufacturing capabilities specifically for bispecific antibodies to support this growing pipeline (https://biospectrumasia.com/analysis/38/27704/cdmos-atmp-growth-upswing-in-apac.html). Recent industry developments highlight advancements in the manufacturing and analytical characterization of bispecific antibodies, including enhanced DNA-to-IND timelines (https://drug-dev.com/lonza-advances-integrated-dna-to-ind-programs-through-acceleration-de-risking-and-innovation/). Novel bioengineering approaches, such as transgenic hookworms and avian bioreactors, are advancing as potential cost-effective platforms for therapeutic antibody production (https://the-scientist.com/bioengineered-hookworms-deliver-therapeutic-antibodies-in-hamsters-74580). The sector is witnessing significant licensing activity and strategic partnerships aimed at de-risking large-scale acquisitions and expanding indications beyond initial targets (https://pharmavoice.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/821919/).

• Akeso — Ivonescimab — Lung Cancer — Phase 3 (China-based trial showed 34% risk of death reduction) (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/)
• Akeso — Ivonescimab — Lung Cancer — Phase 3 (Global trial showed 21% reduction in death risk, not statistically significant) (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/)
• Akeso — Ivonescimab — Squamous non-small cell lung cancer — Clinical Trial (Survival Data) (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/)

• BioNTech — BNT327 — Lung, Breast, Colorectal, Liver — Investigational VEGF/PD-L1 bispecific is being tested in multiple tumor types beyond lung cancer to derisk development (https://pharmavoice.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/821919/)
• Janssen/AbbVie — Teclistamab — Multiple Myeloma — Retrospective data showed improved 2-year overall survival for BCMA CAR T-cell therapy compared to teclistamab (https://cancernetwork.com/view/bcma-car-t-cell-therapy-improves-os-vs-teclistamab-in-multiple-myeloma)

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Drug mechanism of action: PD-1/VEGF bispecific (Ivonescimab) and PD-L1/VEGF bispecific (Pumitamig/BNT327) (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/)
• Trial phase: Phase 3 global clinical trials for ivonescimab and pumitamig (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/)
• Patient population: Lung cancer patients, with China-based trials enrolling 93% male patients with a median age of 64 (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/)
• Comparator treatment: Standard chemotherapy plus a PD-1 inhibitor (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/)
• Clinical endpoints or results: China-based trial demonstrated a 34% cut in risk of death; global trial demonstrated a 21% reduction in death risk which was not statistically significant (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/)
• Ivonescimab (PD-1/VEGF bispecific) achieved positive progression-free survival data in a global phase 3 trial, but the 21% reduction in death risk was not statistically significant for overall survival (https://pharmavoice.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/821919/)
• Teclistamab (BCMAxCD3 bispecific) demonstrated higher incidence of cytokine release syndrome (HR 1.48) and immune effector cell–associated neurotoxicity syndrome (HR 2.02) compared to BCMA CAR T-cell therapy in multiple myeloma patients (https://cancernetwork.com/view/bcma-car-t-cell-therapy-improves-os-vs-teclistamab-in-multiple-myeloma)
• BCMA CAR T-cell therapy (idecabtagene vicleucel or ciltacabtagene autoleucel) showed a hazard ratio of 0.61 for all-cause mortality at 2 years compared to teclistamab in a retrospective cohort study (https://cancernetwork.com/view/bcma-car-t-cell-therapy-improves-os-vs-teclistamab-in-multiple-myeloma)
• Transgenic hookworms utilize CRISPR-Cas9 to knock-in antitoxin-coding genes into the Ancylostoma ceylanicum genome, allowing secretion of functional human single chain antibodies into the host bloodstream (https://the-scientist.com/bioengineered-hookworms-deliver-therapeutic-antibodies-in-hamsters-74580)
• Neion Bio engineers chickens to insert medicine-coding genes into the ovalbumin locus, harnessing native machinery to produce monoclonal antibodies in egg white (https://the-scientist.com/bioengineered-hookworms-deliver-therapeutic-antibodies-in-hamsters-74580)
• Lonza — GS Ori-Go vector technology — N/A — Supports lead-clone titers higher than 11 g/L and demonstrates more than a 30% increase in titers across bispecifics (https://drug-dev.com/lonza-advances-integrated-dna-to-ind-programs-through-acceleration-de-risking-and-innovation/)
• Bio-Techne & Refeyn — icIEF fractionation combined with mass photometry — N/A — Enables simultaneous characterization of charge and size variants in approximately four hours (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/combining-icief-and-mass-photometry-to-simplify-antibody-characterization-17227/)
