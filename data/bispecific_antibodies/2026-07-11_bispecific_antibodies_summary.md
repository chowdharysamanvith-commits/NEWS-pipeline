1. Overview
July 2026 pharmaceutical intelligence highlights significant advancements in bispecific antibody development across oncology, immunology, ophthalmology, and CNS indications. Ollin Biosciences secured $330 million to advance OLN324 into Phase 3 trials for retinal diseases (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet). Bionyra Pharma emerged with $165 million and a collaboration with TrueLab Biopharmaceutical for inflammatory disease assets (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets). In oncology, Johnson & Johnson received a CHMP recommendation for teclistamab in combination with daratumumab for multiple myeloma (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/06/3322355/0/fr/la-recommandation-du-cmuh-fait-passer-le-tecvayli-teclistamab-de-johnson-johnson-associ%c3%a9-au-daratumumab-au-rang-de-traitement-conventionnel-potentiel-pour-le-my%c3%a9lome-multiple-r%c3%a9cid.html). Genmab achieved EU approval for epcoritamab in follicular lymphoma and reported Phase 3 results in diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/06/3322680/0/en/genmab-announces-tepkinly-epcoritamab-in-combination-with-lenalidomide-and-rituximab-is-approved-by-the-european-commission-for-the-treatment-of-relapsed-or-refractory-follicular-l.html). Acumen Pharmaceuticals nominated bispecific candidates for Alzheimer’s disease leveraging JCR Pharmaceuticals’ technology (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/07/3323065/0/en/acumen-pharmaceuticals-to-present-data-on-enhanced-brain-delivery-ebd-technology-and-early-alzheimer-s-disease-insights-at-the-alzheimer-s-association-international-conference-aaic.html). Context Therapeutics reported interim efficacy data for CTIM-76 in solid tumors (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/10/3325745/0/en/context-therapeutics-announces-inducement-grant-under-nasdaq-listing-rule-5635-c-4.html). VERAXA Biotech continues to prioritize its BiTAC platform for conditionally active T cell engagers (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/10/3325749/0/en/veraxa-biotech-ag-appoints-carl-von-halem-as-interim-chief-financial-officer-and-successor-to-torsten-b%c3%bcrgermeister.html). Summit and Akeso are noted for winning streaks in oncology bispecific programs (https://pharmavoice.com/news/competition-merck-summit-akeso-cancer-adc-bispecific/822585/).

• Context Trial Population: Patients with platinum-resistant ovarian cancer who progressed on a median of 7 prior lines of therapy (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/10/3325745/0/en/context-therapeutics-announces-inducement-grant-under-nasdaq-listing-rule-5635-c-4.html)
• Context Endpoint: 29% confirmed overall response rate per RECIST v1.1 (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/10/3325745/0/en/context-therapeutics-announces-inducement-grant-under-nasdaq-listing-rule-5635-c-4.html)
• Acumen Pharmaceuticals — Drug Mechanism: Bispecific antibodies targeting soluble amyloid beta oligomers (AβOs) combined with transferrin-receptor-targeting blood-brain barrier-penetrating technology (J-Brain Cargo); Trial Phase: Preclinical/IND-enabling; Patient Population: Alzheimer’s Disease models (murine and non-human primate); Clinical Endpoints: Antibody levels 14-40x higher in NHPs than native antibodies at 24 hours (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/07/3323065/0/en/acumen-pharmaceuticals-to-present-data-on-enhanced-brain-delivery-ebd-technology-and-early-alzheimer-s-disease-insights-at-the-alzheimer-s-association-international-conference-aaic.html)
• Genmab — Drug Mechanism: IgG1-bispecific antibody (DuoBody-CD3xCD20) binding CD3 on T cells and CD20 on B cells; Trial Phase: Phase 3 (EPCORE DLBCL-4); Patient Population: Relapsed or refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL) who received at least one prior line of treatment; Comparator: Rituximab plus gemcitabine plus oxaliplatin (R-GemOx); Clinical Endpoints: Progression-free survival (PFS) risk reduced by 60% (HR 0.40) (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/06/3322680/0/en/genmab-announces-tepkinly-epcoritamab-in-combination-with-lenalidomide-and-rituximab-is-approved-by-the-european-commission-for-the-treatment-of-relapsed-or-refractory-follicular-l.html)
• Johnson & Johnson — Drug Mechanism: Bispecific antibody (BCMA and CD3) directing T cells to kill myeloma cells; Trial Phase: Phase 3 (MajesTEC-3); Patient Population: Relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma (RRMM) previously treated with 1–3 lines; Comparator: Investigator’s choice of daratumumab, dexamethasone, pomalidomide or bortezomib (DPd/DVd); Clinical Endpoints: Progression-free survival risk reduced by 83.4% (HR 0.17); Overall survival rates 83.3% vs 65.0% at three years (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/06/3322355/0/fr/la-recommandation-du-cmuh-fait-passer-le-tecvayli-teclistamab-de-johnson-johnson-associ%c3%a9-au-daratumumab-au-rang-de-traitement-conventionnel-potentiel-pour-le-my%c3%a9lome-multiple-r%c3%a9cid.html)
