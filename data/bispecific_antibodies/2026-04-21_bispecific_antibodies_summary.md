1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies is characterized by significant venture capital inflow and strategic partnerships aimed at enhancing tumor selectivity and expanding therapeutic indications (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26). Market analysis indicates a potential $60 billion opportunity for bispecific antibodies by 2031, supported by insights into more than 550 clinical trials (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/21/3277647/28124/en/bispecific-antibody-market-landscape-report-2026-a-potential-60-opportunity-by-2031-insight-on-more-than-550-clinical-trials-antibody-dosage-patent-price-sales-outlook.html). Recent developments highlight a shift toward bispecific antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) and T-cell engagers designed to mitigate on-target off-tumor toxicity through logic-gated binding mechanisms (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/21/3277652/0/en/promatix-biosciences-highlights-the-power-of-its-logic-gated-proteomics-driven-bispecific-discovery-platform-to-expand-the-tumour-selective-target-space-for-next-generation-adcs-at.html). Companies such as Promatix Biosciences, Sidewinder Therapeutics, and Third Arc Bio are advancing lead candidates into clinical stages, while major pharmaceutical entities like Boehringer Ingelheim are securing preclinical assets through substantial licensing agreements (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/boehringer-amgen-discard-early-immunology-candidates-due-lack-clinical-potential). Regulatory flexibility is being exercised to accelerate development for rare diseases, impacting how bispecifics are evaluated for approval (https://cancernetwork.com/view/fda-receives-sbla-for-zenocutuzumab-in-advanced-nrg1-cholangiocarcinoma). Concurrently, industry analysis indicates that bispecific antibodies offer transformative therapeutic potential through dual-target engagement in autoimmune diseases (https://lifescienceleader.com/doc/radiotherapeutics-for-neuroendocrine-tumors-with-perspective-therapeutics-thijs-spoor-0002).

• Promatix Biosciences — PBS293-MMAE — Colorectal Cancer — Safety Result: Substantially lower cytotoxicity in normal human keratinocytes compared with cetuximab-MMAE (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/21/3277652/0/en/promatix-biosciences-highlights-the-power-of-its-logic-gated-proteomics-driven-bispecific-discovery-platform-to-expand-the-tumour-selective-target-space-for-next-generation-adcs-at.html).
