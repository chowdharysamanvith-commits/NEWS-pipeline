1. Overview
The pharmaceutical intelligence landscape for May 2026 highlights significant strategic activity in bispecific antibody development amidst broader industry trends in obesity therapeutics, digital health, and artificial intelligence integration. UCB announced a major acquisition of Candid Therapeutics to advance a portfolio of bispecifics for autoimmune conditions (https://biopharmadive.com/news/ucb-candid-deal-bispecifics-t-cell-engagers-autoimmune-china/819155/). Concurrently, Gilead Sciences acquired Ouro Medicines for $1.675 billion to secure a BCMAxCD3 T-cell engager pipeline (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ucb-inks-2b-candid-buyout-join-gilead-autoimmune-field). Clinical data from Merck demonstrated a 55% overall response rate for MK-2010 in non-small cell lung cancer at AACR 2026 (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/). Regulatory milestones include Partner Therapeutics receiving an FDA Commissioner’s National Priority Voucher for zenocutuzumab in cholangiocarcinoma (https://cancernetwork.com/view/zenocutuzumab-earns-fda-national-priority-voucher-in-cholangiocarcinoma). Capital markets remained active with Hemab Therapeutics raising over $300 million via IPO for sutacimig (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ma-used-be-only-way-go-why-seaports-ceo-opted-upsized-255m-ipo). Regional developments include China’s 2026 budget prioritizing biomedicine with RMB 426.4 billion in expenditure (https://biospectrumasia.com/analysis/29/27568/china-budget-2026-healthcare-and-innovation-take-center-stage.html). South Korea’s MFDS approved 15 cell and gene therapy products, primarily regenerative stem cell therapies (https://biospectrumasia.com/opinion/29/27571/south-koreas-cell-and-gene-therapy-surge.html). WuXi Biologics secured South Korean GMP certification for commercial bispecific antibody manufacturing (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing). Strategic partnerships include Earendil Labs and Sanofi entering a $2.56 billion deal for autoimmune bispecifics (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/). These activities indicate a maturing market where clinical efficacy data is increasingly paired with specialized manufacturing and discovery partnerships (https://pharmabiz.com/NewsDetails.aspx?aid=185750&sid=2).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• cizutamig — Mechanism of Action: BCMAxCD3 T-cell engager that kills BCMA-expressing B cells, plasmablasts, and plasma cells — Trial phase: Phase 1 (completed), Phase 2 (planned mid-2026) — Patient population: Autoimmune disease patients (68 treated) — Clinical endpoints or results: Deep depletion of B cells and plasma cells; fewer than 15% of first 47 patients had CRS, all mild (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ucb-inks-2b-candid-buyout-join-gilead-autoimmune-field)
• MK-2010 — Mechanism of Action: Bispecific antibody (VEGF inhibitor activity) — Trial phase: Phase 1/2 — Patient population: PD-L1-positive NSCLC (previously untreated and previously treated) — Clinical endpoints or results: 55% overall response rate (ORR) in untreated patients at 20 mg/kg (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/)
• Zenocutuzumab — Mechanism of Action: Inhibits both HER2 and HER3 to block NRG1-mediated signaling driving tumor growth — Trial phase: Phase 1/2 eNRGy trial — Patient population: 19 evaluable patients with advanced NRG1-positive cholangiocarcinoma — Clinical endpoints or results: Overall response rate of 37% and median progression-free survival of 9.2 months (https://cancernetwork.com/view/zenocutuzumab-earns-fda-national-priority-voucher-in-cholangiocarcinoma)
• Tarlatamab — Mechanism of Action: T cell engager — Trial phase: Phase 3 — Patient population: Small cell lung cancer after platinum-based chemotherapy — Clinical endpoints or results: Improved median overall survival from 8.3 months to 13.6 months; combining with anti-PD-L1 increased median overall survival to 25.3 months (https://the-scientist.com/next-generation-precision-medicine-platforms-come-for-cancer-74414)
• Cevostamab — Mechanism of Action: Targets FcRH5 in patients with multiple myeloma refractory to BCMA and GPRC5D therapies — Trial phase: Phase 2 (NCT05801939) — Patient population: Heavily pretreated population previously receiving BCMA-directed therapy — Clinical endpoints or results: Deepening of MRD post-CAR-T after using cevostamab at 1 year, reaching close to 93% MRD negativity (https://cancernetwork.com/view/cevostamab-may-fill-gap-in-multiple-myeloma-armamentarium)
• Inobrodib — Mechanism of Action: Oral medicine blocking p300 and CBP proteins — Trial phase: Phase 2 study and planned Phase 3 trial — Patient population: Multiple myeloma patients with persistent disease — Clinical endpoints or results: Tested in conjunction with bispecific antibodies to potentially extend depth and durability of response (https://biopharmadive.com/news/cellcentric-series-d-multiple-myeloma-inobrodib/819348/)
• EN001 — Mechanism of Action: Allogeneic umbilical cord-derived mesenchymal stem cells — Trial phase: Phase 2a — Patient population: Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease — Clinical endpoints or results: Trial approved by MFDS (https://biospectrumasia.com/opinion/29/27571/south-koreas-cell-and-gene-therapy-surge.html)
