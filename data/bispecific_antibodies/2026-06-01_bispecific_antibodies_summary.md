1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape is witnessing significant advancements in bispecific antibody therapies across oncology and autoimmune indications, driven by clinical data presented at ASCO 2026 and strategic M&A activity. Clinical trials demonstrated survival benefits for ivonescimab in lung cancer and operational strategies for outpatient bispecific administration in multiple myeloma (https://biopharmadive.com/news/akeso-summit-ivonescimab-asco-2026-harmoni6/821559/) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/tocilizumab-supports-outpatient-bispecific-antibody-use-in-multiple-myeloma). Regeneron and NovaBridge are advancing bispecific candidates in amyloidosis and gastric cancer respectively, utilizing platforms like VelociSuite and conditional T-cell activation (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260529/linvoseltamab-data-in-al-amyloidosis-could-have-tremendous-implications) (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/28/3302657/0/en/novabridge-to-present-at-the-2026-jefferies-global-healthcare-conference.html). Manufacturing capabilities are expanding with WuXi Biologics securing South Korean GMP certification for bispecific production (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing). M&A activity in immunology is increasing, exemplified by UCB’s acquisition of Candid Therapeutics to access immune reset bispecific assets (https://pharmavoice.com/news/lilly-gilead-lead-pharmas-deal-merger-drug-acquisition/819512/). These developments indicate a strategic shift toward dual-targeting mechanisms and next-generation multispecific therapeutics (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/).

2. Key Developments
• Akeso / Summit — Ivonescimab — Squamous non-small cell lung cancer — Phase 3 data showed 34% reduction in risk of death compared to standard treatment (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/)
• Regeneron — Linvoseltamab — Relapsed or refractory systemic light chain amyloidosis — Phase 1/2 LINKER-AL2 trial showed 90% hematologic complete response (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260529/linvoseltamab-data-in-al-amyloidosis-could-have-tremendous-implications)
• Janssen/Johnson & Johnson — Teclistamab — Relapsed/Refractory Multiple Myeloma — Phase 3 MajesTEC-9 demonstrated significant PFS and OS benefit vs investigator's choice (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-monotherapy-improves-pfs-and-os-in-relapsed-refractory-myeloma)
• NovaBridge — Givastomig — Claudin 18.2-positive gastric cancer — Global randomized Phase 2 study following positive Phase 1b topline results (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/28/3302657/0/en/novabridge-to-present-at-the-2026-jefferies-global-healthcare-conference.html)
• Genentech — Glofitamab — CD20-positive large B-cell lymphoma — SKYGLO Phase 3 trial evaluating addition to polatuzumab vedotin and R-CHP (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard)
• Genmab/AbbVie — Epcoritamab — Newly diagnosed high-risk DLBCL — EPCORE DLBCL-2 Phase 3 trial assessing addition to R-CHOP (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard)
• UCB — Candid lead asset (bispecific antibody) — Multiple autoimmune diseases — Acquisition of Candid Therapeutics in May 2026 (https://pharmavoice.com/news/lilly-gilead-lead-pharmas-deal-merger-drug-acquisition/819512/)

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Ivonescimab — Drug mechanism: PD-1/VEGF inhibitor — Trial phase: Phase 3 — Patient population: Squamous non-small cell lung cancer — Outcome: Reduced risk of death by 34% compared to standard treatment (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/)
• Linvoseltamab — Drug mechanism: BCMA-directed CD3 T-cell engager — Trial phase: Phase 1/2 LINKER-AL2 — Patient population: Relapsed or refractory systemic light chain amyloidosis — Outcome: 18 of 20 treated patients achieved hematologic complete response (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260529/linvoseltamab-data-in-al-amyloidosis-could-have-tremendous-implications)
• Teclistamab — Drug mechanism: BCMA-directed bispecific antibody — Trial phase: Phase 3 MajesTEC-9 — Patient population: Patients with RRMM who had received 1 to 3 prior lines of therapy — Outcome: Hazard ratio (HR) of 0.29 for PFS and 0.60 for OS (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-monotherapy-improves-pfs-and-os-in-relapsed-refractory-myeloma)
• Talquetamab/Teclistamab — Drug mechanism: Bispecific antibodies with prophylactic tocilizumab — Trial phase: Phase 2 OPTec/OPTal — Patient population: Patients 18 years and older with ECOG performance status of 0 or 1 — Outcome: No instances of ICANS or grade 3/4 CRS occurred across arms A and B (https://cancernetwork.com/view/tocilizumab-supports-outpatient-bispecific-antibody-use-in-multiple-myeloma)
• Givastomig — Drug mechanism: Claudin 18.2 X 4-1BB bispecific antibody — Trial phase: Phase 1b/2 — Patient population: Claudin 18.2-positive gastric cancer — Outcome: Conditional T-cell activation via 4-1BB signaling pathway (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/28/3302657/0/en/novabridge-to-present-at-the-2026-jefferies-global-healthcare-conference.html)
• HXN-1001 — Drug mechanism: Half-life extended anti-TL1A antibody — Trial phase: Phase IIa — Patient population: Ulcerative colitis patients — Outcome: First patient dosed; safety and efficacy to be assessed (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/)
