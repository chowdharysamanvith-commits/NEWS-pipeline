1. Overview
Recent pharmaceutical intelligence indicates significant capital deployment and clinical advancement in the bispecific antibody sector across oncology and non-oncology indications. Ollin Biosciences secured $330 million in Series B funding to advance its VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody, OLN324, into Phase 3 trials for diabetic macular edema and wet age-related macular degeneration (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet). Concurrently, Bionyra Pharma emerged from stealth with $165 million in Series A financing to progress a TL1AxIL-23p19 bispecific antibody, BYN-003, currently in Phase 1 studies for inflammatory bowel disease (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets). In oncology, the Committee for Medicinal Products for Human Use (CHMP) recommended approval for a teclistamab and daratumumab combination in relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma). Genmab reported positive Phase 3 results for epcoritamab in combination with lenalidomide for relapsed/refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/29/3319290/0/en/genmab-announces-positive-phase-3-results-for-epcoritamab-plus-lenalidomide-in-patients-with-relapsed-refractory-diffuse-large-b-cell-lymphoma-demonstrating-statistically-significa.html). Roche continues to leverage Hemlibra and Vabysmo as key growth drivers following biosimilar erosion of its legacy oncology portfolio (https://www.labiotech.eu/top-pharma-strategy/roche-strategy/). These developments highlight a strategic shift toward dual-targeting modalities to address unmet medical needs in retinal diseases, chronic inflammation, and aggressive lymphomas (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
**OLN324 (Ollin Biosciences)**
*   **Drug Mechanism of Action:** VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody.
*   **Trial Phase:** Phase 3 (planned); previously completed Phase 1b.
*   **Patient Population:** Patients with diabetic macular edema and wet age-related macular degeneration.
*   **Comparator Treatment:** Vabysmo (Genentech).
*   **Clinical Endpoints or Results:** OLN324 topped Vabysmo in an earlier Phase 1b trial for diabetic macular edema (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet).

**OLN102 (Ollin Biosciences)**
*   **Drug Mechanism of Action:** Dual targeting of insulin-like growth factor 1 receptor (IGF-1R) and thyroid-stimulating hormone receptor (TSHR).
*   **Trial Phase:** Phase 1 (planned for this year).
*   **Patient Population:** Thyroid eye disease.
*   **Comparator Treatment:** Tepezza (Amgen).
*   **Clinical Endpoints or Results:** Designed to offer unique clinical outcomes via dual targeting of TSH receptor and IGF-1 receptor connection (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet).

**BYN-003 (Bionyra Pharma)**
*   **Drug Mechanism of Action:** TL1AxIL-23p19 bispecific antibody with half-life extension (HLE) technology.
*   **Trial Phase:** Phase 1.
*   **Patient Population:** Healthy volunteers (study began in April 2026).
*   **Comparator Treatment:** Not specified in current Phase 1 healthy volunteer study.
*   **Clinical Endpoints or Results:** Study began in April 2026; goal to advance through Phase 1 by end of year with proof-of-concept data expected in 2028 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets).

**Epcoritamab (Genmab)**
*   **Drug Mechanism of Action:** Utilizes Genmab’s DuoBody® technology.
*   **Trial Phase:** Phase 3 EPCORE® DLBCL-4 trial.
*   **Patient Population:** Patients with relapsed/refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma.
*   **Comparator Treatment:** Combination with lenalidomide reported.
*   **Clinical Endpoints or Results:** Demonstrated statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvement in Progression-Free Survival (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/29/3319290/0/en/genmab-announces-positive-phase-3-results-for-epcoritamab-plus-lenalidomide-in-patients-with-relapsed-refractory-diffuse-large-b-cell-lymphoma-demonstrating-statistically-significa.html).

**Teclistamab (Johnson & Johnson)**
*   **Drug Mechanism of Action:** Redirecting T cells through 2 targets, BCMA and CD3, to activate the immune system against myeloma cells.
*   **Trial Phase:** Phase 3 MajesTEC-3 randomized study.
*   **Patient Population:** Adult patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma who have received 1 to 3 prior lines of therapy.
*   **Comparator Treatment:** Investigator’s choice of standard-of-care regimens (n = 296).
*   **Clinical Endpoints or Results:** Teclistamab plus daratumumab reducing the risk of disease progression or death by 83.4% compared with standard of care (HR, 0.17; 95% CI, 0.12-0.23; P <.0001) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma).

**Glofitamab (Genentech/Roche)**
*   **Drug Mechanism of Action:** Bispecific antibody.
*   **Trial Phase:** SKYGLO (Phase 3).
*   **Patient Population:** CD20-positive large B-cell lymphoma.
*   **Comparator Treatment:** Not specified in summary.
*   **Clinical Endpoints or Results:** Regimen confers meaningful benefit in aggressive lymphoma; Frontline treatment with polatuzumab vedotin and R-CHP (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard).

**Mosunetuzumab (Genentech/Roche)**
*   **Drug Mechanism of Action:** Fixed duration subcutaneous bispecific antibody.
*   **Trial Phase:** Phase 2 MorningSun (NCT05207670) and Phase 3 SWOG S2308.
*   **Patient Population:** Previously untreated high-tumor burden follicular lymphoma (MorningSun); Low burden follicular lymphoma (SWOG S2308).
*   **Comparator Treatment:** Rituximab (SWOG S2308).
*   **Clinical Endpoints or Results:** Evaluating monotherapy against rituximab in patients with low tumor burden disease (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-).

**Surovatamig (AstraZeneca)**
*   **Drug Mechanism of Action:** CD19-directed bispecific antibody.
*   **Trial Phase:** Phase 3 SOUNDTRACK F1 trial.
*   **Patient Population:** Previously untreated follicular lymphoma.
*   **Comparator Treatment:** Chemotherapy (monotherapy comparison).
*   **Clinical Endpoints or Results:** Initial safety run-in data presented at EHA 2026 (https://cancernetwork.com/view/how-are-key-lymphoma-data-from-asco-and-eha-2026-shaping-treatment).

**Givastomig (NovaBridge Biosciences)**
*   **Drug Mechanism of Action:** CLDN18.2 x 4-1BB bispecific antibody that conditionally activates T cells through the 4-1BB signaling pathway in the tumor microenvironment where CLDN18.2 is expressed.
*   **Trial Phase:** Phase 1b/2.
*   **Patient Population:** First-line HER2-negative metastatic gastric cancer.
*   **Comparator Treatment:** Combination with nivolumab and chemotherapy.
*   **Clinical Endpoints or Results:** FDA Fast Track Designation granted (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/29/3318837/0/en/novabridge-biosciences-appoints-dr-srishti-gupta-as-chief-executive-officer.html).

**Biomanufacturing Technology**

*   **Trial Phase:** N/A (Manufacturing Intelligence).
*   **Patient Population:** N/A.
*   **Comparator Treatment:** N/A.
*   **Clinical Endpoints or Results:** Forecasting key outcomes such as titer and harvest readiness for biologics (https://lifescienceleader.com/doc/from-process-monitoring-to-manufacturing-intelligence-real-time-analytics-and-predictive-control-in-biomanufacturing-0001).
