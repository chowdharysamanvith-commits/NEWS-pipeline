1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies demonstrates significant clinical progression, strategic investment, and manufacturing expansion across oncology and autoimmune indications. Compass Therapeutics reported positive phase 2/3 data for Tovecimig in biliary tract cancer, while Merck presented phase 1/2 results for MK-2010 in non-small cell lung cancer. Regulatory activity includes Regeneron Pharmaceuticals securing FDA approval for Otarmeni and advancing bispecific candidates through its VelociSuite platform, alongside a $2.56 billion licensing agreement between Sanofi and Earendil Labs for autoimmune bispecifics. Manufacturing capabilities are strengthening, evidenced by WuXi Biologics obtaining South Korean GMP certification for bispecific antibody production. Despite setbacks in the TIGIT modality with Gilead and Arcus terminating a phase 3 program, investor confidence remains high with substantial funding rounds for Sidewinder Therapeutics, Third Arc Bio, and EpiBiologics. Clinical focus is shifting toward dual-antigen targeting strategies to widen the therapeutic window, as seen with Promatix Biosciences and iOmx Therapeutics. 4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Tovecimig (Compass Therapeutics) — Mechanism of Action: Bispecific antibody blocking Delta-like ligand 4 (DLL4) and vascular endothelial growth factor A (VEGF-A) signaling pathways. — Trial Phase: Phase 2/3 (COMPANION-002). — Patient Population: Patients with unresectable advanced, metastatic or recurrent biliary tract cancer (BTC) treated in the second-line setting. — Comparator Treatment: Paclitaxel alone. — Clinical Endpoints: Median progression-free survival (PFS) of 4.7 months versus 2.6 months (HR=0.44, p<0.0001); Overall Response Rate (ORR) of 17.1% vs 5.3% (p=0.031). (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/24/3281064/0/en/compass-therapeutics-to-announce-topline-secondary-endpoints-from-the-phase-2-3-companion-002-study-of-tovecimig-in-patients-with-biliary-tract-cancer.html)
• MK-2010 (Merck) — Mechanism of Action: Bispecific antibody inducing hypertension and excess protein in urine, side effects not uncommon with VEGF inhibitor drugs. — Trial Phase: Phase 1/2. — Patient Population: Previously untreated patients with PD-L1-positive NSCLC and previously treated patients. — Comparator Treatment: Not explicitly stated in text. — Clinical Endpoints: 55% ORR at 20 mg/kg in untreated patients, 18% ORR at 20 mg/kg in previously treated patients, and 22% ORR at 30 mg/kg in previously treated patients. (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/)
• PBS293-MMAE (Promatix Biosciences) — Mechanism of Action: Cis-bispecific "AND-gate" targeting through hybrid avidity, requiring both EGFR and EphA2 antigens to be present on the same tumor cell for strong binding. — Trial Phase: Preclinical. — Patient Population: Advanced colorectal cancer. — Comparator Treatment: Cetuximab-MMAE in KRAS mutant colorectal cell line HCT116 xenograft studies. — Clinical Endpoints: 50-fold greater potency than cetuximab-MMAE in HCT116 cells; substantially lower cytotoxicity in normal human keratinocytes. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/21/3277652/0/en/promatix-biosciences-highlights-the-power-of-its-logic-gated-proteomics-driven-bispecific-discovery-platform-to-expand-the-tumour-selective-target-space-for-next-generation-adcs-at.html)
• Zanidatamab — Mechanism of Action: Bispecific HER2-targeting monoclonal antibody. — Trial Phase: Phase 3. — Patient Population: HER2-positive, unresectable locally advanced, recurrent, or metastatic GEA. — Comparator Treatment: Trastuzumab plus chemotherapy. — Clinical Endpoints: Median PFS 12.4 months vs 8.1 months; Median OS 26.4 months vs 19.2 months. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/3-things-you-should-know-about-the-evolving-therapeutic-landscape-of-gea)
• Rilvegostomig (AstraZeneca) — Mechanism of Action: TIGIT/PD-1 bispecific antibody using an Fc-silent anti-TIGIT antibody. — Trial Phase: Not explicitly stated in text, described as "still in the running". — Patient Population: Not explicitly stated in text. — Comparator Treatment: Not explicitly stated in text. — Clinical Endpoints: Not explicitly stated in text. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/gilead-loosens-ties-arcus-after-tigit-dream-finally-ends-phase-3-failure)
• Teclistamab — Mechanism of Action: BCMA-directed bispecific antibody. — Trial Phase: Phase 3 (MajesTEC-3). — Patient Population: Early relapse multiple myeloma. — Comparator Treatment: Historical standards. — Clinical Endpoints: Depth of response and progression-free survival appear improved. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/mechanistic-rationale-and-combination-strategies-in-multiple-myeloma)
