1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape demonstrates a strategic pivot toward bispecific antibodies within oncology, driven by clinical advancements in frontline settings and significant licensing activity. (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2026/) Concurrently, enabling technologies for manufacturing and diagnostics are advancing to support these complex modalities. (https://drug-dev.com/lonza-advances-integrated-dna-to-ind-programs-through-acceleration-de-risking-and-innovation/) Multiple randomized phase 3 trials are evaluating bispecific constructs against standard chemotherapy backbones for aggressive lymphomas. (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard) Recent data presented at the European Hematology Association (EHA) 2026 Congress highlights the expanding role of CD20×CD3 bispecific antibodies in large B-cell lymphoma. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/cd20-cd3-bispecific-antibodies-may-be-feasible-bridging-strategy-in-lbcl) Major pharmaceutical companies are securing exclusive rights to bispecific and trispecific assets from biotech innovators to bolster their oncology pipelines. (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2026/) Manufacturing challenges specific to bispecific antibodies, such as aggregation and purification stability, are being addressed through specialized process development strategies. (https://lifescienceleader.com/doc/reduce-mab-aggregates-practical-strategies--0004) These developments highlight the maturation of bispecific technology from relapsed/refractory settings into first-line therapies. (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard)

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Drug mechanism of action: GS Ori-Go vector technology allows direct progression from bulk pools to clonal cell lines, removing the need for enriched pools and methionine sulfoximine (MSX) selection. (https://drug-dev.com/lonza-advances-integrated-dna-to-ind-programs-through-acceleration-de-risking-and-innovation/)
• Drug mechanism of action: RC148 blocks both PD-1 and VEGF simultaneously to help the immune system fight tumors and overcome resistance. (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2026/)
• Drug mechanism of action: HH160 utilizes a “three-in-one” synergistic mechanism targeting PD-1, CTLA-4, and VEGF-A. (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2026/)
• Drug mechanism of action: VIR-5500 combines a bispecific PSMA and CD3-binding T-cell engager with PRO-XTEN masking technology to reduce off-target effects. (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2026/)
• Drug mechanism of action: CD20×CD3 bispecific antibodies function as T-cell engagers to target CD20-positive B-cells and CD3-positive T-cells. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/cd20-cd3-bispecific-antibodies-may-be-feasible-bridging-strategy-in-lbcl)
• Trial phase: Lonza’s DNA-to-IND offering supports preclinical development leading to first-in-human studies, with IND readiness achievable in six months. (https://drug-dev.com/lonza-advances-integrated-dna-to-ind-programs-through-acceleration-de-risking-and-innovation/)
• Trial phase: SKYGLO and EPCORE DLBCL-2 are randomized phase 3 trials evaluating bispecific antibodies for untreated patients in the frontline setting. (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard)
• Trial phase: Phase 3 (EPCORE DLBCL-1) and Retrospective Analysis (EHA 2026). (https://cancernetwork.com/view/epcoritamab-yields-superior-pfs-vs-chemoimmunotherapy-in-pretreated-lbcl)
• Patient population: Vanderbilt’s SSP platform was tested in Phase 1 trials of patients with head and neck squamous cell carcinoma and pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma using panitumumab-IRDye800. (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/new-single-cell-tool-shows-why-antibody-based-drugs-fail-17268)
• Patient population: SKYGLO targets CD20-positive large B-cell lymphoma; EPCORE DLBCL-2 targets newly diagnosed, high-risk DLBCL; VIR-5500 targets advanced, metastatic prostate cancer. (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard)
• Patient population: Relapsed/refractory large B-cell lymphoma (LBCL) patients ineligible for or relapsed after high-dose therapy with autologous stem cell transplant (HDT-ASCT); median age 72.0 years in epcoritamab arm. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/epcoritamab-yields-superior-pfs-vs-chemoimmunotherapy-in-pretreated-lbcl)
• Comparator treatment: In the frontMIND trial context, bispecific trials are positioned against standard R-CHOP or modified R-CHP regimens. (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard)
• Comparator treatment: Investigator's choice of chemoimmunotherapy (CIT), including R-GemOx or bendamustine plus rituximab (BR). (https://cancernetwork.com/view/epcoritamab-yields-superior-pfs-vs-chemoimmunotherapy-in-pretreated-lbcl)
• Clinical endpoints or results: GS Ori-Go supports lead-clone titers higher than 11 g/L and demonstrates more than a 30% increase in titers across modalities including bispecifics. (https://drug-dev.com/lonza-advances-integrated-dna-to-ind-programs-through-acceleration-de-risking-and-innovation/)
• Clinical endpoints or results: SSP results showed significant variability in drug-target engagement across tumor cell types and aligned with the binding-site barrier hypothesis for large molecules. (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/new-single-cell-tool-shows-why-antibody-based-drugs-fail-17268)
• Clinical endpoints or results: Bispecific antibodies have demonstrated activity in the relapsed/refractory setting, with ongoing trials assessing frontline efficacy. (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard)
• Clinical endpoints or results: Median PFS was 3.5 months with epcoritamab vs 3.0 months with CIT; Complete Response (CR) rate was 38% with epcoritamab vs 26% with CIT. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/epcoritamab-yields-superior-pfs-vs-chemoimmunotherapy-in-pretreated-lbcl)
• Clinical endpoints or results: Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS) occurred in 53% of epcoritamab-treated patients. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/epcoritamab-yields-superior-pfs-vs-chemoimmunotherapy-in-pretreated-lbcl)
• Clinical endpoints or results: Immune effector cell-associated neurotoxicity syndrome (ICANS) was rare and predominantly low-grade (9 cases in epcoritamab arm, 2 grade 3–4). (https://cancernetwork.com/view/epcoritamab-yields-superior-pfs-vs-chemoimmunotherapy-in-pretreated-lbcl)
• Clinical endpoints or results: Grade 3 to 4 treatment-emergent adverse events occurred in 76% of the epcoritamab arm vs 79% of CIT arm. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/epcoritamab-yields-superior-pfs-vs-chemoimmunotherapy-in-pretreated-lbcl)
• Clinical endpoints or results: Combination of Glofitamab-gxbm + Gemcitabine and Oxaliplatin (GemOx) conferred deep remissions more rapidly than bispecific monotherapy with an ORR of 75% during bridging. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/cd20-cd3-bispecific-antibodies-may-be-feasible-bridging-strategy-in-lbcl)
• Clinical endpoints or results: Manufacturing strategies for bsAbs focus on mild pH purification to preserve stability and boost yield. (https://lifescienceleader.com/doc/reduce-mab-aggregates-practical-strategies--0004)
• Clinical endpoints or results: Emerging strategies include multispecific formats to better address tumor heterogeneity, though trade-offs include increased hydrophobicity and faster clearance. (https://lifescienceleader.com/doc/unlocking-the-full-potential-of-antibody-drug-conjugates-adcs-0001)

• Option: BeOne was granted an exclusive option for HH160; Huahui Health received a $20 million upfront payment and is eligible for up to $1.9 billion in milestone payments. (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2026/)
• Collaboration: Astellas entered a global strategic collaboration for VIR-5500; Vir Biotechnology received $335 million in upfront and near-term milestone payments and is eligible for up to $1.37 billion in additional milestones. (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2026/)
• Licensing: The strategic global licensing agreement between Pfizer and Innovent Biologics includes multi-specific antibodies with differentiated immune-engaging features, though specific financial terms for these assets were not detailed separately from the broader $10.5 billion deal. (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2026/)
• Manufacturing: Cytiva is offering specialized purification solutions like MabSelect™ Mild Elution Resin to protect sensitive antibodies like bsAbs from harsh low-pH conditions during manufacturing. (https://lifescienceleader.com/doc/reduce-mab-aggregates-practical-strategies--0004)
• Competition: ADCs and bispecific antibodies are dueling modalities gaining traction in lung cancer, creating a competitive landscape for market share. (https://pharmavoice.com/news/competition-merck-summit-akeso-cancer-adc-bispecific/822585/)
• Regulatory: A House bill aims to crack down on China biotech deals, which may impact licensing strategies for foreign companies developing bispecifics and ADCs. (https://pharmavoice.com/news/competition-merck-summit-akeso-cancer-adc-bispecific/822585/)
• Target Selection: Companies are betting big on PD-1/VEGF bispecifics, though evidence suggests a strategic pivot toward liver cancer targets may be more effective than lung cancer targets. (https://pharmavoice.com/news/competition-merck-summit-akeso-cancer-adc-bispecific/822585/)
