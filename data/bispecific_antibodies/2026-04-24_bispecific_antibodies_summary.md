MONTHLY PHARMA INTELLIGENCE BRIEF

1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape in April 2026 is characterized by significant advancements in bispecific antibody modalities, regulatory approvals, and strategic partnerships across oncology and autoimmune indications. Sanofi reported mixed clinical outcomes for its bispecific drug Lunsekimig while securing a positive CHMP opinion for Cenrifki in multiple sclerosis (https://biopharmadive.com/news/ai-pharma-scientist-drug-research-owkin/818280/) (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/22/3279472/0/en/press-release-sanofi-and-regeneron-s-dupixent-approved-in-the-us-as-the-first-biologic-medicine-for-young-children-with-uncontrolled-chronic-spontaneous-urticaria.html). Regeneron Pharmaceuticals expanded its portfolio with U.S. FDA approval for Dupixent in children and Phase 3 success for Cemdisiran in myasthenia gravis (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/22/3279470/0/en/dupixent-dupilumab-approved-in-the-u-s-as-the-first-biologic-medicine-for-young-children-with-uncontrolled-chronic-spontaneous-urticaria-csu.html) (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/21/3278163/0/en/cemdisiran-dosed-subcutaneously-every-12-weeks-demonstrates-rapid-deep-and-sustained-disease-control-in-generalized-myasthenia-gravis-gmg-phase-3-trial.html). Multiple biotechnology firms secured substantial venture capital to advance bispecific programs, including Sidewinder Therapeutics and Third Arc Bio (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26). Merck reported Phase 1/2 results for MK-2010 in non-small cell lung cancer, while Promatix Biosciences highlighted preclinical data for its cis-bispecific ADC PBS293 (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/) (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/21/3277652/0/en/promatix-biosciences-highlights-the-power-of-its-logic-gated-proteomics-driven-bispecific-discovery-platform-to-expand-the-tumour-selective-target-space-for-next-generation-adcs-at.html). Strategic partnerships emerged, notably a ~$2.56B deal between Sanofi and Earendil Labs to advance bispecific antibodies for autoimmune diseases (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/). Expert consensus discussions focused on combining BCMA-directed bispecific antibodies with anti-CD38 therapy in multiple myeloma to improve depth of response (https://cancernetwork.com/view/mechanistic-rationale-and-combination-strategies-in-multiple-myeloma).

2. Key Developments
• Sanofi — Lunsekimig — Asthma, Nasal Polyps, Eczema — Mid-stage tests showed success in asthma and nasal polyps but missed its mark in an eczema trial (https://biopharmadive.com/news/ai-pharma-scientist-drug-research-owkin/818280/).
• Sanofi — Cenrifki (tolebrutinib) — Secondary Progressive Multiple Sclerosis — Positive CHMP opinion recommending EU approval based on HERCULES phase 3 study (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/22/3279472/0/en/press-release-sanofi-and-regeneron-s-dupixent-approved-in-the-us-as-the-first-biologic-medicine-for-young-children-with-uncontrolled-chronic-spontaneous-urticaria.html).
• Regeneron — Dupixent (dupilumab) — Chronic Spontaneous Urticaria — U.S. FDA approval for children aged 2 to 11 years (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/22/3279470/0/en/dupixent-dupilumab-approved-in-the-u-s-as-the-first-biologic-medicine-for-young-children-with-uncontrolled-chronic-spontaneous-urticaria-csu.html).
• Regeneron — Cemdisiran — Generalized Myasthenia Gravis — Phase 3 trial demonstrated rapid, deep, and sustained disease control with subcutaneous dosing every 12 weeks (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/21/3278163/0/en/cemdisiran-dosed-subcutaneously-every-12-weeks-demonstrates-rapid-deep-and-sustained-disease-control-in-generalized-myasthenia-gravis-gmg-phase-3-trial.html).
• Merck — MK-2010 — Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer — Phase 1/2 trial achieved a 55% overall response rate in untreated PD-L1-positive patients (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/).
• Promatix Biosciences — PBS293 — Colorectal Cancer — Preclinical data demonstrated 50x potency over cetuximab-MMAE using a cis-bispecific ADC (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/21/3277652/0/en/promatix-biosciences-highlights-the-power-of-its-logic-gated-proteomics-driven-bispecific-discovery-platform-to-expand-the-tumour-selective-target-space-for-next-generation-adcs-at.html).
• Earendil Labs — HXN-1001 — Ulcerative Colitis — Phase IIa trial dosing initiated for a half-life extended anti-TL1A antibody (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/).
• Sidewinder Therapeutics — Lead bispecific antibody-drug conjugate — Lung, head and neck, gastrointestinal, and colorectal cancers — Planning to take program into the clinic next year (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).
• Third Arc Bio — ARC101 — Solid tumors — Currently in a phase 1 trial as a bispecific T cell engager (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Drug: Lunsekimig — Mechanism: Bispecific drug aimed at two popular targets — Trial Phase: Mid-stage tests — Patient Population: Patients with asthma, nasal polyps, and eczema — Comparator: Not explicitly stated — Clinical Endpoints: Succeeded in studies in asthma and nasal polyps but missed its mark in an eczema trial (https://biopharmadive.com/news/ai-pharma-scientist-drug-research-owkin/818280/).
• Drug: Cenrifki (tolebrutinib) — Mechanism: Oral, brain-penetrant Bruton’s tyrosine kinase inhibitor targeting smoldering neuroinflammation — Trial Phase: Phase 3 (HERCULES) — Patient Population: Non-relapsing secondary progressive multiple sclerosis (SPMS) — Comparator: Not explicitly stated — Clinical Endpoints: Significantly delayed the onset of disability progression (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/22/3279472/0/en/press-release-sanofi-and-regeneron-s-dupixent-approved-in-the-us-as-the-first-biologic-medicine-for-young-children-with-uncontrolled-chronic-spontaneous-urticaria.html).
• Drug: MK-2010 — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody — Trial Phase: Phase 1/2 — Patient Population: Untreated PD-L1-positive NSCLC — Comparator: Not mentioned — Clinical Endpoints: 55% overall response rate; side effects included hypertension and excess protein in the urine (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/).
• Drug: PBS293 — Mechanism: Cis-bispecific “AND-gate” targeting strategy through hybrid avidity requiring both EGFR and EphA2 antigens — Trial Phase: Preclinical — Patient Population: HCT116 colorectal cancer cells — Comparator: Cetuximab-MMAE — Clinical Endpoints: Low-nanomolar potency over 50-fold greater than cetuximab-MMAE (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/21/3277652/0/en/promatix-biosciences-highlights-the-power-of-its-logic-gated-proteomics-driven-bispecific-discovery-platform-to-expand-the-tumour-selective-target-space-for-next-generation-adcs-at.html).
• Drug: HXN-1001 — Mechanism: Half-life extended anti-TL1A antibody — Trial Phase: Phase IIa — Patient Population: Ulcerative colitis patients — Comparator: Placebo (PBO) — Clinical Endpoints: Safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics, preliminary efficacy (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/).
• Drug: BCMA-directed bispecific antibodies — Mechanism: Promote T-cell redirection and activation against myeloma cells — Trial Phase: MajesTEC-3 — Patient Population: Early relapse multiple myeloma — Comparator: Historical standards — Clinical Endpoints: Improved depth of response and progression-free survival (https://cancernetwork.com/view/mechanistic-rationale-and-combination-strategies-in-multiple-myeloma).
• Technology: VelociSuite® — Mechanism: Produces optimized fully human antibodies and new classes of bispecific antibodies — Trial Phase: N/A (Technology Platform) — Patient Population: N/A — Comparator: N/A — Clinical Endpoints: N/A (Platform capability) (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/22/3279470/0/en/dupixent-dupilumab-approved-in-the-u-s-as-the-first-biologic-medicine-for-young-children-with-uncontrolled-chronic-spontaneous-urticaria-csu.html).
