1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape regarding bispecific antibodies is evolving with significant strategic partnerships and clinical advancements reported in June 2026. (https://biopharmadive.com/news/jazz-abcellera-t-cell-engager-multispecific-antibodies-deal/823125/)
Jazz Pharmaceuticals has initiated a collaboration with AbCellera to develop next-generation T-cell-engaging medicines targeting solid tumors. (https://biopharmadive.com/news/jazz-abcellera-t-cell-engager-multispecific-antibodies-deal/823125/)
Johnson & Johnson announced positive Phase 3 data for Talvey, demonstrating superior progression-free survival in earlier-line multiple myeloma. (https://biopharmadive.com/news/jj-multiple-myeloma-drug-talvey-earlier-stage-disease/822904/)
In the Asia-Pacific region, Contract Development and Manufacturing Organisations are increasingly focusing on bispecific antibody production capabilities to meet growing demand. (https://biospectrumasia.com/analysis/38/27704/cdmos-atmp-growth-upswing-in-apac.html)
Genentech and Genmab are evaluating bispecific antibodies in randomized phase 3 trials for untreated high-risk diffuse large B-cell lymphoma. (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard)
Major licensing agreements in 2026 demonstrate industry confidence in bispecific and trispecific modalities for solid tumors. (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2026/)
Lonza has introduced GS Ori-Go vector technology to support early-stage biomanufacturing of bispecifics with increased titers. (https://drug-dev.com/lonza-advances-integrated-dna-to-ind-programs-through-acceleration-de-risking-and-innovation/)
Manufacturing intelligence reports indicate that bispecific antibodies require specialized purification strategies to maintain stability compared to traditional monoclonal antibodies. (https://lifescienceleader.com/doc/reduce-mab-aggregates-practical-strategies--0004)
Industry analysis highlights a significant focus on PD-1/VEGF bispecifics, though evidence suggests the liver may be a more viable target than lung cancer for this class. (https://pharmavoice.com/news/competition-merck-summit-akeso-cancer-adc-bispecific/822585/)
These developments indicate a strategic shift toward integrating development, process technologies, and manufacturing services within the same ecosystem. (https://biospectrumasia.com/analysis/38/27704/cdmos-atmp-growth-upswing-in-apac.html)

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Talvey — Mechanism of action involves binding to the CD3 protein on immune cells and targeting GPRC5D on malignant blood plasma cells (https://biopharmadive.com/news/jj-multiple-myeloma-drug-talvey-earlier-stage-disease/822904/)
• Talvey — Trial phase is Phase 3, presented at the European Hematology Association annual meeting (https://biopharmadive.com/news/jj-multiple-myeloma-drug-talvey-earlier-stage-disease/822904/)
• Talvey — Patient population includes people with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma (https://biopharmadive.com/news/jj-multiple-myeloma-drug-talvey-earlier-stage-disease/822904/)
• Talvey — Comparator treatment was the standard regimen (https://biopharmadive.com/news/jj-multiple-myeloma-drug-talvey-earlier-stage-disease/822904/)
• Talvey — Clinical endpoints included risk of death (reduced by as much as 53%) and risk of disease progression or death (reduced by up to 72%) after two years (https://biopharmadive.com/news/jj-multiple-myeloma-drug-talvey-earlier-stage-disease/822904/)
• Glofitamab/Epcoritamab — Trial Phase: SKYGLO and EPCORE DLBCL-2 are randomized phase 3 trials designed to evaluate bispecific therapies for untreated patients (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard)
• VIR-5500 — Mechanism: Combines a bispecific PSMA and CD3-binding T-cell engager with PRO-XTEN masking technology to keep engagers inactive until reaching the tumor microenvironment (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2026/)
• RC148 — Mechanism: Blocks both PD-1 and VEGF simultaneously to help the immune system fight tumors and overcome resistance mechanisms (https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/oncology-deals-2026/)
• GS Ori-Go technology — Supports lead-clone titers higher than 11 g/L and demonstrates more than a 30% increase in titers for bispecifics (https://drug-dev.com/lonza-advances-integrated-dna-to-ind-programs-through-acceleration-de-risking-and-innovation/)
• Bispecific Antibodies — Manufacturing Consideration: bsAbs are sensitive to harsh low-pH conditions during Protein A purification, requiring mild-elution resins to preserve stability (https://lifescienceleader.com/doc/reduce-mab-aggregates-practical-strategies--0004)
• Bispecific Antibodies — Mechanism: One arm binds to transcytosis receptors such as the transferrin receptor (TfR1) or CD98hc to ferry drugs across the endothelial wall (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/antibody-therapy-points-to-a-new-treatment-era-for-primary-membranous-nephropathy-17262)
• PD-1/VEGF Bispecifics — Patient Population: Lung cancer patients (primary focus), Liver cancer patients (emerging focus) (https://pharmavoice.com/news/competition-merck-summit-akeso-cancer-adc-bispecific/822585/)
