MONTHLY PHARMA INTELLIGENCE BRIEF

1. Overview
The pharmaceutical sector is witnessing significant consolidation and clinical advancement in the bispecific antibody space, highlighted by UCB's $2.2 billion acquisition of Candid Therapeutics to expand its autoimmune pipeline (https://biopharmadive.com/news/ucb-candid-deal-bispecifics-t-cell-engagers-autoimmune-china/819155/). Gilead Sciences similarly acquired Ouro Medicines for $1.675 billion to develop T-cell engager programs for autoimmune disorders (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ucb-inks-2b-candid-buyout-join-gilead-autoimmune-field). Clinical data presented at AACR 2026 demonstrated efficacy for Merck’s MK-2010 in non-small cell lung cancer, showing a 55% overall response rate in untreated patients (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/). Amgen reported improved median overall survival with Tarlatamab in small cell lung cancer, extending survival from 8.3 months to 13.6 months (https://the-scientist.com/next-generation-precision-medicine-platforms-come-for-cancer-74414). BeOne Medicines received FDA priority review for a tislelizumab and zanidatamab combination for HER2-positive gastric cancers (https://cancernetwork.com/view/fda-grants-priority-review-to-tislelizumab-zanidatamab-in-1l-her2-gastric-cancers). Manufacturing capabilities expanded with WuXi Biologics securing South Korean GMP certification for bispecific antibody production (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing). Hemab Therapeutics raised $301.5 million via an IPO to fund late-stage clinical testing of sutacimig for rare blood diseases (https://biopharmadive.com/news/seaport-hemab-ipo-price-biotech-2026/818965/). These developments underscore a strategic shift toward immune-resetting therapies and dual-targeting modalities to address unmet needs in autoimmune and oncology indications (https://biopharmadive.com/news/ucb-candid-deal-bispecifics-t-cell-engagers-autoimmune-china/819155/).

2. Key Developments
• UCB — cizutamig — Autoimmune diseases — Acquisition of Candid Therapeutics adds four bispecifics to portfolio, with two in early-stage human testing (https://biopharmadive.com/news/ucb-candid-deal-bispecifics-t-cell-engagers-autoimmune-china/819155/).
• Gilead Sciences — Ouro BCMAxCD3 TCE — Autoimmune disorders — Acquisition of Ouro Medicines for $1.675 billion upfront to develop T-cell engager programs (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ucb-inks-2b-candid-buyout-join-gilead-autoimmune-field).
• Pfizer — Elrexfio — Multiple Myeloma — Dual-acting antibody met primary objective in Phase 3 trial for patients with disease progression after initial treatment (https://biopharmadive.com/news/fda-real-time-trials-abbvie-kestrel-lilly-profluent/818719/).
• Amgen — Tarlatamab — Small Cell Lung Cancer — Phase 3 study results showed median overall survival improved to 13.6 months (https://the-scientist.com/next-generation-precision-medicine-platforms-come-for-cancer-74414).
• BeOne Medicines — Zanidatamab — HER2-positive gastric cancer — FDA Priority Review granted for combination with tislelizumab (https://cancernetwork.com/view/fda-grants-priority-review-to-tislelizumab-zanidatamab-in-1l-her2-gastric-cancers).
• Hemab Therapeutics — sutacimig — Glanzmann thrombasthenia — Bispecific antibody advancing to Phase 3 study following IPO (https://biopharmadive.com/news/seaport-hemab-ipo-price-biotech-2026/818965/).
• Merck — MK-2010 — Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer — Phase 1/2 trial data showed 55% overall response rate in untreated patients (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/).
• WuXi Biologics — Bispecific Antibody — Biliary Tract Cancer — South Korean GMP certification for commercial manufacturing (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing).
• Bristol Myers Squibb — CD40xFAP bispecific — Solid tumors — Discontinued phase 1 development following portfolio review (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bms-calls-time-zymeworks-collab-ending-work-phase-1-stage-cancer-bispecific).

• BeOne Medicines — Strategic objective involves regulatory filing for first-line HER2+ gastric cancer (https://cancernetwork.com/view/fda-grants-priority-review-to-tislelizumab-zanidatamab-in-1l-her2-gastric-cancers).
• Hemab Therapeutics — Strategic objective is to fund the advancement of drug therapies into late-stage clinical testing following a $301.5 million IPO (https://biopharmadive.com/news/seaport-hemab-ipo-price-biotech-2026/818965/).
• WuXi Biologics — Strategic objective is to expand its integrated CRDMO model to support commercial-scale production of complex biologics (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing).
• Bristol Myers Squibb — Strategic objective involves portfolio prioritization and resource allocation to high-impact programs (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bms-calls-time-zymeworks-collab-ending-work-phase-1-stage-cancer-bispecific).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• cizutamig — Mechanism of action directs the immune system’s T cells to BCMA, a protein on the surface of B cells (https://biopharmadive.com/news/ucb-candid-deal-bispecifics-t-cell-engagers-autoimmune-china/819155/).
• Tarlatamab — Trial phase is Phase 3 in patients with small cell lung cancer (https://the-scientist.com/next-generation-precision-medicine-platforms-come-for-cancer-74414).
• Tarlatamab — Clinical endpoints include median overall survival improved from 8.3 months to 13.6 months (https://the-scientist.com/next-generation-precision-medicine-platforms-come-for-cancer-74414).
• MK-2010 — Mechanism is a bispecific antibody acting as a VEGF inhibitor (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/).
• MK-2010 — Patient population includes PD-L1-positive non-small cell lung cancer (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/).
• Zanidatamab — Mechanism is a HER2-targeted bispecific antibody (https://cancernetwork.com/view/fda-grants-priority-review-to-tislelizumab-zanidatamab-in-1l-her2-gastric-cancers).
• Zanidatamab — Clinical endpoints include Median PFS reached 12.4 months compared with 8.1 months in the control arm (https://cancernetwork.com/view/fda-grants-priority-review-to-tislelizumab-zanidatamab-in-1l-her2-gastric-cancers).
• sutacimig — Mechanism of action is a bispecific antibody being studied in rare clotting disorders (https://biopharmadive.com/news/seaport-hemab-ipo-price-biotech-2026/818965/).
• Elrexfio — Comparator treatment was a combination of Johnson & Johnson’s Darzalex, Bristol Myers Squibb’s Pomalyst and dexamethasone (https://biopharmadive.com/news/fda-real-time-trials-abbvie-kestrel-lilly-profluent/818719/).

• Bristol Myers Squibb — Discontinued the remaining program under its collaboration agreement with Zymeworks, ending a partnership originally signed in 2015 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bms-calls-time-zymeworks-collab-ending-work-phase-1-stage-cancer-bispecific).

• Windward Bio — Secured $165M crossover financing led by OrbiMed to advance WIN027 into respiratory and dermatology studies (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/04/3286605/0/en/windward-bio-announces-165m-crossover-financing-to-advance-pipeline-of-long-acting-immunology-therapies-with-best-in-disease-potential.html).
