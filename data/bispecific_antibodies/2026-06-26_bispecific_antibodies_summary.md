1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies demonstrates significant advancement across ophthalmology, immunology, and oncology sectors during the reporting period. Ollin Biosciences secured substantial funding to advance its VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody, OLN324, into Phase 3 trials for retinal diseases. In the immunology space, Prolium Bioscience initiated a Phase 1/2 study for PRO-203, a CD20xCD3 T-cell engager, in systemic sclerosis and lupus nephritis. China Medical System received approval for Silevimig, the world’s first bispecific antibody for passive immunization against rabies, in China. Oxford BioTherapeutics advanced Obrixtamig to Phase 3 while its partnered program BI 3820768 entered the clinic. Enabling technologies also progressed, with Lonza introducing GS Ori-Go vector technology and Asimov launching tools to support multispecific antibody production. Clinical data presented at the 2026 ASCO Annual Meeting and EHA Congress highlight the evaluation of CD19- and CD20-directed bispecifics in follicular lymphoma. Venture capital funding remains robust for companies developing bispecific therapeutics across oncology and autoimmune indications.
