1. Overview
The pharmaceutical intelligence landscape for bispecific antibodies in 2026 is characterized by significant venture capital investment and clinical progression across oncology, autoimmune, ophthalmology, and neurology indications. Multiple biotechnology firms have secured funding to advance bispecific T-cell engagers and dual-targeting antibodies into clinical trials, with Ollin Biosciences raising $330 million and Bionyra Pharma securing $165 million (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet) (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets). Key regulatory developments include the European Commission granting marketing authorization for Genmab’s epcoritamab in follicular lymphoma and a positive CHMP opinion for Johnson & Johnson’s teclistamab combination in multiple myeloma (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/15/3327556/0/en/genmab-announces-net-sales-of-darzalex-daratumumab-for-second-quarter-of-2026.html) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma). In parallel, Phase 3 trials are evaluating frontline bispecific regimens for lymphoma, challenging established chemotherapy standards with agents like glofitamab and epcoritamab (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard). Strategic partnerships are emerging to expand pipelines into inflammatory diseases and improve manufacturing scalability, such as the collaboration between TrueLab Biopharmaceutical and Bionyra Pharma (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/top-biotech-deals-in-june-2026/). The data indicates a strategic shift towards targeting specific cytokines and immune checkpoints to address unmet medical needs in solid and hematologic tumors (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26). Acumen Pharmaceuticals is also advancing bispecific antibodies for Alzheimer’s disease using Enhanced Brain Delivery technology (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/15/3327603/0/en/acumen-pharmaceuticals-highlights-enhanced-brain-delivery-ebd-program-data-and-early-alzheimer-s-disease-insights-at-the-alzheimer-s-association-international-conference-aaic-2026.html).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• OLN324 — Mechanism of Action: VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody; Trial Phase: Phase 3 planned; Patient Population: Diabetic Macular Edema and Wet Age-Related Macular Degeneration; Outcome: Topped Vabysmo in earlier Phase 1b trial for DME (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet).
• BYN-003 — Mechanism of Action: TL1AxIL-23p19 bispecific antibody with half-life extension technology; Trial Phase: Phase 1; Patient Population: Healthy volunteers (initially); Outcome: Study began in April 2026 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets).
• epcoritamab (TEPKINLY) — Mechanism of Action: IgG1-bispecific antibody binding CD3 on T cells and CD20 on B cells to induce T-cell-mediated killing; Trial Phase: Phase 3 (EPCORE FL-1); Patient Population: Relapsed or Refractory Follicular Lymphoma; Clinical Endpoints: Reduced risk of disease progression or death by 79% (HR 0.21) and 96% Overall Response Rate compared to 81% for R2 alone (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/15/3327556/0/en/genmab-announces-net-sales-of-darzalex-daratumumab-for-second-quarter-of-2026.html).
• Teclistamab — Mechanism of Action: Off-the-shelf subcutaneous bispecific antibody that redirects T cells through BCMA and CD3 targets; Trial Phase: Phase 3 (MajesTEC-3); Patient Population: Relapsed/Refractory Multiple Myeloma; Clinical Endpoints: Reduced risk of disease progression or death by 83.4% compared with standard of care (HR, 0.17) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma).
• glofitamab — Mechanism of Action: Bispecific antibody; Trial Phase: Phase 3 (SKYGLO); Patient Population: CD20-positive large B-cell lymphoma; Outcome: Regimen confers meaningful benefit in aggressive lymphoma (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard).
• Mosunetuzumab — Mechanism of Action: Bispecific antibody; Trial Phase: Phase 3 (SWOG S2308); Patient Population: Low tumor burden follicular lymphoma; Outcome: Monotherapy compared to rituximab (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-).
• Surovatamig — Mechanism of Action: CD19-directed bispecific antibody; Trial Phase: Phase 3 (SOUNDTRACK-F1); Patient Population: Previously untreated follicular lymphoma; Outcome: Initial safety run-in data (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-).
• PF’4404 — Mechanism of Action: Bispecific antibody designed to target VEGF and PD-L1; Trial Phase: Phase 3; Patient Population: Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer; Outcome: Evaluated in combination with sigvotatug vedotin (https://cancernetwork.com/view/sigvotatug-vedotin-does-not-significantly-improve-survival-in-nsclc-trial).
• Acumen EBD Bispecifics — Mechanism of Action: Bispecific antibodies binding Aβ Oligomers and Transferrin Receptors; Trial Phase: Preclinical; Patient Population: Cynomolgus Monkeys; Outcome: Showed enhanced brain delivery compared to native anti-AβO antibodies (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/15/3327603/0/en/acumen-pharmaceuticals-highlights-enhanced-brain-delivery-ebd-program-data-and-early-alzheimer-s-disease-insights-at-the-alzheimer-s-association-international-conference-aaic-2026.html).
• Manufacturing Engineering — Scientific challenge: Structural complexity introduces challenges including chain mispairing, product heterogeneity, and purification difficulties; Scientific solution: Advances in antibody engineering incorporate developability considerations to promote correct heavy- and light-chain assembly (https://lifescienceleader.com/doc/platform-strategy-for-improving-manufacturability-and-efficacy-in-bispecific-antibodies-0001).
