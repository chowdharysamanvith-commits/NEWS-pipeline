1. Overview
Recent pharmaceutical intelligence highlights significant advancements in the development and manufacturing of bispecific antibodies, particularly within oncology and autoimmune indications. Hemab Therapeutics successfully raised $301.5 million through an initial public offering to fund the advancement of its lead bispecific antibody candidate, sutacimig (https://biopharmadive.com/news/seaport-hemab-ipo-price-biotech-2026/818965/). Pfizer reported positive Phase 3 trial results for its dual-acting antibody drug, Elrexfio, which demonstrated a delay in progression or death compared to standard therapy in multiple myeloma patients (https://biopharmadive.com/news/fda-real-time-trials-abbvie-kestrel-lilly-profluent/818719/). Compass Therapeutics faced significant market backlash after its DLL4xVEGF-A bispecific tovecimig failed to meet the overall survival endpoint in a phase 2/3 biliary tract cancer trial (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/compass-therapeutics-stock-heads-south-after-cancer-bispecific-misses-overall-survival). Merck presented phase 1/2 trial results for its bispecific antibody MK-2010 in non-small cell lung cancer, demonstrating substantial response rates in treatment-naive patients (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/). Amgen reported that the bispecific T-cell engager tarlatamab demonstrated improved median overall survival in small cell lung cancer patients (https://the-scientist.com/next-generation-precision-medicine-platforms-come-for-cancer-74414). BeOne Medicines secured priority review for the combination of tislelizumab and the HER2-targeted bispecific antibody zanidatamab in first-line HER2-positive gastric cancers (https://cancernetwork.com/view/fda-grants-priority-review-to-tislelizumab-zanidatamab-in-1l-her2-gastric-cancers). Strategic commercial activity includes a ~$2.56 billion deal between Earendil Labs and Sanofi to advance bispecific antibodies for autoimmune diseases (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/). WuXi Biologics secured South Korean GMP certification for the commercial manufacturing of a bispecific antibody targeting biliary tract cancer (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing). These developments highlight significant capital deployment and clinical validation within the bispecific antibody sector during the reporting period (https://biopharmadive.com/news/seaport-hemab-ipo-price-biotech-2026/818965/).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• sutacimig — Bispecific antibody — Phase 3 (planned) — Patients with Glanzmann thrombasthenia and Factor VII syndrome — Rare clotting disorders (https://biopharmadive.com/news/seaport-hemab-ipo-price-biotech-2026/818965/).
• Elrexfio — Dual-acting antibody — Phase 3 — People whose disease got worse after an initial treatment round — Comparator: Johnson & Johnson’s Darzalex, Bristol Myers Squibb’s Pomalyst and dexamethasone — Delayed progression or death (https://biopharmadive.com/news/fda-real-time-trials-abbvie-kestrel-lilly-profluent/818719/).
• Tecvayli — Bispecific — Approved — Multiple Myeloma — Earlier disease course — Competitor to Elrexfio (https://biopharmadive.com/news/fda-real-time-trials-abbvie-kestrel-lilly-profluent/818719/).
• JANX008 — Masked T-cell engager designed to bind EGFR on cancer cells and CD3 on T cells — Phase 1 — Metastatic solid tumors — Discontinued due to subpar activity (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/janux-axes-solid-tumor-t-cell-engager-after-poor-phase-1-showing).
• JANX007 — Targets prostate-specific membrane antigen (PSMA) and CD3 — Phase 1 — Metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer — Ongoing (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/janux-axes-solid-tumor-t-cell-engager-after-poor-phase-1-showing).
• tovecimig — DLL4xVEGF-A bispecific antibody — Phase 2/3 — Biliary tract cancer — Comparator: Chemotherapy alone — Median PFS 4.7 months vs 2.6 months; Median OS 8.9 months vs 9.4 months (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/compass-therapeutics-stock-heads-south-after-cancer-bispecific-misses-overall-survival).
• MK-2010 — Bispecific antibody — Phase 1/2 — Previously untreated patients with PD-L1-positive non-small cell lung cancer — 55% overall response rate (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/).
• PBS293 — Bispecific ADC targeting EGFR and EphA2 with AND gate activity — Preclinical — Solid Tumors — Bind tumor cells expressing pairs of receptors while minimizing binding to tissues expressing only one receptor (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/).
• IOMX-0675 — Simultaneous blockade of LILRB1 and LILRB2 receptors — Early Development — Solid Tumors — Restores cytotoxic T-cell and NK-cell function by intercepting LILRB1/2-mediated immune suppression (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/).
• Tarlatamab — Bispecific T-cell engager binding CD3 and Delta-like protein 3 (DLL3) — Phase 3 — Small cell lung cancer — Median overall survival increased from 8.3 months to 13.6 months (https://the-scientist.com/next-generation-precision-medicine-platforms-come-for-cancer-74414).
• Tarlatamab Combination — Combination with anti-PD-L1 antibody — Phase 3 clinical trials — Small cell lung cancer — Median overall survival increased to 25.3 months (https://the-scientist.com/next-generation-precision-medicine-platforms-come-for-cancer-74414).
• Xaluritamig — Anti-STEAP1 XmAb 2+1 T-cell redirecting immune therapy relying on avidity — Phase 3 — Castrate-resistant prostate cancer — Target: Six-Transmembrane Epithelial Antigen of the Prostate 1 (STEAP1) (https://the-scientist.com/next-generation-precision-medicine-platforms-come-for-cancer-74414).
• AMG 305 — Dual-targeting T-cell engager requiring binding to two targets on the tumor cell — Phase 1 (completed) — Solid tumors — Completed Phase 1 clinical study (https://the-scientist.com/next-generation-precision-medicine-platforms-come-for-cancer-74414).
• Zanidatamab — HER2-targeted bispecific antibody — Phase 3 (HERIZON-GEA-01) — HER2-positive unresectable locally advanced or metastatic gastroesophageal adenocarcinoma — Comparator: Trastuzumab + chemotherapy (Arm A) — Median PFS 12.4 months vs 8.1 months; Median OS 26.4 months vs 19.2 months (https://cancernetwork.com/view/fda-grants-priority-review-to-tislelizumab-zanidatamab-in-1l-her2-gastric-cancers).
• BCMA-directed Bispecific Antibodies — Phase 3 studies — Early relapse multiple myeloma — Comparator: CAR-T therapy, anti-CD38 therapy — Efficacy signals in anti-CD38 exposed populations (https://cancernetwork.com/view/case-based-sequencing-decisions-in-early-relapse-multiple-myeloma).
• Tocilizumab (IL-6 inhibitor) and Dexamethasone — Clinical Practice Protocols — Patients receiving bispecific antibodies — Comparator: Inpatient care — Reduction in hospital admissions for CRS via outpatient monitoring kits (https://cancernetwork.com/view/managing-bispecific-logistics-remote-monitoring-and-crs-mitigation-strategies).
