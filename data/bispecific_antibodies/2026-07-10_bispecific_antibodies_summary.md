1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies in 2026 is characterized by significant venture capital investment and advancement across multiple therapeutic modalities, including T-cell engagers and antibody-drug conjugates (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26). Funding activity remains robust, with multiple biotechs securing Series A through D rounds to support clinical development of bispecific assets targeting oncology, autoimmune diseases, and metabolic conditions (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26). Specific pipeline progress includes the initiation of Phase 1 studies for novel T-cell engagers and the planning of Phase 3 trials for VEGF/Ang2 bispecifics (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26). Regulatory milestones include the CHMP recommendation for teclistamab in combination with daratumumab for relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma). Genmab announced statistically significant progression-free survival improvements in a Phase 3 trial for epcoritamab in diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/06/3322680/0/en/genmab-announces-tepkinly-epcoritamab-in-combination-with-lenalidomide-and-rituximab-is-approved-by-the-european-commission-for-the-treatment-of-relapsed-or-refractory-follicular-l.html). Manufacturing capabilities are expanding, with WuXi Biologics securing South Korean GMP certification for facilities enabling commercial manufacturing of a bispecific antibody targeting biliary tract cancer (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing). The sector demonstrates a shift toward targeting difficult-to-treat indications such as solid tumors and severe autoimmune diseases through multispecific mechanisms (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• OLN324 — Mechanism: VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody — Trial Phase: Phase 3 planned — Patient Population: Ophthalmology indications (implied by Vabysmo comparison) — Outcome: Early trial success toppling Genentech’s Vabysmo (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• MMT-205 — Mechanism: Activates Tie2 and inhibits VEGF — Trial Phase: IND-enabling studies — Patient Population: Diabetic macular edema and neovascular age-related macular degeneration — Outcome: Potential best-in-class bispecific antibody (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• TRIV-573 — Mechanism: Inhibits KLK5/7 and Th2 cytokine IL-13 — Trial Phase: Phase 1 initiated, Phase 2 planned — Patient Population: Atopic dermatitis — Outcome: First-in-human clinical studies initiated (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• ARC101 — Mechanism: Bispecific T cell engager — Trial Phase: Phase 1 — Patient Population: Solid tumors — Outcome: Ongoing development for oncology and immunology & inflammation conditions (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• EPI-326 — Mechanism: EGFR degrader bispecific antibody — Trial Phase: Clinical trial planned — Patient Population: Non-small cell lung cancer and head and neck squamous cell carcinoma — Outcome: Launch of clinical trial expected early in the year (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• LBL-051 — Mechanism: Targets BCMA, CD19 and CD3 — Trial Phase: Clinical testing planned — Patient Population: Severe autoimmune diseases — Outcome: Designed to reset the immune system (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• PRO-203 — Mechanism: CD20xCD3 T-cell engager — Trial Phase: Clinical candidate being studied — Patient Population: Systemic sclerosis and additional conditions driven by dysfunctional B-cells — Outcome: Planning to launch more trials this year (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• CBX-250 — Mechanism: Targets peptide human leukocyte antigen (pHLA) found on myeloid cancer cells — Trial Phase: Phase 1 — Patient Population: Acute myeloid leukemia, chronic myeloid leukemia, myelodysplastic syndromes and chronic myelomonocytic leukemia — Outcome: Readout due at the end of 2026 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
