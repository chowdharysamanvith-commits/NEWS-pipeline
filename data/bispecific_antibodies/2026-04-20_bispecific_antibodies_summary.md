1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies demonstrates significant capital investment, regulatory advancement, and strategic partnership activity across oncology and immunology sectors during the reporting period. Johnson & Johnson reported that its bispecific antibodies, Tecvayli and Talvey, contributed significantly to oncology revenue in the first quarter of 2026, helping offset losses from the autoimmune drug Stelara. Regulatory milestones include Partner Therapeutics submitting a supplemental biologics license application (sBLA) for zenocutuzumab for advanced cholangiocarcinoma, following its accelerated approval for NRG1 fusion-positive non–small cell lung cancer. Strategic partnerships are emerging, exemplified by major licensing deals for preclinical bispecific assets targeting inflammatory bowel disease and autoimmune diseases. While some early-stage immunology candidates have been discontinued due to lack of clinical potential, the specific bispecific modality remains a priority for pipeline expansion. Key developments include the advancement of EGFR degraders and CD20xCD3 engagers into human studies and the filing of Clinical Trial Applications for novel bispecific candidates. (https://biopharmadive.com/news/johnson-johnson-2026-q1-earnings-stelara-tremfya-myeloma/817422/) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/fda-receives-sbla-for-zenocutuzumab-in-advanced-nrg1-cholangiocarcinoma) (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/) (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/boehringer-amgen-discard-early-immunology-candidates-due-lack-clinical-potential)

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Drug: Zenocutuzumab — Mechanism of Action: Bispecific antibody targeting NRG1 fusions — Trial phase: Phase 1/2 eNRGy trial (NCT02912949) — Patient population: Advanced NRG1+ Cholangiocarcinoma — Clinical endpoints or results: ORR 36.8% (95% CI, 16.3%-61.6%), Median DoR 12.9 months. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/fda-receives-sbla-for-zenocutuzumab-in-advanced-nrg1-cholangiocarcinoma)
• Drug: EpimAb’s FIT-Ig — Mechanism of Action: Bispecific antibodies utilizing basic structural parts of monoclonal antibodies without complex changes, targeting IL-13 and OX-40L simultaneously — Trial phase: First-in-Human Phase 1 expected H1 2026 — Patient population: Atopic Dermatitis. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/15/3274072/0/en/epimab-biotherapeutics-to-present-a-novel-prodrug-t-cell-engager-protce-platform-at-the-2026-american-association-for-cancer-research-annual-meeting.html)
• Drug: REGN5668 — Mechanism of Action: MUC16 × CD28 costimulatory bispecific antibody — Trial phase: Phase 1/2 — Patient population: Recurrent ovarian or endometrial cancer — Clinical endpoints or results: Preliminary clinical activity observed with no significant cytokine release syndrome reported in the dose-expansion portion. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/regn5668-combo-yields-preliminary-activity-in-recurrent-ovarian-endometrial-cancer)
• Drug: BCMA-directed bispecific antibodies — Mechanism of Action: BCMA-directed (T-cell engagement implied by safety profile) — Trial phase: Clinical Practice/Expert Discussion — Patient population: Multiple Myeloma — Clinical endpoints or results: Key toxicities include cytokine release syndrome, infection risk, and cytopenias requiring proactive management. (https://cancernetwork.com/view/safety-considerations-for-bispecific-antibodies-in-multiple-myeloma)
• Drug: ARC101 — Mechanism of Action: Bispecific T cell engager — Trial phase: Phase 1 — Patient population: Solid tumors — Clinical endpoints or results: Ongoing trial with readout expected end of 2026. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• Drug: EPI-326 — Mechanism of Action: Bispecific antibody acting as EGFR degrader — Trial phase: Clinical trial launch (pre-Phase 1) — Patient population: Non-small cell lung cancer and head and neck squamous cell carcinoma — Clinical endpoints or results: Goal to launch clinical trial early 2026. (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• Technology Platform: CRISPR Gene Editing — Mechanism of Action: Reprogram hematopoietic stem cells into antibody-secreting B cells — Trial phase: Preclinical/Research — Patient population: HIV/Malaria/Influenza antigens (in mouse models) — Clinical endpoints or results: Successfully neutralized HIV ex vivo in mouse models; encodes multiple antibodies against different antigens. (https://the-scientist.com/gene-editing-turns-the-immune-system-into-a-custom-protein-factory-74368)
