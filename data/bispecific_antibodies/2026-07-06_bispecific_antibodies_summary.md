MONTHLY PHARMA INTELLIGENCE BRIEF

1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies is characterized by significant commercial growth for established assets and aggressive expansion into frontline oncology settings. Ollin Biosciences secured $330 million in Series B funding to advance its VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody, OLN324, into a multi-trial Phase 3 program for retinal diseases (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet). Concurrently, Bionyra Pharma emerged from stealth with $165 million in Series A financing to develop a pipeline including a TL1AxIL-23p19 bispecific antibody, BYN-003 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets). In oncology, clinical development is shifting towards evaluating bispecific T-cell engagers in first-line therapy for aggressive lymphomas (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard). Johnson & Johnson received a positive CHMP opinion for teclistamab in combination with daratumumab for relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma based on phase 3 data (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma). WuXi Biologics has secured Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) certification from South Korea’s Ministry of Food and Drug Safety for the commercial manufacturing of a bispecific antibody targeting biliary tract cancer (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing). Industry analysis highlights a heavy investment focus on PD-1/VEGF bispecific antibody programs by drugmakers, with emerging evidence suggesting liver disease may offer a superior therapeutic opportunity (https://pharmavoice.com/news/competition-merck-summit-akeso-cancer-adc-bispecific/822585/). These developments underscore a strategic shift toward dual-targeting mechanisms to overcome limitations of monotherapy in complex diseases (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets).

2. Key Developments
• Ollin Biosciences — OLN324 — Diabetic Macular Edema / Wet Age-Related Macular Degeneration — Phase 3 (planned) — OLN324 topped Vabysmo in an earlier Phase 1b trial and is advancing to at least three Phase 3 trials (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet).
• Bionyra Pharma — BYN-003 — Inflammatory Bowel Disease — Phase 1 — BYN-003 is a TL1AxIL-23p19 bispecific antibody that began a Phase 1 study in healthy volunteers in April (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets).
• Roche — Hemlibra — Hemophilia A — Commercial sales reached CHF 4.8 billion, up 11% year-on-year (https://www.labiotech.eu/top-pharma-strategy/roche-strategy/).
• Johnson & Johnson — Teclistamab + Daratumumab — Relapsed/Refractory Multiple Myeloma — CHMP Positive Opinion (Phase 3 MajesTEC-3) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma).
• Genentech — Glofitamab — CD20-positive large B-cell lymphoma — SKYGLO trial evaluating addition to polatuzumab vedotin and R-CHP in frontline setting (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard).
• Genmab/AbbVie — Epcoritamab — Newly diagnosed, high-risk DLBCL — EPCORE DLBCL-2 trial assessing addition to R-CHOP (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard).
• WuXi Biologics — Bispecific Antibody — Biliary Tract Cancer — GMP Certification for Commercial Manufacturing (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing).
• VERAXA Biotech — BiTAC-TCE — Cancer (T-cell engager) — Cell line development initiated with ATUM to advance lead program (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/02/3321216/0/en/veraxa-biotech-initiates-cell-line-development-with-atum-to-advance-its-lead-bitac-tce-program.html).
• Earendil Labs / Sanofi — Bispecific Antibodies — Autoimmune Diseases — ~$2.56B Partnership Deal (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/).
• Mosunetuzumab — CD20/CD3 — Follicular Lymphoma — Phase 3 SWOG S2308 trial comparing mosunetuzumab monotherapy with rituximab in low burden disease (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-).
• Tafasitamab — CD19 — Diffuse Large B-Cell Lymphoma — Phase 3 frontMIND trial established a progression-free survival advantage for tafasitamab plus lenalidomide and R-CHOP in high-risk disease (https://cancernetwork.com/view/how-are-key-lymphoma-data-from-asco-and-eha-2026-shaping-treatment).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• OLN324 — Mechanism: VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody — Trial Phase: Phase 3 (planned) — Patient Population: Diabetic macular edema and wet age-related macular degeneration — Comparator: Vabysmo (farcicimab) — Outcome: Topped Vabysmo in an earlier Phase 1b trial (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet).
• BYN-003 — Mechanism: TL1AxIL-23p19 bispecific antibody with half-life extension (HLE) technology — Trial Phase: Phase 1 — Patient Population: Healthy volunteers (initially) — Outcome: Study began in April with goal of advancing through Phase 1 by end of year (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets).
• Teclistamab — Mechanism: Off-the-shelf subcutaneous bispecific antibody that redirects T cells through two targets, BCMA and CD3, to activate the immune system against myeloma cells — Trial Phase: Phase 3 (MajesTEC-3) — Patient Population: Relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma — Comparator: Standard of care — Outcome: Reduced risk of disease progression or death by 83.4% (HR, 0.17; 95% CI, 0.12-0.23; P <.0001) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma).
• Tafasitamab — Mechanism: CD19 monoclonal antibody — Trial Phase: Phase 3 (frontMIND) — Patient Population: Newly diagnosed high-risk diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (IPI score 3 or more) — Comparator: R-CHOP alone — Outcome: 25% lower risk of progression or death (PFS benefit) without yet demonstrating statistically significant overall survival benefit (https://cancernetwork.com/view/how-are-key-lymphoma-data-from-asco-and-eha-2026-shaping-treatment).
• Surovatamig (AZD0486) — Mechanism: CD19-directed bispecific antibody — Trial Phase: Phase 3 (SOUNDTRACK-F1) — Patient Population: Previously untreated follicular lymphoma — Comparator: Chemotherapy (monotherapy comparison) — Outcome: Initial safety run-in data presented (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-).
• PF'4404 — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody designed to target VEGF and PD-L1 — Trial Phase: Ongoing evaluation — Patient Population: Early-stage lung cancer and other tumors that express IB6 — Outcome: Being evaluated in combination with sigvotatug vedotin (https://cancernetwork.com/view/sigvotatug-vedotin-does-not-significantly-improve-survival-in-nsclc-trial).
• BCMA bispecific antibodies — Mechanism: T-cell redirecting therapy targeting BCMA — Trial Phase: Phase 3 — Patient Population: Relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma, early relapsed, high-risk — Comparator: Conventional treatment approaches (Dara-based triplets) — Outcome: Substantial improvements in progression-free survival (https://cancernetwork.com/view/relapsed-refractory-multiple-myeloma-redefining-treatment-at-first-relapse).
