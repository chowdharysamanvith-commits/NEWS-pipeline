1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies in mid-2026 is characterized by significant venture capital investment and advancement into clinical trials across oncology, hematology, ophthalmology, and autoimmune indications. Funding activity remains robust, with multiple biotechs securing Series A through D rounds to support bispecific programs targeting VEGF, Ang2, EGFR, and cytokine pathways. Regulatory pathways are maturing, with European Commission approvals and CHMP recommendations for key candidates like epcoritamab and teclistamab in lymphoma and multiple myeloma. Analytical capabilities for biotherapeutic characterization are simultaneously maturing, with mass spectrometry platforms enhancing the analysis of complex antibody structures. The broader metabolic pipeline focuses on peptide agonists, while the bispecific antibody sector distinguishes itself through novel mechanisms such as T-cell engagers and degraders. Key players are progressing candidates from IND-enabling studies into Phase 1 and Phase 3 trials, signaling a shift toward late-stage validation. Manufacturing capabilities are expanding, with GMP certifications enabling commercial production for complex modalities. The convergence of advanced manufacturing and targeted funding suggests a competitive environment for next-generation multispecific modalities. 4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• OLN324 — Mechanism: VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody — Phase: Phase 3 (planned) — Population: Diabetic macular edema and wet age-related macular degeneration — Comparator: Vabysmo — Result: Topped Vabysmo in earlier Phase 1b trial for diabetic macular edema (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet)
• Epcoritamab — Mechanism: IgG1-bispecific antibody created using Genmab's DuoBody technology that binds CD3 on T cells and CD20 on B cells — Phase: Phase 3 (EPCORE DLBCL-4) — Population: Adult patients with relapsed or refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma — Comparator: R-GemOx — Result: 60% reduction in the risk of disease progression and death (HR 0.40 [95% CI 0.30, 0.55]) (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard)
• Teclistamab — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody that redirects T cells via BCMA and CD3 targets — Phase: Phase 3 (MajesTEC-3) — Population: Relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma — Comparator: Investigator’s choice of daratumumab plus dexamethasone with pomalidomide or bortezomib — Result: 83.4% reduction in the risk of disease progression or death (HR 0.17; 95% CI 0.12–0.23) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma/)
• Ivonescimab — Mechanism: Combines activity of two cancer medicines (described as "Two drugs in one") — Phase: Phase 3 — Population: Squamous non-small cell lung cancer — Comparator: Standard treatment — Result: Reduced risk of death by 34% (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/)
• BYN-003 — Mechanism: TL1AxIL-23p19 bispecific antibody with half-life extension technology — Phase: Phase 1 — Population: Healthy volunteers — Result: Study began in healthy volunteers (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets)
• ACU301/ACU401 — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody derived from sabirnetug combined with J-Brain Cargo transferrin-receptor-targeting technology — Phase: IND-enabling — Population: Alzheimer’s Disease — Result: Preclinical studies showed antibody levels 14-40x higher in non-human primates than native antibodies at 24 hours (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/07/3323065/0/en/acumen-pharmaceuticals-to-present-data-on-enhanced-brain-delivery-ebd-technology-and-early-alzheimer-s-disease-insights-at-the-alzheimer-s-association-international-conference-aaic.html)
• CTIM-76 — Mechanism: CLDN6 x CD3 T cell engaging bispecific antibody — Phase: Phase 1 — Population: Platinum-resistant ovarian cancer (PROC) — Result: 29% confirmed overall response rate per RECIST v1.1 in 7 efficacy-evaluable PROC patients (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/06/3322747/0/en/context-therapeutics-added-to-russell-2000-and-russell-3000-indexes.html)
• BiTAC-TCE — Mechanism: Splits the T-cell engager into two complementary precursors that require simultaneous engagement of two distinct antigens to activate the CD3-binding domain — Phase: Preclinical — Population: Cancer cells displaying both target molecules — Result: Candidate attacked cancer cells displaying both target molecules while sparing cells expressing only one target (Article 4)
• PF'4404 — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody targeting VEGF and PD-L1 — Phase: Early-stage/Combination — Population: Early-stage lung cancer and other tumors expressing IB6 — Result: Evaluated in combination with sigvotatug vedotin (https://cancernetwork.com/view/sigvotatug-vedotin-does-not-significantly-improve-survival-in-nsclc-trial)
• TRIV-573 — Mechanism: Inhibits KLK5/7 and Th2 cytokine IL-13 — Phase: Phase 1 (initiated), Phase 2 (planned) — Population: Atopic dermatitis — Result: First-in-human clinical studies initiated (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• ARC101 — Mechanism: Bispecific T cell engager — Phase: Phase 1 — Population: Solid tumors — Result: Currently in trial (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• EPI-326 — Mechanism: EGFR degrader bispecific antibody — Phase: Phase 1 (planned) — Population: Non-small cell lung cancer and head and neck squamous cell carcinoma — Result: Clinical trial launch expected early 2026 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• LBL-051 — Mechanism: Trispecific T-cell engager targeting BCMA, CD19 and CD3 — Phase: Clinical testing (planned) — Population: Severe autoimmune diseases — Result: Designed to reset the immune system (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• Hemlibra — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody — Phase: Commercial/Approved — Population: Hemophilia A patients — Outcome: Sales reached CHF 4.8 billion in 2025 (https://www.labiotech.eu/top-pharma-strategy/roche-strategy/)
• Glofitamab — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody — Phase: Phase 3 (SKYGLO) — Population: CD20-positive large B-cell lymphoma — Comparator: Polatuzumab vedotin and R-CHP — Status: Enrollment completed (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard/)
• Vabysmo — Mechanism: Dual-targeting antibody — Phase: Commercial/Approved — Population: Retinal diseases — Outcome: One of Roche's fastest-growing products since early 2022 launch (https://www.labiotech.eu/top-pharma-strategy/roche-strategy/)
• Mosunetuzumab — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody — Phase: 3 (SWOG S2308) — Population: Low tumor burden follicular lymphoma — Comparator: Rituximab (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-)
• Surovatamig — Mechanism: CD19-directed bispecific antibody — Phase: 3 (SOUNDTRACK F1) — Population: Previously untreated follicular lymphoma — Comparator: Chemotherapy (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-)
• Epcoritamab-bysp — Mechanism: CD20 bispecific antibody — Phase: 2 (NCT06045247) — Population: Elderly and frail patients with newly diagnosed DLBCL — Regimen: Plus rituximab, cyclophosphamide, vincristine, and prednisone (R-miniCVP) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/how-are-key-lymphoma-data-from-asco-and-eha-2026-shaping-treatment)
• Biotherapeutic Characterization — Mechanism: Mass spectrometry for proteoform analysis — Phase: N/A (Analytical) — Population: N/A — Results: Platforms enabling identification of more than 10,000 proteins in cell line samples and high-throughput intact biotherapeutic characterization (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/mass-spectrometry-platforms-push-deeper-into-proteomics-and-biopharma-at-asms-2026-17277)
• Genetic Code Expansion (GCE) — Mechanism: Engineering biological systems to incorporate non-canonical amino acids beyond the 20 that occur naturally — Application: Enables antibody drug conjugates and potentially other antibody therapeutics — Technology: Machine learning platforms to accelerate translation of GCE machinery (https://the-scientist.com/genetic-code-expansion-with-machine-learning-can-bridge-the-lab-to-market-gap-74682/)
