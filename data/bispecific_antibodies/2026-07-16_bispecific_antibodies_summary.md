1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies in 2026 is characterized by significant venture capital investment and advancement across diverse therapeutic areas, including oncology, autoimmune diseases, and ophthalmology (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26). Key regulatory milestones include Genmab securing European Commission marketing authorization for epcoritamab in follicular lymphoma (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/15/3327556/0/en/genmab-announces-net-sales-of-darzalex-daratumumab-for-second-quarter-of-2026.html). Clinical data presentations at ASCO 2026 highlighted ivonescimab reducing the risk of death in squamous non-small cell lung cancer by 34% (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/). Manufacturing capabilities are expanding, with WuXi Biologics securing South Korean GMP certification for bispecific antibody production (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing). Strategic partnerships are driving pipeline expansion, such as Biocytogen collaborating with Whitehawk Therapeutics on bispecific antibody-drug conjugates (https://pharmashots.com/34404/biocytogen-collaborates-with-whitehawk-therapeutics-to-develop-bispecific-adc/). The sector also faces competition between antibody-drug conjugates and bispecific antibodies, particularly in lung cancer indications (https://pharmavoice.com/news/competition-merck-summit-akeso-cancer-adc-bispecific/822585/).
