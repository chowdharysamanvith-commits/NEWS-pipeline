MONTHLY PHARMA INTELLIGENCE BRIEF

1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies demonstrated significant clinical progress and strategic activity during the reporting period, highlighted by Merck’s phase 1/2 data for MK-2010 in non-small cell lung cancer (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/). Regeneron Pharmaceuticals reinforced its technology platform capabilities, noting VelociSuite produces new classes of bispecific antibodies (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/29/3283560/0/en/regeneron-reports-first-quarter-2026-financial-and-operating-results.html). Historical data indicates a regulatory approval for a Regeneron bispecific agent targeting myeloma in July 2025, while subsequent reporting notes a rejection for a Regeneron cancer bispecific candidate in August 2025 (https://biopharmadive.com/news/foundayo-prescriptions-psychedelics-regeneron-trump-amgen-novo-bridgebio/818284/). Compass Therapeutics reported mixed clinical data for tovecimig in biliary tract cancer, showing progression-free survival benefits despite an overall survival endpoint miss (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/compass-therapeutics-stock-heads-south-after-cancer-bispecific-misses-overall-survival). Amgen presented data at AACR 2026 on multiple T-cell engagers, including approved tarlatamab and late-stage xaluritamig (https://statnews.com/2026/04/20/aacr-2026-car-t-multiple-myeloma-merck-rural-health-ai/). Emerging biotechs are advancing next-generation bispecific platforms, including Promatix Biosciences’ “AND gate” antibody-drug conjugates (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/). Strategic partnerships include a ~$2.56 billion deal between Earendil Labs and Sanofi to advance bispecific antibodies for autoimmune diseases (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/). Supply chain disruptions in the Middle East pose risks to biologics transport, including antibody-based therapies (https://drugdiscoverynews.com/how-the-iran-war-is-quietly-testing-global-drug-supply-chains-17137). WuXi Biologics secured South Korean GMP certification to enable commercial manufacturing of a bispecific antibody for biliary tract cancer (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing). These developments collectively signal a maturing ecosystem for bispecific therapeutics across oncology and autoimmune indications (https://the-scientist.com/next-generation-precision-medicine-platforms-come-for-cancer-74414).

• Regeneron — Bispecific Antibody — Myeloma — Regulatory Approval (July 2025) (https://biopharmadive.com/news/foundayo-prescriptions-psychedelics-regeneron-trump-amgen-novo-bridgebio/818284/)
• Regeneron — Bispecific Antibody — Cancer — Regulatory Rejection (August 2025) (https://biopharmadive.com/news/foundayo-prescriptions-psychedelics-regeneron-trump-amgen-novo-bridgebio/818284/)
• Janux Therapeutics — JANX008 — Metastatic Solid Tumors — Phase 1 (Discontinued due to subpar efficacy) (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/janux-axes-solid-tumor-t-cell-engager-after-poor-phase-1-showing)
• Compass Therapeutics — Tovecimig — Biliary Tract Cancer — Phase 2/3 (OS miss, PFS improvement) (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/compass-therapeutics-stock-heads-south-after-cancer-bispecific-misses-overall-survival)
• Hemab Therapeutics — Sutacimig — Glanzmann thrombasthenia — Phase 3 planning / Phase 2 positive bleeding reduction (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/seaport-hemab-chart-courses-180m-ipos-fund-depression-clotting-candidates)

• Janssen — Zanidatamab — HER2-positive Gastroesophageal Adenocarcinoma — Phase 3 (Median PFS 12.4 months vs 8.1 months) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/3-things-you-should-know-about-the-evolving-therapeutic-landscape-of-gea)
• WuXi Biologics — Bispecific Antibody — Biliary Tract Cancer — Commercial Manufacturing (GMP Certified) (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing)
• Earendil Labs / Sanofi — Bispecific Antibodies — Autoimmune Diseases — Strategic Partnership (~$2.56B deal) (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/)

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Tovecimig — Mechanism of Action is a DLL4 x VEGF-A bispecific antibody designed to simultaneously block Delta-like ligand 4 and vascular endothelial growth factor A signaling pathways critical to angiogenesis and tumor vascularization (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/24/3281064/0/en/compass-therapeutics-to-announce-topline-secondary-endpoints-from-the-phase-2-3-companion-002-study-of-tovecimig-in-patients-with-biliary-tract-cancer.html)
• COMPANION-002 Study — Clinical Endpoints showed a median progression-free survival of 4.7 months for the tovecimig combination arm versus 2.6 months for the control arm (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/24/3281064/0/en/compass-therapeutics-to-announce-topline-secondary-endpoints-from-the-phase-2-3-companion-002-study-of-tovecimig-in-patients-with-biliary-tract-cancer.html)
• Zanidatamab — Mechanism: Bispecific HER2-targeting monoclonal antibody — Clinical Endpoints: Median PFS 12.4 months (Arm C) vs 8.1 months (Arm A); Median OS 26.4 months (Arm C) vs 19.2 months (Arm A) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/3-things-you-should-know-about-the-evolving-therapeutic-landscape-of-gea)
• Tarlatamab (Amgen) — Mechanism: Bispecific T-cell engager binding CD3 on T cells and DLL3 on tumor cells — Clinical Endpoints: Median overall survival improved from 8.3 months to 13.6 months (https://statnews.com/2026/04/20/aacr-2026-car-t-multiple-myeloma-merck-rural-health-ai/)
• Sutacimig — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody targeting clotting disorders — Clinical Endpoints: Clinically meaningful bleeding reduction (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/seaport-hemab-chart-courses-180m-ipos-fund-depression-clotting-candidates)
• MK-2010 — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody with side effects including hypertension and excess protein in urine, consistent with VEGF inhibitor activity (https://www.labiotech.eu/trends-news/aacr-2026-cancer-therapeutics/)
• JANX008 — Mechanism: Masked T-cell engager binding EGFR on cancer cells and CD3 on T cells — Outcome: Discontinued due to dose expansion efficacy not meeting internal thresholds (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/janux-axes-solid-tumor-t-cell-engager-after-poor-phase-1-showing)
• Xaluritamig (Amgen) — Mechanism: Bispecific T-cell engager with avidity-dependent activity (CD3 binding domain and two STEAP1 binding domains) — Trial Phase: Phase 3 (https://the-scientist.com/next-generation-precision-medicine-platforms-come-for-cancer-74414)
