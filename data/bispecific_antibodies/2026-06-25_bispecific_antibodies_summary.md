1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies demonstrates significant advancement across oncology, immunology, and manufacturing sectors during June 2026. Bionyra emerged from stealth with $165 million in funding to advance next-generation immune drugs, including a bispecific antibody targeting TL1A and IL-23 (https://biopharmadive.com/news/bionyra-immune-drug-startup-sofinnova-jeito/823296/). In the Asia-Pacific region, manufacturing capabilities for bispecific antibodies are expanding, with China identified as a key market focusing on these modalities (https://biospectrumasia.com/analysis/38/27704/cdmos-atmp-growth-upswing-in-apac.html). Ollin Biosciences secured substantial funding to advance its VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody OLN324 into Phase 3 trials for retinal diseases (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet). China Medical System (CMS) achieved regulatory approval for Silevimig for rabies passive immunization, marking a milestone in infectious disease prevention (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/22/3315186/0/en/cms-class-1-innovative-drug-silevimig-injection-approved-in-china-becoming-the-world-s-first-bispecific-antibody-for-passive-immunization-against-rabies.html). Akeso and Summit Therapeutics reported positive Phase 3 data for ivonescimab in squamous non-small cell lung cancer, demonstrating a 34% reduction in the risk of death (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/). WuXi Biologics secured Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) certification from South Korea’s Ministry of Food and Drug Safety for three manufacturing facilities (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing). Earendil Labs and Sanofi announced a strategic deal valued at approximately $2.56 billion to advance bispecific antibodies (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/). The industry continues to invest in startups making bispecific antibodies to more effectively treat conditions like inflammatory bowel disease and psoriasis (https://biopharmadive.com/news/bionyra-immune-drug-startup-sofinnova-jeito/823296/).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
BYN-003 is a bispecific antibody that combines TL1A with a subtype of IL-23 (https://biopharmadive.com/news/bionyra-immune-drug-startup-sofinnova-jeito/823296/). The drug is currently in Phase 1 studies (https://biopharmadive.com/news/bionyra-immune-drug-startup-sofinnova-jeito/823296/). The patient population includes individuals with inflammatory bowel disease and atopic dermatitis (https://biopharmadive.com/news/bionyra-immune-drug-startup-sofinnova-jeito/823296/). OLN324 is a VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody that demonstrated superior results to Vabysmo in an earlier Phase 1b trial for diabetic macular edema (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet). Silevimig functions as a bispecific antibody for passive immunization against rabies, approved as a Class 1 Innovative Drug in China (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/22/3315186/0/en/cms-class-1-innovative-drug-silevimig-injection-approved-in-china-becoming-the-world-s-first-bispecific-antibody-for-passive-immunization-against-rabies.html). Ivonescimab combines the activity of two best-selling cancer medicines and demonstrated a 34% reduced risk of death compared to standard treatment (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/). PRO-203 is a CD20xCD3 T-cell engager that binds CD20+ B-cells and CD3 on T-cells to redirect T-cells to eradicate B-cells via T-cell dependent cellular cytotoxicity (TDCC) (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/22/3315254/0/en/prolium-bioscience-announces-first-patient-dosed-in-its-multinational-phase-1-2-study-of-pro-203-in-systemic-sclerosis-and-last-patient-last-visit-in-an-investigator-led-study-of-p.html). The VERAXA BiTAC therapeutic strategy uses two complementary precursors to enable tumor-restricted activation, delivering a systemically inactive prodrug and a cell-impermeable proactivator through two separate antibodies (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/24/3316702/0/en/veraxa-biotech-expands-r-d-hub-in-heidelberg-as-innovative-bitac-tce-and-adc-programs-advance-towards-clinical-development.html). HXN-1001 is a half-life extended anti-TL1A antibody currently in Phase IIa for Ulcerative Colitis (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/). PF’4404 targets VEGF and PD-L1 and is being evaluated in combination with sigvotatug vedotin (https://cancernetwork.com/view/sigvotatug-vedotin-does-not-significantly-improve-survival-in-nsclc-trial/). Surovatamig (AZD0486) is a CD19-directed bispecific antibody in Phase 3 SOUNDTRACK-F1 trial for previously untreated follicular lymphoma (https://cancernetwork.com/view/how-are-key-lymphoma-data-from-asco-and-eha-2026-shaping-treatment). Epcoritamab-bysp (Epkinly) is a CD20 bispecific antibody in Phase 2 study for elderly and frail patients with newly diagnosed DLBCL (https://cancernetwork.com/view/how-are-key-lymphoma-data-from-asco-and-eha-2026-shaping-treatment). Mosunetuzumab (Lunsumio) is a bispecific antibody in Phase 2 MorningSun trial and Phase 3 SWOG S2308 study for untreated follicular lymphoma (https://cancernetwork.com/view/how-are-key-lymphoma-data-from-asco-and-eha-2026-shaping-treatment).
