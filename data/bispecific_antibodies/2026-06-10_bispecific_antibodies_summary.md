1. Overview
The pharmaceutical industry is witnessing a significant expansion in the development of bispecific antibody drugs, particularly those targeting PD-1 and VEGF pathways, driven by recent clinical data and strategic manufacturing investments (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/). While initial excitement centers on lung cancer indications, evidence suggests potential efficacy in other tumor types such as hepatocellular carcinoma and colorectal cancer (https://pharmavoice.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/821919/). Concurrently, the Asia-Pacific region is strengthening its manufacturing capabilities to support the complex production requirements of these advanced therapies (https://biospectrumasia.com/analysis/38/27704/cdmos-atmp-growth-upswing-in-apac.html). Key players are diversifying their pipelines beyond lung cancer to mitigate clinical risks and capitalize on broader market opportunities (https://pharmavoice.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/821919/). Manufacturing partnerships in China are emerging to facilitate the scale-up of bispecific antibody programs alongside other modalities (https://biospectrumasia.com/analysis/38/27704/cdmos-atmp-growth-upswing-in-apac.html). Significant capital inflow into bispecific antibody modalities is driven by their potential in oncology and autoimmune indications (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26). Recent industry developments highlight significant advancements in the manufacturing, clinical validation, and preclinical tooling of bispecific and multispecific antibody technologies (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing). Strategic partnerships are expanding, with Earendil Labs and Sanofi forging a deal valued at approximately $2.56 billion to advance bispecific antibodies for autoimmune diseases (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/). These activities collectively indicate a maturing ecosystem for multispecific therapeutics across development, manufacturing, and research domains (https://the-scientist.com/bio-x-cell-introduces-first-commercially-available-ready-to-use-anti-mouse-trispecific-antibody-for-preclinical-research-74611).

• Lonza: Strategic objective: Strengthening biologics development services to accelerate DNA-to-IND programs and reduce development risk for early-stage pharma and biotech companies (https://drug-dev.com/lonza-advances-integrated-dna-to-ind-programs-through-acceleration-de-risking-and-innovation/).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Ivonescimab (Akeso/Summit): Mechanism targets PD-1 and VEGF; Phase 3 China-based trial showed a 34% reduction in risk of death compared to chemotherapy plus a PD-1 inhibitor (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/).
• Ivonescimab (Akeso/Summit): Global Phase 3 trial data released in May 2025 showed a 21% reduction in death risk compared to chemotherapy alone, which was not statistically significant (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/).
• Ivonescimab (Akeso/Summit): Patient demographics in the Chinese Phase 3 trial included approximately 93% male patients with a median age of 64 (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/).
• Pumitamig / BNT327 (BioNTech/BMS): Mechanism targets VEGF and PD-L1; currently in Phase 3 testing for lung, breast, colorectal, and liver tumors (https://biopharmadive.com/news/pharma-merck-summit-vegf-pd1-drug-research-cancer/822035/).
• SL-325 (Shattuck Labs): Mechanism involves blockade of the clinically validated DR3/TL1A pathway; described as a DR3 antagonist antibody designed for more complete blockade (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/09/3308777/0/en/shattuck-labs-announces-exercise-of-outstanding-common-stock-warrants-and-provides-financial-update.html).
• CLN-978 (Cullinan Therapeutics): Mechanism is CD19xCD3 T cell engagement; data presented at EULAR 2026 Congress included single target dose escalation in SLE and RA (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/06/3307681/0/en/cullinan-therapeutics-presents-initial-clinical-data-for-cln-978-a-cd19xcd3-t-cell-engager-at-the-eular-2026-congress.html).
• Teclistamab: Mechanism: BCMA-directed bispecific antibody; Comparator: BCMA CAR T-cell therapy; Results: Higher incidence of cytokine release syndrome (CRS) and immune effector cell–associated neurotoxicity syndrome (ICANS) with CAR T vs teclistamab (https://cancernetwork.com/view/bcma-car-t-cell-therapy-improves-os-vs-teclistamab-in-multiple-myeloma).
• GS Ori-Go Vector (Lonza): Mechanism: Next-generation vector platform for GS Expression System; Results: Supports direct progression from bulk pools to clonal cell lines, removing need for MSX selection (https://drug-dev.com/lonza-advances-integrated-dna-to-ind-programs-through-acceleration-de-risking-and-innovation/).
• Anti-mouse PD-L1 × TIGIT × LAG3 Trispecific Antibody (Bio X Cell): Mechanism: Tetravalent trispecific format enabling simultaneous interrogation of three immune checkpoint pathways; Result: Enables study of T-cell exhaustion and immune suppression within a single in vivo system (https://the-scientist.com/bio-x-cell-introduces-first-commercially-available-ready-to-use-anti-mouse-trispecific-antibody-for-preclinical-research-74611).

• Earendil Labs and Sanofi: Entered a licensing agreement to advance bispecific antibodies for autoimmune diseases, valued at approximately $2.56 billion (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/).
