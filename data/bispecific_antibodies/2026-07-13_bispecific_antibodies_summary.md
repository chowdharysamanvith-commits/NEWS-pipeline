1. Overview
The pharmaceutical intelligence landscape for June and July 2026 highlights significant venture capital investment directed toward bispecific antibody programs across oncology, autoimmune, and ophthalmology indications. Multiple biotechnology firms secured funding rounds exceeding $50 million to advance bispecific T-cell engagers, dual-targeting antibodies, and bispecific antibody-drug conjugates into clinical testing. Key developments include the progression of Ollin Biosciences' VEGF/Ang2 bispecific into Phase 3 trials and the initiation of first-in-human studies for several autoimmune and oncology candidates. In oncology, regulatory advancements and Phase 3 trial data are defining the landscape for hematologic malignancies, with the CHMP issuing a positive opinion for Johnson & Johnson's teclistamab combination. The funding activity underscores a strategic shift toward multispecific modalities to address complex disease mechanisms and improve therapeutic efficacy compared to monospecific antibodies. Additionally, the BIO 2026 conference highlighted significant advancements in artificial intelligence applications for antibody discovery, with Genentech integrating AI into all antibody programs to streamline design. 4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• OLN324: Mechanism involves VEGF/Ang2 bispecific targeting; Indication includes dry age-related macular degeneration; Stage is Phase 3; Topped Vabysmo in Phase 1b. (Source: https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet)
• MMT-205: Mechanism activates Tie2 and inhibits VEGF; Indication includes diabetic macular edema and neovascular age-related macular degeneration; Stage is Preclinical/IND-enabling. (Source: https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• TRIV-573: Mechanism inhibits KLK5/7 and Th2 cytokine IL-13; Indication is atopic dermatitis; Stage is Phase 1/2. (Source: https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• ARC101: Mechanism is a bispecific T cell engager; Indication is solid tumors; Stage is Phase 1. (Source: https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• EPI-326: Mechanism is an EGFR degrader bispecific antibody; Indication is non-small cell lung cancer and head and neck squamous cell carcinoma; Stage is Phase 1 planned. (Source: https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• PRO-203: Mechanism is a CD20xCD3 T-cell engager; Indication is severe autoimmune disease (systemic sclerosis); Stage is Phase 1. (Source: https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)
• BYN-003: Mechanism is a TL1AxIL-23p19 bispecific antibody with half-life extension; Indication is IBD; Stage is Phase 1; Healthy volunteers enrolled. (Source: https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets)
• Sutacimig: Mechanism is a bispecific antibody binding endogenous Factor VIIa and TLT-1 on activated platelets; Indication is Glanzmann Thrombasthenia; Stage is Phase 2 LTE; Sustained reduction in annualized treated bleeding rate. (Source: https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/12/3325841/0/en/hemab-therapeutics-presents-new-clinical-data-from-hmb-002-in-von-willebrand-disease-and-introduces-hmb-003-for-heavy-menstrual-bleeding-at-the-isth-2026-congress.html)
• CTIM-76: Mechanism is a Claudin 6 x CD3 T cell engaging bispecific antibody; Indication is Platinum-Resistant Ovarian Cancer; Stage is Phase 1; 29% confirmed overall response rate per RECIST v1.1. (Source: https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/10/3325745/0/en/context-therapeutics-announces-inducement-grant-under-nasdaq-listing-rule-5635-c-4.html)
• BiTAC-TCE: Mechanism involves conditionally active T cell engagers; Indication is Solid Tumors; Stage is Preclinical/IND-Ready; Goal to have VXA-102 IND/CTA-ready by beginning of 2028. (Source: https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/10/3325749/0/en/veraxa-biotech-ag-appoints-carl-von-halem-as-interim-chief-financial-officer-and-successor-to-torsten-b%c3%bcrgermeister.html)
• Acumen’s EBD candidates: Mechanism combines AβO-selective antibody expertise with JCR’s transferrin-receptor-targeting blood-brain barrier-penetrating technology; Indication is Alzheimer’s Disease; Stage is Preclinical; Achieved antibody levels 14-40x higher in NHPs than native antibodies at 24 hours. (Source: https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/07/3323065/0/en/acumen-pharmaceuticals-to-present-data-on-enhanced-brain-delivery-ebd-technology-and-early-alzheimer-s-disease-insights-at-the-alzheimer-s-association-international-conference-aaic.html)
• Teclistamab: Mechanism involves redirecting T cells through two targets, BCMA and CD3, to activate the immune system against myeloma cells; Indication is Relapsed/Refractory Multiple Myeloma; Stage is Phase 3 MajesTEC-3; Combination reduced risk of disease progression or death by 83.4% compared with standard of care (HR, 0.17; 95% CI, 0.12-0.23; P <.0001). (Source: https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma)
• Surovatamig: Mechanism is a CD19-directed bispecific antibody; Indication is Previously Untreated Follicular Lymphoma; Stage is Phase 3 SOUNDTRACK-F1; Initial safety run-in data presented at EHA 2026. (Source: https://cancernetwork.com/view/how-are-key-lymphoma-data-from-asco-and-eha-2026-shaping-treatment)
• PF’4404: Mechanism is a novel bispecific antibody designed to target VEGF and PD-L1; Indication is Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer; Stage is Phase 3 in combination with sigvotatug vedotin. (Source: https://cancernetwork.com/view/sigvotatug-vedotin-does-not-significantly-improve-survival-in-nsclc-trial)
• AI in Discovery: Agentic AI allows for an iterative "lab in the loop" process where autonomous agents form hypotheses, conduct experiments, and analyze results; Focus is on antibody design and discovery processes. (Source: https://lifescienceleader.com/doc/bio-china-ai-and-fda-0001)
