1. Overview
The pharmaceutical news cycle for June 2026 highlights significant advancements in bispecific antibody development across manufacturing, clinical trials, and regulatory approvals. Lonza announced an enhanced preclinical development offering designed to accelerate timelines for biologics, explicitly including bispecifics within its supported modalities (https://drug-dev.com/lonza-advances-integrated-dna-to-ind-programs-through-acceleration-de-risking-and-innovation/). The pharmaceutical landscape demonstrates significant capital flow and clinical advancement focused on bispecific antibody modalities across ophthalmology, oncology, and immunology sectors (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet). Regulatory progress is evident in the European Medicines Agency's Committee for Medicinal Products for Human Use (CHMP) positive opinion for teclistamab combinations in multiple myeloma (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma). China Medical System received approval for Silevimig, the world’s first bispecific antibody for passive immunization against rabies in China (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/22/3315186/0/en/cms-class-1-innovative-drug-silevimig-injection-approved-in-china-becoming-the-world-s-first-bispecific-antibody-for-passive-immunization-against-rabies.html). Clinical data presented at ASCO 2026 demonstrated that ivonescimab reduced the risk of death in squamous non-small cell lung cancer (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/). These developments underscore a strategic shift toward multi-specific modalities to address complex disease mechanisms in hematology, oncology, and autoimmunity (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/26/3318149/0/en/chmp-recommendation-advances-johnson-johnson-s-tecvayli-teclistamab-plus-daratumumab-as-a-potential-standard-of-care-for-relapsed-refractory-multiple-myeloma.html).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• GS Ori-Go (Lonza) — Mechanism of Action: Next-generation vector platform for the GS Expression System designed to improve efficiency and consistency in early-stage biomanufacturing — Trial Phase: Preclinical/IND-enabling stage (DNA-to-IND program) — Clinical Endpoints: Platform demonstrates strong bulk pool stability and more than a 30% increase in titers across multiple modalities (https://drug-dev.com/lonza-advances-integrated-dna-to-ind-programs-through-acceleration-de-risking-and-innovation/)
• OLN324 (Ollin Biosciences) — Mechanism: VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody — Patient Population: Patients with diabetic macular edema and wet age-related macular degeneration — Clinical Endpoints: Topped Vabysmo in earlier Phase 1b trial for DME (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet)
• BYN-003 (Bionyra Pharma) — Mechanism: TL1AxIL-23p19 bispecific antibody with half-life extension technology — Patient Population: Healthy volunteers (Phase 1) and IBD patients — Clinical Endpoints: Began Phase 1 study in healthy volunteers in April 2026 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets)
• Tecvayli + Daratumumab (Johnson & Johnson) — Mechanism: Teclistamab redirects T cells through BCMA and CD3 targets to activate the immune system against myeloma cells — Trial Phase: Phase 3 (MajesTEC-3) — Clinical Endpoints: 83.4% reduction in risk of disease progression or death compared with standard of care (HR, 0.17); 3-year overall survival rate was 83.3% with teclistamab plus daratumumab versus 65.0% with standard of care (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma)
• Ivonescimab (Akeso/Summit) — Mechanism: Combines the activity of two of the best-selling cancer medicines ("Two drugs in one") — Patient Population: Patients with squamous non-small cell lung cancer — Clinical Endpoints: Reduced risk of death by 34% compared to standard treatment (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/)
• PRO-203 (Prolium Bioscience) — Mechanism of Action: Binds CD20+ B-cells and CD3 on T-cells to form an immunological synapse, redirecting T-cells to eradicate B-cells via T-cell dependent cellular cytotoxicity (TDCC) — Patient Population: Systemic sclerosis (SSc) patients and treatment-refractory lupus nephritis (LN) patients — Trial Phase: Multinational, open-label Phase 1/2 study (NCT07641634) (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/22/3315254/0/en/prolium-bioscience-announces-first-patient-dosed-in-its-multinational-phase-1-2-study-of-pro-203-in-systemic-sclerosis-and-last-patient-last-visit-in-an-investigator-led-study-of-p.html)
• BiTAC-TCE/ADC (VERAXA Biotech) — Mechanism of Action: Uses two complementary precursors (systemically inactive prodrug and cell-impermeable proactivator) for tumor-restricted activation — Clinical Endpoints: In vitro studies demonstrated discrimination between breast cancer and healthy cells and efficient killing of 3D tumor cell spheroids (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/24/3316702/0/en/veraxa-biotech-expands-r-d-hub-in-heidelberg-as-innovative-bitac-tce-and-adc-programs-advance-towards-clinical-development.html)
• PF’4404 (Pfizer) — Mechanism of Action: Targets VEGF and PD-L1 as a novel bispecific antibody — Trial Phase: Phase 3 combination study with sigvotatug vedotin — Patient Population: NSCLC and solid tumors (https://cancernetwork.com/view/sigvotatug-vedotin-does-not-significantly-improve-survival-in-nsclc-trial)
• Surovatamig (AstraZeneca) — Mechanism of Action: CD19-directed bispecific antibody activity — Trial Phase: Phase 3 (SOUNDTRACK F1) — Patient Population: Previously untreated follicular lymphoma (https://cancernetwork.com/view/how-are-key-lymphoma-data-from-asco-and-eha-2026-shaping-treatment)
• Epcoritamab-bysp (Genmab/AbbVie) — Mechanism of Action: CD20 bispecific antibody activity combined with R-miniCVP chemotherapy — Trial Phase: Phase 2 study with R-miniCVP in elderly/frail patients — Patient Population: Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/how-are-key-lymphoma-data-from-asco-and-eha-2026-shaping-treatment)
