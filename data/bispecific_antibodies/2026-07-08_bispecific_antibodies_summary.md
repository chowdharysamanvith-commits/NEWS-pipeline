1. Overview
Recent pharmaceutical intelligence highlights significant advancements in bispecific antibody development across hematology oncology indications and solid tumor competitive landscapes. Genmab secured European Commission approval for epcoritamab in combination with lenalidomide and rituximab for relapsed or refractory follicular lymphoma. Concurrently, Genmab reported positive Phase 3 topline results for epcoritamab plus lenalidomide in relapsed/refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma, demonstrating statistically significant progression-free survival benefits. Johnson & Johnson received a positive CHMP recommendation for teclistamab in combination with daratumumab for relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma based on Phase 3 data. VERAXA Biotech initiated cell line development for its lead BiTAC-TCE bispecific program to support IND-enabling activities. In parallel, industry analysis indicates intensified competition between antibody-drug conjugates and bispecific antibodies within the lung cancer therapeutic area. Evidence suggests a strategic shift regarding optimal indications for PD-1/VEGF bispecifics, with the liver potentially presenting a more viable target than lung cancer. Summit and Akeso are highlighted as key players currently maintaining a winning streak in this competitive environment against ADCs. 4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
Epcoritamab is an IgG1-bispecific antibody created using Genmab's DuoBody technology that binds CD3 on T cells and CD20 on B cells to induce T-cell-mediated killing. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/06/3322680/0/en/genmab-announces-tepkinly-epcoritamab-in-combination-with-lenalidomide-and-rituximab-is-approved-by-the-european-commission-for-the-treatment-of-relapsed-or-refractory-follicular-l.html)
The Phase 3 EPCORE DLBCL-4 trial (NCT06508658) evaluated epcoritamab plus lenalidomide versus rituximab plus gemcitabine plus oxaliplatin (R-GemOx) in adult patients with relapsed or refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/06/3322680/0/en/genmab-announces-tepkinly-epcoritamab-in-combination-with-lenalidomide-and-rituximab-is-approved-by-the-european-commission-for-the-treatment-of-relapsed-or-refractory-follicular-l.html)
EPCORE DLBCL-4 demonstrated a 60% reduction in the risk of disease progression and death (HR 0.40 [95% CI 0.30, 0.55]; p value < 0.0001) in the U.S. cohort. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/06/3322680/0/en/genmab-announces-tepkinly-epcoritamab-in-combination-with-lenalidomide-and-rituximab-is-approved-by-the-european-commission-for-the-treatment-of-relapsed-or-refractory-follicular-l.html)
Teclistamab is a subcutaneously administered bispecific antibody that redirects T cells via BCMA and CD3 targets to activate the immune system against cancer. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/06/3322355/0/fr/la-recommandation-du-cmuh-fait-passer-le-tecvayli-teclistamab-de-johnson-johnson-associ%c3%a9-au-daratumumab-au-rang-de-traitement-conventionnel-potentiel-pour-le-my%c3%a9lome-multiple-r%c3%a9cid.html)
The Phase 3 MajesTEC-3 study (NCT05083169) assessed teclistamab plus daratumumab subcutaneously versus investigator's choice of daratumumab plus dexamethasone with pomalidomide or bortezomib in patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/06/3322355/0/fr/la-recommandation-du-cmuh-fait-passer-le-tecvayli-teclistamab-de-johnson-johnson-associ%c3%a9-au-daratumumab-au-rang-de-traitement-conventionnel-potentiel-pour-le-my%c3%a9lome-multiple-r%c3%a9cid.html)
MajesTEC-3 results showed an 83.4% reduction in the risk of disease progression or death (HR 0.17; 95% CI 0.12–0.23; p < 0.0001) for the teclistamab plus daratumumab arm. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/06/3322355/0/fr/la-recommandation-du-cmuh-fait-passer-le-tecvayli-teclistamab-de-johnson-johnson-associ%c3%a9-au-daratumumab-au-rang-de-traitement-conventionnel-potentiel-pour-le-my%c3%a9lome-multiple-r%c3%a9cid.html)
VERAXA's BiTAC-TCE approach splits the T-cell engager into two complementary precursors that require simultaneous engagement of two distinct antigens to activate the CD3-binding domain. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/06/3322298/0/en/veraxa-biotech-provides-business-update-and-further-details-on-partnering-strategy-and-development-timelines.html)
Initial preclinical data for VERAXA's BiTAC-TCE candidate demonstrated a superior safety profile with matching efficacy compared with a more traditional T-cell engager. (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/06/3322298/0/en/veraxa-biotech-provides-business-update-and-further-details-on-partnering-strategy-and-development-timelines.html)
Industry analysis indicates that while both modalities are gaining traction, there is a strategic shift regarding optimal indications for PD-1/VEGF bispecifics. (https://pharmavoice.com/news/competition-merck-summit-akeso-cancer-adc-bispecific/822585/)
Evidence suggests that while development efforts are heavily focused on lung cancer, the liver may present a more viable target for these therapies. (https://pharmavoice.com/news/competition-merck-summit-akeso-cancer-adc-bispecific/822585/)
