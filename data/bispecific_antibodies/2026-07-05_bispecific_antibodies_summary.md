1. Overview
Recent pharmaceutical intelligence indicates significant capital deployment and clinical advancement for bispecific antibody programs across ophthalmology, immunology, and oncology sectors. Ollin Biosciences secured $330 million in Series B funding to advance its VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody, OLN324, into phase 3 trials for retinal diseases (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet). Concurrently, Bionyra Pharma emerged from stealth with $165 million in Series A financing to develop a pipeline including a TL1AxIL-23p19 bispecific antibody, BYN-003, for inflammatory conditions (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets). In oncology, Akeso and Summit Therapeutics reported that ivonescimab reduced the risk of death by 34% in patients with squamous non-small cell lung cancer (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/). The Committee for Medicinal Products for Human Use (CHMP) has recommended approval for a teclistamab combination regimen, signaling a shift toward earlier treatment lines in relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma). WuXi Biologics secured South Korean Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) certification for the commercial manufacturing of a bispecific antibody targeting biliary tract cancer (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing). Earendil Labs announced a strategic partnership with Sanofi to advance bispecific antibodies for autoimmune diseases (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/). These developments underscore the growing commercial and clinical momentum for complex antibody modalities in vision-threatening, inflammatory, and malignant diseases.

3. Companies in Focus
Ollin Biosciences is executing a pipeline expansion strategy focused on ophthalmology, leveraging a VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody to challenge existing market standards (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet). The company aims to advance OLN324 through at least three phase 3 trials following positive phase 1b data that showed it topped Vabysmo in diabetic macular edema (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet). Bionyra Pharma is pursuing a strategic objective to address unmet needs in immunology and inflammation through licensed assets from Chinese biotechs (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets). The company plans to progress three assets through phase 1 by the end of the year, with proof-of-concept data targeted for 2028 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets). WuXi Biologics is expanding its integrated CRDMO model and commercial manufacturing capabilities for bispecific antibodies (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing). Akeso and Summit Therapeutics are focused on the commercialization and development of ivonescimab outside China (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/). Earendil Labs is advancing its bispecific antibody pipeline through a partnership with Sanofi for autoimmune diseases (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/). Johnson & Johnson is establishing the off-the-shelf immunotherapy combination of teclistamab plus daratumumab as a new standard of care earlier in the treatment pathway for multiple myeloma (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma). Pfizer is assessing sigvotatug vedotin across multiple disease stages and evaluating combination with PF’4404 bispecific antibody (https://cancernetwork.com/view/sigvotatug-vedotin-does-not-significantly-improve-survival-in-nsclc-trial). Bristol Myers is involved in biotech deals subject to scrutiny under the proposed COINS Act regarding China biotech transactions (https://pharmavoice.com/news/competition-merck-summit-akeso-cancer-adc-bispecific/822585/).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
OLN324 functions as a VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody designed to target retinal disease mechanisms (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet). In an earlier phase 1b trial, OLN324 demonstrated superior results compared to Vabysmo in the lead indication of diabetic macular edema (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet). OLN102 is designed to hit both IGF-1R and thyroid-stimulating hormone receptor (TSHR) for thyroid eye disease (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet). BYN-003 is a TL1AxIL-23p19 bispecific antibody incorporating half-life extension (HLE) technology (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets). BYN-003 began a Phase 1 study in healthy volunteers in April 2026 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets). BYN-002 is an anti-TL1A monoclonal antibody with HLE technology for patients with IBD (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets). Ivonescimab combines the activity of two of the best-selling cancer medicines and reduced the risk of death by 34% in squamous non-small cell lung cancer (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/). Teclistamab is an off-the-shelf subcutaneous bispecific antibody that redirects T cells through two targets, BCMA and CD3, to activate the immune system against myeloma cells (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma). In the phase 3 MajesTEC-3 trial, teclistamab plus subcutaneous daratumumab reduced the risk of disease progression or death by 83.4% compared with standard of care (HR, 0.17; 95% CI, 0.12-0.23; P <.0001) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma). Overall survival outcomes in MajesTEC-3 favored the combination (HR, 0.46; 95% CI, 0.32-0.65; P <.0001), with a 3-year OS rate of 83.3% with teclistamab plus daratumumab vs 65.0% with standard of care (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma). Surovatamig is a CD19-directed bispecific antibody evaluated in previously untreated follicular lymphoma patients (https://cancernetwork.com/view/how-are-key-lymphoma-data-from-asco-and-eha-2026-shaping-treatment). Epcoritamab-bysp is a CD20 bispecific antibody yielding encouraging early results in elderly and frail patients with diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (https://cancernetwork.com/view/how-are-key-lymphoma-data-from-asco-and-eha-2026-shaping-treatment). PF’4404 is a bispecific antibody designed to target VEGF and PD-L1 (https://cancernetwork.com/view/sigvotatug-vedotin-does-not-significantly-improve-survival-in-nsclc-trial). The phase 3 SWOG S2308 trial compares rituximab with mosunetuzumab among patients with low burden follicular lymphoma (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-). The phase 3 EPCORE FL-2 trial evaluates epcoritamab plus rituximab and lenalidomide (R2) vs chemoimmunotherapy and maintenance in untreated follicular lymphoma (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-). The phase 3 SOUNDTRACK F1 trial assesses surovatamig with rituximab in untreated follicular lymphoma (https://cancernetwork.com/view/where-do-bispecific-antibodies-stand-in-the-follicular-lymphoma-landscape-).

5. Business & Deals
Ollin Biosciences completed a $330 million Series B round spearheaded by TCGX and ARCH Venture Partners (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet). Ollin acquired OLN324 and OLN102 via licensing agreements with Chinese biotechs (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/ollin-hauls-330m-enlist-vabysmo-challenger-phase-3-eye-disease-gauntlet). Bionyra Pharma raised an oversubscribed Series A round of $165 million co-led by Jeito Capital and Sofinnova Partners (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets). Bionyra licensed ex-China rights to BYN-001 from NovaRock Biotherapeutics (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets). Bionyra licensed two TL1A-targeting assets, including BYN-003, from TrueLab Biopharmaceutical (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bionyara-emerges-165m-and-three-licensed-ii-assets). Earendil Labs and Sanofi entered a deal valued at approximately $2.56 billion to advance bispecific antibodies for autoimmune diseases (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/). WuXi Biologics obtained South Korean GMP certification to enable commercial manufacturing of a bispecific antibody, strengthening its integrated CRDMO model (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing). The CHMP recommendation for teclistamab plus daratumumab marks an important step towards establishing the off-the-shelf immunotherapy combination as a new standard of care earlier in the treatment pathway for multiple myeloma (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-combo-earns-positive-chmp-opinion-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma). A House bill aims to crack down on China biotech deals under the COINS Act, following two big deals involving Pfizer and Bristol Myers (https://pharmavoice.com/news/competition-merck-summit-akeso-cancer-adc-bispecific/822585/). The pace of community adoption of bispecific antibodies is increasing in lymphoma and being driven by adoption in solid malignancies (https://cancernetwork.com/view/how-are-key-lymphoma-data-from-asco-and-eha-2026-shaping-treatment). Competition is brewing between antibody-drug conjugates and bispecifics as both modalities gain traction in lung cancer (https://pharmavoice.com/news/competition-merck-summit-akeso-cancer-adc-bispecific/822585/).
