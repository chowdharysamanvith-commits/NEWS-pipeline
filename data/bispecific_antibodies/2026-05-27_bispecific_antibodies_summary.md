1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape is witnessing a strategic evolution from bispecific antibodies to trispecific antibodies, driven by the need to overcome limitations such as antigen escape and tumor heterogeneity (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/). Significant clinical advancements were reported in May 2026, including Regeneron’s positive Phase 1/2 data for Lynozyfic in systemic amyloid light chain amyloidosis and Astellas’ preparation of ASP2138 for Phase 3 in gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/22/3300032/0/en/otarmeni-lunsotogene-parvec-receives-ema-filing-acceptance-for-genetic-hearing-loss.html) (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/facing-looming-xtandi-patent-cliff-astellas-pins-hopes-more-deals-while-still-cutting-costs). Manufacturing capabilities are expanding, evidenced by WuXi Biologics securing South Korean GMP certification for bispecific antibody commercial manufacturing (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing). M&A activity remains robust, highlighted by UCB’s acquisition of Candid Therapeutics to secure a lead bispecific antibody asset for autoimmune indications (https://pharmavoice.com/news/lilly-gilead-lead-pharmas-deal-merger-drug-acquisition/819512/). While some ASCO 2026 abstracts focused on ADCs and monoclonal combinations, the broader industry focus is shifting toward complex biologics and multispecific modalities (https://biopharmadive.com/news/asco-2026-abstracts-merck-biontech-lilly-moderna/820898/).

2. Key Developments
• Regeneron — Lynozyfic (linvoseltamab) — Systemic amyloid light chain amyloidosis — Phase 1/2 — 100% hematologic complete response at 240 mg dose (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/22/3300032/0/en/otarmeni-lunsotogene-parvec-receives-ema-filing-acceptance-for-genetic-hearing-loss.html)
• Astellas — ASP2138 — Gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma — Phase 3 preparation — Readied for study (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/facing-looming-xtandi-patent-cliff-astellas-pins-hopes-more-deals-while-still-cutting-costs)
• Crescent Biopharma — CR-001 — Solid Tumors (NSCLC, gastrointestinal, gynecological) — Phase 1/2 (ASCEND Study) — Trial in Progress presentation at ASCO 2026 (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/21/3299810/0/en/crescent-biopharma-announces-trial-in-progress-presentation-for-ascend-study-of-cr-001-a-pd-1-x-vegf-bispecific-antibody-at-upcoming-american-society-of-clinical-oncology-asco-2026.html)
• Pfizer — tilrekimig — Moderate to severe atopic dermatitis — Phase 3 — Trial met primary endpoint with statistically significant increase in patients seeing eczema reduction >75% at week 16 (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/)
• Sanofi — KT501 — Rheumatoid arthritis (B-cell-mediated autoimmune diseases) — First-in-human study — Evaluating safety and tolerability (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/)
• Johnson & Johnson — JNJ-79635322 — Relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma — Phase 1 — 86.1% overall response rate (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/)
• UCB — Lead bispecific antibody asset — Multiple Autoimmune Diseases — Acquisition — Secured via Candid Therapeutics acquisition (May 2026) (https://pharmavoice.com/news/lilly-gilead-lead-pharmas-deal-merger-drug-acquisition/819512/)
• WuXi Biologics — Bispecific antibody — Biliary tract cancer — South Korean GMP certification — Commercial manufacturing readiness (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing)

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Drug mechanism of action: Lynozyfic is a fully human BCMAxCD3 bispecific antibody designed to bridge BCMA on myeloma cells with CD3-expressing T cells to facilitate T-cell activation and cancer-cell killing (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/22/3300032/0/en/otarmeni-lunsotogene-parvec-receives-ema-filing-acceptance-for-genetic-hearing-loss.html)
• Drug mechanism of action: ASP2138 functions as an LDN18.2xCD3 bispecific T-cell engager (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/facing-looming-xtandi-patent-cliff-astellas-pins-hopes-more-deals-while-still-cutting-costs)
• Drug mechanism of action: Trispecific antibodies bind to three different targets simultaneously, such as two tumor markers (e.g., BCMA, GPRC5D, CD38) and CD3 on T cells (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/)
• Clinical endpoints or results: In the higher-dose group (240 mg) for Lynozyfic, 100% achieved a hematologic complete response (13 of 13) (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/22/3300032/0/en/otarmeni-lunsotogene-parvec-receives-ema-filing-acceptance-for-genetic-hearing-loss.html)
• Clinical endpoints or results: JNJ-79635322 showed 100% overall response rate in patients naive to BCMA and GPRC5D-directed therapies (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/)
• Clinical endpoints or results: Participant 2 in the CARv3-TEAM-E glioblastoma trial demonstrated a 60.7% reduction in cross-sectional tumor area by day 69 (https://cancernetwork.com/view/mechanistic-and-translational-insights-of-carv3-team-e-in-glioblastoma)
• Clinical endpoints or results: Pfizer tilrekimig Phase 2 trial met primary endpoint with statistically significant increase in patients seeing eczema reduction >75% at week 16 (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/)
• Comparator treatment: JNJ-79635322 toxicity profile compared to bispecifics (Tecvayli, Elrexfio, Lynozyfic) (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/)

5. Business & Deals
• UCB acquired Candid Therapeutics in May 2026 to secure a bispecific antibody asset focused on immune reset (https://pharmavoice.com/news/lilly-gilead-lead-pharmas-deal-merger-drug-acquisition/819512/)
• Earendil Labs and Sanofi entered a deal valued at approximately $2.56 billion to advance bispecific antibodies for autoimmune diseases (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/)
• Sanofi agreed to pay $180 million upfront to Kali Therapeutics to license trispecific T-cell engager KT501 (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/)
• AbbVie paid Ichnos Glenmark Innovation (IGI) $700 million upfront for rights to CD38×BCMA×CD3 trispecific antibody ISB 2001 (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/)
• Excalipoint Therapeutics licensed assets EXP011 and EXP012 for $10 million upfront from Lepu Biopharma (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/)
• WuXi Biologics strengthened its position in the Asia-Pacific region by obtaining South Korean GMP certification, enabling end-to-end commercial manufacturing for complex biologics (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing)
• Total upfront payments for immune drugs in 2026 reached ~$12.7 billion, reflecting increased investment in immunology and inflammation (https://pharmavoice.com/news/lilly-gilead-lead-pharmas-deal-merger-drug-acquisition/819512/)
