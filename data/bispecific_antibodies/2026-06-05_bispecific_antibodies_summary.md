1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies is defined by significant clinical advancements and strategic consolidation during the reporting period. At ASCO 2026, Akeso and Summit Therapeutics presented Phase 3 data for the bispecific antibody ivonescimab in non-small cell lung cancer, demonstrating a survival benefit compared to chemotherapy (https://biopharmadive.com/news/asco-2026-daraxonrasib-ivonescimab-proteus-erleada-results/821622/). Concurrently, the sector saw M&A activity as RallyBio merged with Avenzo Therapeutics to advance a pipeline including bispecific ADCs, while Candid Therapeutics was acquired by UCB for its bispecific autoimmune portfolio (https://biopharmadive.com/news/avenzo-rallybio-reverse-merger-cancer-drugs/821613/). In the Asia-Pacific region, WuXi Biologics expanded its manufacturing infrastructure to support bispecific antibody programs, reflecting a strategic shift toward high-value biologics segments (https://biospectrumasia.com/analysis/38/27704/cdmos-atmp-growth-upswing-in-apac.html). Regulatory scrutiny on cross-border biotech deals involving Chinese firms may impact future licensing strategies for companies sourcing assets from that region (https://biopharmadive.com/news/binsa-china-biotech-deals-house-moolenaar-dingell/821841/). Clinical data also highlighted the emergence of trispecific antibodies as the next evolution following the success of bispecifics (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/). Strategic partnerships are becoming a necessity to manage the economic burden and complexity of developing these therapies (https://biospectrumasia.com/analysis/38/27704/cdmos-atmp-growth-upswing-in-apac.html).

2. Key Developments
• Akeso / Summit Therapeutics — Ivonescimab — Non-small cell lung cancer — Phase 3 — Reduced risk of death by one-third compared to chemotherapy in a China-based study (https://biopharmadive.com/news/akeso-summit-ivonescimab-asco-2026-harmoni6/821559/)
• Takeda / Innovent Biologics — IBI363 — Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer — Phase 1 — Median progression-free survival of 10.1 months and overall survival of 18.2 months in squamous NSCLC (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/asco-long-term-early-data-backs-tremendous-potential-takedas-innovent-oncology-collab)
• BioNTech / Bristol Myers Squibb — Pumitamig — First-line Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer — Phase 2/3 — Interim analysis showed encouraging anti-tumor activity in the ROSETTA Lung-02 trial (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/30/3303897/0/en/global-data-for-biontech-and-bristol-myers-squibb-s-pd-l1xvegf-a-bispecific-pumitamig-shows-encouraging-efficacy-in-patients-with-non-small-cell-lung-cancer-in-rosetta-lung-02-tria.html)
• Immatics — IMA401 — Melanoma/Lung Cancer — Phase 1 — Data presented at ASCO 2026 showing deep and durable responses (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/31/3303923/0/en/immatics-presents-data-on-ima401-magea4-8-bispecific-at-2026-asco-annual-meeting-with-simultaneous-publication-in-nature-medicine-supporting-development-of-ima401-ima402-combinatio.html)
• Genentech — Glofitamab — Diffuse Large B-Cell Lymphoma — Phase 3 — SKYGLO trial evaluating addition to polatuzumab vedotin and R-CHP as frontline treatment (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260530/regimen-confers-meaningful-benefit-in-aggressive-lymphoma-may-become-a-new-standard)
• Janssen — Teclistamab — Relapsed/Refractory Multiple Myeloma — Phase 3 — MajesTEC-9 trial showed significant PFS and OS improvement vs investigator's choice (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-monotherapy-improves-pfs-and-os-in-relapsed-refractory-myeloma)
• Crescent Biopharma — CR-001 — Solid Tumors — Phase 1/2 — PD-1 x VEGF bispecific antibody evaluated in the ASCEND global clinical trial (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/02/3305600/0/en/crescent-biopharma-to-present-at-june-investor-conferences.html)
• WuXi Biologics — Bispecific Antibodies — Manufacturing — GMP Certification — Received South Korea GMP certification for three manufacturing facilities enabling commercial manufacturing (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing)

3. Companies in Focus
• Akeso and Summit Therapeutics: Collaborating on the development of the bispecific antibody ivonescimab, which is being evaluated as a front-line treatment option in lung cancer (https://biopharmadive.com/news/asco-2026-daraxonrasib-ivonescimab-proteus-erleada-results/821622/).
• Takeda and Innovent Biologics: Engaged in co-global development and commercialization of IBI363 following a multi-billion dollar licensing agreement (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/asco-long-term-early-data-backs-tremendous-potential-takedas-innovent-oncology-collab).
• BioNTech and Bristol Myers Squibb: Strategic partnership developing PD-L1xVEGF-A bispecific pumitamig for solid tumors including NSCLC (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/30/3303897/0/en/global-data-for-biontech-and-bristol-myers-squibb-s-pd-l1xvegf-a-bispecific-pumitamig-shows-encouraging-efficacy-in-patients-with-non-small-cell-lung-cancer-in-rosetta-lung-02-tria.html).
• UCB: Acquired Candid Therapeutics in a $2.2 billion transaction, securing a pair of bispecific antibodies it was developing for autoimmune conditions (https://biopharmadive.com/news/avenzo-rallybio-reverse-merger-cancer-drugs/821613/).
• WuXi Biologics: Strategic objective is to expand its integrated CRDMO model to support commercial-scale production of complex biologics, particularly bispecific antibodies, in South Korea and the Asia-Pacific region (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing).
• Avenzo Therapeutics: Formed via a reverse merger with RallyBio, the company is advancing a portfolio that includes two bispecific ADCs alongside small molecule programs (https://biopharmadive.com/news/avenzo-rallybio-reverse-merger-cancer-drugs/821613/).
• CytomX Therapeutics: Strategic objective involves expanding research collaboration with Regeneron to develop next-generation bispecific immunotherapies using the PROBODY® platform (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/03/3305966/37704/en/cytomx-announces-expansion-of-strategic-research-collaboration-with-regeneron-in-the-field-of-conditional-bispecific-therapeutics-for-the-treatment-of-cancer.html).
• Crescent Biopharma: Strategic objective is to rapidly advance the lead program CR-001, a PD-1 x VEGF bispecific antibody, as a single agent or in combination regimens for solid tumors (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/02/3305600/0/en/crescent-biopharma-to-present-at-june-investor-conferences.html).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Ivonescimab is a bispecific antibody that targets two troublesome targets instead of one, distinguishing it from traditional monoclonal antibodies (https://biopharmadive.com/news/asco-2026-daraxonrasib-ivonescimab-proteus-erleada-results/821622/).
• IBI363 is a PD-1/IL-2 alpha-biased bispecific designed to block PD-1 signaling while acting on IL-2 to activate and expand tumor-specific T cells (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/asco-long-term-early-data-backs-tremendous-potential-takedas-innovent-oncology-collab).
• In a Phase 1 proof-of-concept study, the 3 mg dose group of IBI363 achieved a median progression-free survival (PFS) of 10.1 months and a median overall survival (OS) of 18.2 months in patients with squamous NSCLC (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/asco-long-term-early-data-backs-tremendous-potential-takedas-innovent-oncology-collab).
• Teclistamab is a BCMA-directed bispecific antibody evaluated in relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma patients (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-monotherapy-improves-pfs-and-os-in-relapsed-refractory-myeloma).
• A retrospective analysis indicated that BCMA-directed CAR T-cell therapy demonstrated superior overall survival compared to the bispecific antibody teclistamab in multiple myeloma, though with higher immune-related toxicities (https://cancernetwork.com/view/bcma-car-t-cell-therapy-improves-os-vs-teclistamab-in-multiple-myeloma).
• Talquetamab-tgvs Phase 2 OPTec/OPTal trial supported outpatient administration with prophylactic tocilizumab (https://cancernetwork.com/view/tocilizumab-supports-outpatient-bispecific-antibody-use-in-multiple-myeloma).
• Izalontamab Brengitecan is a bispecific ADC targeting EGFR and HER3 with topoisomerase I inhibitor payload (https://cancernetwork.com/view/izalontamab-brengitecan-improves-os-pfs-in-advanced-metastatic-tnbc).
• Manufacturing Technology: Microbial manufacturing platforms are being deployed to support bispecific antibody production (https://biospectrumasia.com/analysis/38/27704/cdmos-atmp-growth-upswing-in-apac.html).
• Industry Trend: Trispecific antibodies aim to address limitations of bispecifics such as antigen escape and tumor heterogeneity by engaging three targets simultaneously (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/).
• CytomX Therapeutics / Regeneron: Utilizing CytomX’s PROBODY® and Regeneron's Veloci-Bi® platforms to develop conditional bispecific immunotherapies designed to be localized to the tumor microenvironment (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/03/3305966/37704/en/cytomx-announces-expansion-of-strategic-research-collaboration-with-regeneron-in-the-field-of-conditional-bispecific-therapeutics-for-the-treatment-of-cancer.html).

• UCB acquired Candid Therapeutics in a $2.2 billion transaction, securing a pair of bispecific antibodies it was developing for autoimmune conditions (https://biopharmadive.com/news/avenzo-rallybio-reverse-merger-cancer-drugs/821613/).
• Takeda paid Innovent $1.2 billion upfront and agreed to $10.2 billion in potential milestone payments for the rights to IBI363 (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/asco-long-term-early-data-backs-tremendous-potential-takedas-innovent-oncology-collab).
• Sidewinder Therapeutics raised $137 million in a Series B round to take its lead bispecific antibody-drug conjugate into the clinic (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).
• Third Arc Bio extended its Series A financing by $52 million for a total of $217 million to continue developing multifunctional antibodies (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).
• EpiBiologics raised $107 million in a Series B round to take its lead bispecific antibody into the clinic (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26).
• WuXi Biologics partnered with Earendil Labs in March 2026 to support development and manufacturing of bispecific and multispecific antibodies (https://biospectrumasia.com/analysis/38/27704/cdmos-atmp-growth-upswing-in-apac.html).
• CytomX Therapeutics & Regeneron: Expansion of strategic research collaboration in the field of conditional bispecific therapeutics for the treatment of cancer (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/03/3305966/37704/en/cytomx-announces-expansion-of-strategic-research-collaboration-with-regeneron-in-the-field-of-conditional-bispecific-therapeutics-for-the-treatment-of-cancer.html).
