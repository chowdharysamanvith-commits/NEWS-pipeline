1. Overview
Recent pharmaceutical intelligence indicates significant advancement in bispecific antibody development across multiple oncology indications, highlighted by data presentations at the 2026 ASCO Annual Meeting and EHA Congress (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/02/3305600/0/en/crescent-biopharma-to-present-at-june-investor-conferences.html). Crescent Biopharma is advancing its lead PD-1 x VEGF bispecific antibody, CR-001, through a Phase 1/2 global trial in solid tumors (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/29/3303813/0/en/crescent-biopharma-announces-grants-of-inducement-awards.html). BioNTech reported encouraging efficacy for its PD-L1 x VEGF-A bispecific, Pumitamig, in first-line non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/30/3303897/0/en/global-data-for-biontech-and-bristol-myers-squibb-s-pd-l1xvegf-a-bispecific-pumitamig-shows-encouraging-efficacy-in-patients-with-non-small-cell-lung-cancer-in-rosetta-lung-02-tria.html). Regeneron’s bispecific antibody linvoseltamab demonstrated rapid and deep hematologic responses in patients with relapsed or refractory systemic light chain amyloidosis (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260529/linvoseltamab-data-in-al-amyloidosis-could-have-tremendous-implications). Akeso and Summit Therapeutics presented data showing that ivonescimab reduced the risk of death in squamous non-small cell lung cancer patients by 34% compared to standard treatment (https://statnews.com/2026/05/31/squamous-cell-lung-cancer-akeso-summit-harmoni-asco-2026/). Earendil Labs announced a strategic partnership with Sanofi valued at approximately $2.56 billion to advance bispecific antibodies for autoimmune diseases (https://pharmashots.com/32548/earendil-labs-doses-first-patient-with-hxn-1001-in-p-iia-trial-in-ulcerative-colitis/). WuXi Biologics secured South Korean Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) certification for the commercial manufacturing of a bispecific antibody (https://pharmamanufacturing.com/industry-news/news/55373337/wuxi-biologics-gets-south-korean-gmp-certification-for-bispecific-antibody-manufacturing). These developments underscore a strategic industry shift toward dual-targeting modalities to address resistance mechanisms and improve patient outcomes in solid tumors and hematologic malignancies (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/02/3305600/0/en/crescent-biopharma-to-present-at-june-investor-conferences.html).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• CR-001 — Mechanism: PD-1 x VEGF bispecific antibody — Trial Phase: Phase 1/2 — Patient Population: Multiple solid tumor types including NSCLC, gastrointestinal, and gynecological cancers — Clinical Endpoints: Safety and efficacy in first-line and previously treated patients (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/02/3305600/0/en/crescent-biopharma-to-present-at-june-investor-conferences.html)
• Linvoseltamab — Mechanism: Bispecific B-cell maturation antigen (BCMA)-directed CD3 T-cell engager — Trial Phase: Phase 1/2 (LINKER-AL2) — Patient Population: 20 patients with relapsed or refractory systemic AL amyloidosis — Clinical Endpoints: 100% achieved very good partial response or better; 90% achieved hematologic complete response (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260529/linvoseltamab-data-in-al-amyloidosis-could-have-tremendous-implications)
• Ivonescimab — Mechanism: PD-1/VEGF dual-target bispecific antibody — Trial Phase: Phase 3 (HARMONi-6) — Patient Population: Previously untreated advanced squamous non–small cell lung cancer — Clinical Endpoints: Median OS 27.89 months vs 23.69 months; 24-month OS rate 64.7% vs 48.6% (https://cancernetwork.com/view/ivonescimab-chemotherapy-improves-os-squamous-nsclc-harmoni-6)
• Teclistamab — Mechanism: BCMA-directed bispecific antibody — Trial Phase: Phase 3 (MajesTEC-9) — Patient Population: Relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma with 1 to 3 prior lines of therapy — Clinical Endpoints: Median PFS not reached vs 8.2 months; 18-month OS rate 79.2% vs 68.6% (https://cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-monotherapy-improves-pfs-and-os-in-relapsed-refractory-myeloma)
• Izalontamab Brengitecan — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody-drug conjugate targeting EGFR and HER3 with topoisomerase I inhibitor payload — Trial Phase: Phase 3 (PANKU-Breast02) — Patient Population: Previously treated unresected locally advanced or metastatic triple-negative breast cancer — Clinical Endpoints: Median PFS 8.5 months vs 3.1 months; Median OS 15.9 months vs 12.5 months (https://cancernetwork.com/view/izalontamab-brengitecan-improves-os-pfs-in-advanced-metastatic-tnbc)
• Rilvegostomig — Mechanism: Dual PD-1/TIGIT bispecific antibody — Trial Phase: Phase 2 (I-SPY 2.2) — Patient Population: Early-stage, high-risk, HER2-negative breast cancer — Clinical Endpoints: Pathologic complete response (pCR) rates reached 53% in immune-positive subgroups (https://cancernetwork.com/view/rilvegostomig-combo-demonstrates-activity-in-her2-negative-breast-cancer)
• Epcoritamab — Mechanism: IgG1-bispecific antibody binding CD3 on T cells and CD20 on B cells — Trial Phase: Phase 3 (EPCORE DLBCL-1, EPCORE FL-1) — Patient Population: Relapsed/refractory large B-cell lymphoma and follicular lymphoma — Clinical Endpoints: Full results comparing monotherapy to investigator’s choice chemotherapy and combination with rituximab/lenalidomide (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/29/3303823/0/en/transactions-with-shares-and-linked-securities-in-genmab-a-s-made-by-managerial-employees-and-their-closely-associated-persons.html)
