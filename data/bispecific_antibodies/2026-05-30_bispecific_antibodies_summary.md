1. Overview
The pharmaceutical landscape for bispecific antibodies is expanding across oncology and autoimmune indications, driven by clinical efficacy in hematologic malignancies and solid tumors. Regeneron Pharmaceuticals reported significant hematologic responses with linvoseltamab in systemic light chain amyloidosis, while Genmab continues to advance epcoritamab in lymphoma settings (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260529/linvoseltamab-data-in-al-amyloidosis-could-have-tremendous-implications). Strategic activity includes UCB’s acquisition of Candid Therapeutics to secure an autoimmune bispecific asset and Novabridge Biosciences progressing givastomig in gastric cancer (https://pharmavoice.com/news/lilly-gilead-lead-pharmas-deal-merger-drug-acquisition/819512/). Manufacturing capabilities are scaling to meet demand, with AsymBio operationalizing a commercial site in Shanghai for bispecific production (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/27/3301997/0/en/asymbio-showcases-comprehensive-conjugation-toolbox-to-support-process-development-for-next-generation-bioconjugates.html). Industry trends indicate a shift toward trispecific antibodies to address antigen escape, alongside operational strategies to administer bispecific T-cell engagers in community oncology practices (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/). Bristol Myers Squibb faces competitive pressure in multiple myeloma from emerging bispecific antibody classes (https://biopharmadive.com/news/bristol-myers-mezigdomide-asco-2026-successor-results/821509/).

4. Clinical & Scientific Highlights
• Linvoseltamab — Mechanism: Bispecific B-cell maturation antigen (BCMA)-directed CD3 T-cell engager — Trial Phase: Phase 1/2 (LINKER-AL2) — Patient Population: Adults with relapsed or refractory systemic light chain amyloidosis — Clinical Endpoints: 100% objective response rate and 90% hematologic complete response (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260529/linvoseltamab-data-in-al-amyloidosis-could-have-tremendous-implications)
• Linvoseltamab — Safety Profile: No dose-limiting toxicities occurred; most common treatment-emergent adverse events included cytokine release syndrome (grade 1, 35%; grade 2, 15%) and infusion-related reactions (35%, all grade 1/grade 2) (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20260529/linvoseltamab-data-in-al-amyloidosis-could-have-tremendous-implications)
• ASP2138 — Mechanism: LDN18.2xCD3 bispecific T-cell engager — Trial Phase: Phase 3 preparation — Patient Population: Gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma (https://fiercebiotech.com/biotech/facing-looming-xtandi-patent-cliff-astellas-pins-hopes-more-deals-while-still-cutting-costs)
• Epcoritamab — Mechanism: IgG1-bispecific antibody created using Genmab's DuoBody technology, administered subcutaneously to bind CD3 on T cells and CD20 on B cells — Trial Phase: Phase 3 — Patient Population: Relapsed/refractory large B-cell lymphoma and follicular lymphoma (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/29/3303823/0/en/transactions-with-shares-and-linked-securities-in-genmab-a-s-made-by-managerial-employees-and-their-closely-associated-persons.html)
• Givastomig — Mechanism: Claudin 18.2 X 4-1BB bispecific antibody that conditionally activates T cells via the 4-1BB signaling pathway in the tumor microenvironment — Trial Phase: Phase 2 (global, randomized) — Patient Population: Claudin 18.2-positive gastric cancer and other gastrointestinal malignancies (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/28/3302657/0/en/novabridge-to-present-at-the-2026-jefferies-global-healthcare-conference.html)
• Ragistomig — Mechanism: Bispecific antibody integrating PD-L1 as a tumor engager and 4-1BB as a conditional T cell activator — Trial Phase: Development — Patient Population: Solid tumors (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/28/3302657/0/en/novabridge-to-present-at-the-2026-jefferies-global-healthcare-conference.html)
• CR-001 — Mechanism: Investigational tetravalent bispecific antibody combining PD-1 checkpoint inhibition and VEGF blockade — Trial Phase: Phase 1/2 (ASCEND) — Patient Population: Solid tumors (NSCLC, GI, Gyn) (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/29/3303813/0/en/crescent-biopharma-announces-grants-of-inducement-awards.html)
• THN622 — Mechanism: Bispecific anti-fibrin inflammatory epitope and anti-VEGF blocking antibody — Trial Phase: In development — Patient Population: Retinal conditions characterized by vascular dysfunction, including DME (https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/27/3302124/0/en/therini-bio-doses-first-patients-in-phase-1b-trial-of-novel-anti-inflammatory-therapy-thn391-for-diabetic-macular-edema.html)
• Trispecific Antibodies — Scientific Context: Designed to bind three different targets simultaneously to address antigen escape and tumor heterogeneity limitations seen with bispecific antibodies (https://www.labiotech.eu/in-depth/trispecific-antibodies-field-review/)
• Bispecific T-cell Engagers — Operational Protocol: Community oncology practices are developing strategies to manage cytokine release syndrome (CRS) and immune effector cell-associated neurotoxicity syndrome (ICANS) (https://cancernetwork.com/view/moving-bispecific-antibodies-into-community-practice-operational-strategies-and-expert-consensus)
